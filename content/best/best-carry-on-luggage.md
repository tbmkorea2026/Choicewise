## How we chose the best carry-on luggage

We scored each suitcase on **durability (30%)**, **packing and organisation (25%)**, **maneuverability (20%)**, **weight (10%)** and **value (15%)**. See [how we review products](/how-we-review/).

## Carry-on buying guide

### Check airline size limits

Size limits vary by airline. A 22 × 14 × 9 inch bag fits most US carriers, but many international and budget airlines use smaller limits.

### Wheels and handle

Four-wheel spinners are easier to maneuver in airports, while two-wheel designs handle rough pavements and cobblestones better.

### Warranty

A long warranty is a good sign of build quality. Several brands on this list offer lifetime coverage.
