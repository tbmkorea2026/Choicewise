## How they print

The **B21 Pro** prints with heat directly on heat-sensitive labels. It's simple and cheap to run, but thermal labels can **fade over time** with heat and sunlight, which matters for products stored in windows or warm places.

The **M2** heats a **ribbon** that transfers ink onto the label. That gives more **durable, smudge-resistant** labels and lets you print in **different colors and finishes** (gold, silver, holographic and more), at the cost of buying ribbons.

## Who each one suits

- **M2:** small brands labeling candles, cosmetics, coffee bags, jars and bottles; cables and equipment; anything that needs to look premium or last outdoors.
- **B21 Pro:** home and office organization, price labels, gift and planner stickers, and small photo prints.
