## How to choose MIER running shorts

### 1. Liner type

- **2-in-1 compression liner:** support and phone storage; many runners choose these to reduce chafing.
- **Brief or mesh liner:** built-in underwear, light and airy.
- **Linerless:** the most freedom; wear your own underwear or compression shorts.

### 2. Inseam

- **3":** maximum freedom of movement for faster runs and races.
- **4–5":** the most versatile everyday length.
- **7":** more coverage for the gym and casual wear.

### 3. Pockets

If you run with a phone, the **SprintDry 2-in-1** has a dedicated liner phone pocket and MIER says the **women's SprintDry 4"** back zip pocket fits a phone. Other styles have zip pockets for essentials; check the pocket size on the product page if you want to carry a large phone.

- **Free shipping** on orders over **$60**: two pairs usually qualify.
