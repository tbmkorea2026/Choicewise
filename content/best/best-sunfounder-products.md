## How to choose

### 1. Already have a Raspberry Pi 5?

Pick a **Pironman** case. The **Pironman 5** is enough for one SSD; the **Pironman 5-MAX** adds a second NVMe slot for a **NAS with RAID 0/1** or an **SSD plus a Hailo-8L AI accelerator**. See [Pironman 5 vs 5-MAX](/best/sunfounder-pironman-5-vs-pironman-5-max/).

### 2. Want to learn robotics and AI?

The **PiCar-X** is the cheaper, simpler robot (Python and Scratch, ages 10+). The **PiDog** is more complex, with **12 servos** and more sensors. Both need a Raspberry Pi, and their AI chat projects use online LLM services such as ChatGPT or Gemini, or local models through Ollama.

### 3. Starting from zero?

The **ESP32 Ultimate Starter Kit** includes its board, **100+ projects** and **50+ video tutorials** in **C++, MicroPython and Scratch**, so you don't need anything else except a computer.
