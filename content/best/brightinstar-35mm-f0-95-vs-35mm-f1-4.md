## Quick verdict

- **APS-C or M43 camera?** Get the **35mm F0.95**: it's made for your sensor and its F0.95 aperture gives the shallowest focus and best low-light performance of the two.
- **Full-frame camera, or shooting video?** Get the **35mm F1.4**: it covers full-frame, weighs a listed 218 g and has a de-click aperture ring.

Both cost **$189.99** and both are **manual focus**.

## What the numbers mean

**Field of view.** On an APS-C camera, both lenses frame like about **52.5mm** on full-frame. Mounted on a full-frame body, the F1.4 gives a true **35mm** wide-normal view; the F0.95 doesn't cover the full sensor.

**Light and blur.** F0.95 is about one stop brighter than F1.4. On APS-C that means faster shutter speeds at night and creamier backgrounds, but also razor-thin focus up close. One F0.95 buyer found close focusing at F0.95 tricky and saw color fringing in backlit scenes until about F2.

**Video.** The F1.4's **de-clicked mode** lets you change aperture smoothly while filming. Brightin Star doesn't list that feature for the F0.95.

Same price, different cameras. **APS-C shooters** get more speed from the **F0.95**; **full-frame and video shooters** should choose the **F1.4**.
