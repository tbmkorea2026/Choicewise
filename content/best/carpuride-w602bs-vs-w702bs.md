## Quick verdict

- **Want the biggest, easiest-to-read map?** Get the **W702BS** ($429.90). Its 7-inch 1024×600 screen is the largest, and it has the most customer feedback of any Carpuride product.
- **Want a middle size with a Type-C port?** Get the **W602BS** ($449.99). Carpuride pitches 6 inches as the balance between visibility and dash space.
- **Want it compact and cheapest?** The **W502BS** ($409.90) has a 5-inch screen.

With code **WEEPYRUFF** (30% off at the time of writing), that's about **$301 vs $315**, or **$287** for the W502BS.

## Screen size

The **7-inch W702BS** shows the most map at 1024×600, which helps on long tours and when glancing quickly. The **5-inch W502BS** suits bikes with little room above the bars. The **6-inch W602BS** sits in between; Carpuride says it's the size riders ask for most. Its resolution isn't listed.

## The Type-C port

Only the **W602BS** has a built-in **Type-C port**. Carpuride says it lets you power the screen from USB and set it up, including the compass, without it being on the bike. It's a convenience, not a must-have, since both take power from the BMW mount when installed.

## Ratings and evidence

The **W702BS** has **2,142 store reviews** at 4.82/5, far more than the W602BS's 231 at 4.73/5. Both breakdowns show **no reviews below 4 stars**, which looks filtered. Carpuride's Trustpilot score is **4.3/5 from 442 reviews (21% 1-star)**; see our [Carpuride review](/reviews/carpuride-review/).

## Before you buy either

- **Check compatibility:** both are BMW-only (specific models), and neither supports HarmonyOS or some Android phones.
- **TPMS:** tire pressure needs factory BMW TPMS or Carpuride's sensors; check the sensor clears your brakes.
- **Returns:** 30 days with prior approval; opened units without a fault can be refused, and you pay return shipping.
- **Warranty:** 2 years on main parts; after 3 months, you pay shipping to the service center.
- **Ride safely:** set routes before you ride and use the Wonder Wheel rather than the touchscreen.

## The bottom line

On features the two are almost identical. Pick the **W702BS** for the **bigger screen, lower price and far more reviews**, or the **W502BS** for the smallest size. Pick the **W602BS** if a **6-inch screen** fits your cockpit better and you like the **Type-C port**.
