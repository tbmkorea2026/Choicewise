## How to choose

- **Solid walls and ceiling, and you want the floor free?** The **Indoor Play Gym**, or the **Wall Climbing Gym** for $516 less with a smaller climbing wall.
- **Renting, or plasterboard walls?** A two-tower set (ask support to confirm it needs no wall fixing): the **Large Indoor Playground** for monkey bars, the **Large Playground Climber** for younger kids who also like playhouses.
- **Smaller budget or room?** The **Indoor Monkey Bars**; compare it with its cheaper sibling in [Indoor Monkey Bars vs Climbing Monkey Bars](/best/woodandhearts-indoor-monkey-bars-vs-climbing-monkey-bars/).

## Buying tips

- **Measure twice:** check ceiling height, wall width and the free space around each set before ordering. Returns mean paying shipping both ways.
- **Budget for mats:** a soft landing area is part of the safety advice and isn't included.
- **Check the wall:** wall systems need brick, concrete, solid studs or metal profiles, not drywall alone.
- **Allow time:** production plus shipping from Ukraine takes 3–25 business days, longer in the holidays.
- **Use code PARK** for 5% off. See our full [Wood and Hearts review](/reviews/woodandhearts-review/).
