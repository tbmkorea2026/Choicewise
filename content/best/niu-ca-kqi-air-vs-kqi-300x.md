## Weight and portability

This is the big one. The **KQi Air weighs 11.9 kg**, about **half the 300X's 22.1 kg**, thanks to its carbon-fibre frame. If you'll carry it every day, the Air is the clear pick even at C$100 more.

## Ride and safety

The **300X** has **front suspension**, **wider 10.5-inch self-healing tubeless tires** and **two disc brakes plus an electric brake**, which help on broken pavement and wet roads. The **Air** lists **9.5-inch tubeless tires**, a single **mechanical disc** and regenerative braking, with no suspension listed. Both have turn signals.

## Power, speed and range

The 300X has more power (**1000 W vs 700 W**), claims **25% climbing**, and lists the longer range (**60 km vs 50 km**). The Air lists the higher top speed (**32 km/h vs 25 km/h**), though provincial rules may cap both. Expect less range in Canadian winters.

## Ratings and warranty

The 300X has **11 store reviews** averaging 4.5, the Air just **2** at 5.0, too few to rely on. Both have NIU's **2-year warranty** on the main body and **free change-of-mind returns** within 30 days and 10 km. NIU's Trustpilot score is **1.4**, so see our [NIU Canada review](/reviews/niu-ca-review/) before buying.

## The bottom line

Choose the **KQi Air** if lightness matters most; choose the **KQi 300X** for comfort, braking and power at a lower price. Also read the [KQi 300X review](/reviews/niu-ca-kqi-300x-review/) and the [best NIU Canada scooters and e-bikes](/best/best-niu-ca-scooters-ebikes/).
