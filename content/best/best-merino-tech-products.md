## How to build a merino kit

1. **Start with a base layer set** (top + pants) in **250 g/m²** for skiing, winter hiking and cold-weather travel.
2. **Add a lightweight tee** (165–180 g/m²) for warmer days and travel.
3. **Add merino underwear** (190 g/m²) for multi-day trips; merino's odor resistance is a big advantage here.
4. **Go heavier (320)** only if you spend long periods in very cold conditions. See our [250 vs 320 comparison](/best/merino-tech-250-vs-320-half-zip/).

## Caring for merino

Machine wash cold with a mild or wool-specific detergent, skip fabric softener and bleach, and lay flat to dry. Store clean wool in a dry, airtight place to keep moths away.
