## How to choose

- **Cat owner on a budget?** The **EBO SE 2**: laser, 2K and auto-recharge for $169.99.
- **Want a friendlier, more expressive robot?** The **EBO Air 2** adds an expression display, AI noise cancellation and smarter self-charging for $29 more.
- **Rugs, thresholds or a bigger home?** The **Air 2 Plus** (3K, big battery) or the **ROLA Mini** (long battery, tip-proof).
- **Just want a moving camera cheaply?** The **EBO SE** at $139, without the laser.
