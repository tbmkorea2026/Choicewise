## The 3-step checklist

1. **A lamp.** Every gel product here cures under UV/LED light. The **Mini UV LED Lamp ($17.99)** is the cheapest option; NailSala also resells **SUNUV** salon-style lamps ($29.99–$41.99) if you want to cure a whole hand at once.
2. **One colour route.** Pick just one to start:
   - **Gel pens:** fewest steps, top coat included.
   - **Semi-cured strips:** no painting at all.
   - **Solid gel:** most colours, but also needs base and top coat.
3. **A removal plan.** Taking gel off badly (peeling or picking) is how nails get damaged. Budget for the **Gel Remover Kit ($23.99)** from day one.

## Small print worth reading

- **Lamp:** check whether the version you pick is rechargeable; the listing conflicts.
- **Solid gel:** "Base Coat, Top Coat, and UV/LED Lamp are required."
- **Returns:** opened or used nail products can't be returned.

## Safety basics

Keep gel off your skin (repeated contact can trigger allergies), cure each layer fully, protect your hands from UV with fingerless gloves or sunscreen, and use removers in a ventilated room. Stop and see a doctor if your skin reacts.
