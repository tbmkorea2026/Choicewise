## Length and silhouette

The **milkmaid dress** is the more traditional, **renaissance-friendly** shape, falling to **mid-calf**. The **puffy corset dress** trades length for **volume**: a **two-layer skirt** that stops **above the knee** gives a playful, fuller look, and its **pockets** are a practical bonus.

## Fit

Both dresses use the same fit system: **smocked back**, **adjustable straps** and a **side zip**, with no stretch in the body. Scarlet Darkness recommends matching **bust and waist** to its chart and sizing up if you're between sizes.
