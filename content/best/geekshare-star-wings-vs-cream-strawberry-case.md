## The key differences

### 1. What's in the box

The **Cream Strawberry** case comes with a **matching pair of thumb grip caps**, worth $9.99 if bought separately. The **Star Wings** case is the case only.

### 2. Matching accessories

**Star Wings** has a matching **silicone dock cover** ($16.99), so you can dress up the whole setup in one style.

### 3. Switch 2 price

For Switch 2, **Cream Strawberry** stays at **$21.99**, while the **Star Wings** Switch 2 case is **$26.99**. Note that Cream Strawberry's description only lists Switch and Switch OLED compatibility even though Switch 2 variants are on sale, so confirm the fit with GeekShare.

## What they share

Both are **soft silicone** cases with a **frosted hydrophobic coating** to help with everyday bumps, scratches, dust and light splashes, and both are designed to stay on in the dock, with GeekShare warning the fit can be tight.
