## How to choose

- **Hilly area, no need to fold?** The **E26 3.0 Pro**.
- **Need to fold it into a car or flat?** **Engine Pro 3.0 Boost** or **EP-2 3.0 Boost** (see [the comparison](/best/engwe-engine-pro-3-boost-vs-ep-2-3-boost/)), or the **Engine Pro 2.0**.
- **Easy mounting and lower price?** The step-through **L20 Boost**.

## Buying tips

- **Check the weight:** all five are 31–35 kg.
- **Inspect on arrival and ride under 10 km** until you're sure: change-of-mind returns cost €200 per bike.
- **Consider the extended warranty** at checkout: standard cover is 12 months on most models (24 on the EP-2 3.0 Boost).
- **Wear a helmet** and don't modify speed limits. See our full [ENGWE review](/reviews/engwe-review/).
