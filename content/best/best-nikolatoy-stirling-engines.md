## How to choose a Stirling engine model

### 1. Flame or no flame?

Most Stirling models run on a small **alcohol burner**, which means an open flame and a hot cylinder. If you want something for an office or a home with young children, a **low-temperature-difference** model that runs on a hot drink is the simpler choice.

### 2. Just motion, or electricity too?

Basic models simply spin a flywheel. **Generator models** turn that motion into a small electrical output that lights an LED, which makes the energy conversion visible, and the **Cross Twin** adds a **voltmeter** so you can measure it, great for school projects.

### 3. Learning tool or décor?

The Classic and generator models are best for learning. The **Retro Fan** is primarily a decorative showpiece that happens to run on heat.

## Safety tips

- Use alcohol burners on a stable, heat-resistant surface, away from anything flammable.
- Let the engine cool before touching the hot cylinder.
- Children should only run flame-powered models with adult supervision.
