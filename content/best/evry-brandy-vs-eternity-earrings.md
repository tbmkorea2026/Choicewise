## Materials

**Brandy** is made of **stainless steel** with 18k plating, the more water-tolerant base in EVRY's range. **Eternity**'s standard options are **14k plated**, which EVRY's help center describes as plating over **brass**; choose a **"Waterproof"** variant if durability in water matters.

## Look and fit

**Brandy** is a **chunky** statement piece in three sizes, from a small mini to a 25 mm hoop. **Eternity** is a slim **3 mm huggie** with a row of CZ stones, easy to **stack** with other earrings.

## Before you buy either
