## How to choose

- **Battery box size:** Group 24 boxes fit the Group 24; tighter spaces suit the 100Ah Mini.
- **Inverter loads:** a 100A BMS supports about 1,280 W; for 2,000 W-class inverters at 12V, look at the 200A 314Ah models.
- **48V home systems:** the server rack talks to compatible inverters over CAN/RS485.
- **Want to see charge level on your phone?** Pick a Bluetooth model. Compare the two 100Ah options in [Group 24 vs Mini Bluetooth](/best/wattcycle-12v-100ah-group-24-vs-mini-bluetooth/).

## Buying tips and safety

- **Check your charger:** LiFePO4 needs about 14.4–14.6V charging; old lead-acid chargers may not suit.
- **Don't install until you're sure:** installed batteries can't be returned for change of mind, and you pay return shipping on unused ones.
- **Register within 3 months** for the extra 2 warranty years.
- **Report damage within 48 hours** of delivery.
