## Quick verdict

- **Most buyers, especially cat owners:** get the **EBO SE 2** ($169.99). You get the same 2K camera, laser and battery for $29 less, plus Wi-Fi 6 and Pet Fun Patrol.
- **Want more personality and clearer calls?** Get the **EBO Air 2** ($199) for its customizable expression display, desktop clock, AI noise cancellation and long-range self-charging. It also has 77 store reviews, while the SE 2 is brand new.

With code **TRANTRUNG** (10% off at the time of writing), that's about **$153 vs $179**.

## What's the same

Both are tennis-ball-sized 2K robots you drive from the Enabot app, with **night vision**, **two-way audio**, **24/7 recording**, **motion alerts**, a **built-in laser**, **auto-recharge** and **microSD storage up to 256 GB**. Battery, size, noise (61 dB) and obstacle limits are essentially identical.

## What's different

- **Connectivity:** the SE 2 has **Wi-Fi 6**, which Enabot says gives a more stable, lower-latency connection.
- **Personality:** the Air 2's **expression display** lets you set custom emojis and shows a **desktop clock**; the SE 2 has animated eyes.
- **Audio:** the Air 2 adds **AI noise cancellation** for clearer two-way talk.
- **Pet play:** the SE 2 adds a **feather toy** and **Pet Fun Patrol**.
- **Track record:** the Air 2 has **77** store reviews; the SE 2 is new.

## Before you buy either

- **Floors:** both only cross ~6 mm obstacles, so measure rugs and thresholds.
- **Returns:** 14 days for any reason, but you pay return shipping and the robot must look unused.
- **Warranty:** 1 year (2 in Europe), country of purchase only. Enabot's Trustpilot score is 3.3/5.
- **Laser safety:** never aim the laser at eyes.

## The bottom line

The **EBO SE 2** is the better value: the same core robot as the Air 2 with newer Wi-Fi and extra pet play for **$29 less**. Choose the **EBO Air 2** if you want the expression display, clearer audio and a model with an established review history.
