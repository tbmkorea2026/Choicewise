## Where they really differ

**Price and track record.** The 689BT costs **$30 less** and has **almost four times as many reviews**, which matters more than small spec differences.

**Gateway access.** The 689BT lists **FCA gateway access via AutoAuth**, relevant if you work on newer Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep or Ram vehicles with a secure gateway (you'll need an AutoAuth account). The 892BT page doesn't mention it.

**Convenience extras.** The 892BT's **magnetic dongle** can stick to the car while you work, and its AI explains fault codes in plain language. It also runs general apps (PDF reader, YouTube).
