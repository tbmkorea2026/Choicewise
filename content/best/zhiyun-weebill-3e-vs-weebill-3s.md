## Quick verdict

- **Choose the Weebill 3E** if you want a **3 kg sling gimbal with native vertical shooting** for the lowest price, and you don't need a light on the gimbal or add-on modules.
- **Choose the Weebill 3S** if you'll use a **built-in fill light** for vlogs and interviews, or plan to add **image transmission or a follow-focus motor** later.

## The key differences

### 1. The fill light

The **Weebill 3S** has a **built-in 1000-lux fill light** with color temperature adjustable from **2600K to 5500K**. ZHIYUN pitches it for face lighting, product details and low-light scenes on the go. It won't replace a proper video light, but it saves carrying one for quick talking-head shots. The **3E has no light**.

### 2. Expansion modules

ZHIYUN says the **3S supports image transmission and follow-focus modules** and the **3E doesn't**. If you might later want to monitor from a phone or control focus from the gimbal, the 3S leaves that door open.

### 3. Smart features and kits

The 3S product page lists **AI tracking, wireless remote control, and voice and motion-sensing control**. The page doesn't clearly say whether these work with the **Standard** kit or rely on accessories in the **Combo**. If they matter to you, **check the box contents** before you buy.

### 4. Price

At the time of writing, the 3E costs **$199** and the 3S starts at **$271**, a **$72 gap** for the Standard kit and **$174** for the Combo.

## What they share

Both have a **3 kg payload**, **native vertical shooting**, **Bluetooth camera control**, a **2600 mAh battery** rated for **up to 11.5 hours**, **2-hour charging** and the same tilt range. Both are rated highly by buyers on the ZHIYUN store.

### A note on weight

ZHIYUN markets the 3E as having a **"featherlight sub-1kg body"**, but its own spec table lists **1,054.8 g** for the main unit, and its FAQ says **1050 g**. In practice, the two gimbals weigh about the same, a little over 1 kg before you add a camera.
