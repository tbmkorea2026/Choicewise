## The key differences

### 1. Material and look

The **Sarah** combines **TR90**, a light and flexible plastic, with **metal** hinges and temples. Its front is mostly black with a two-tone tortoiseshell strip along the top. The **Sahana** is **acetate** throughout, with Lensmart describing a **brown, cream and amber** tortoiseshell pattern, a more traditional look.

### 2. Bridge and fit

Both frames are **142 mm wide**, but the **bridge** is very different: **15 mm** on the Sarah versus **21 mm** on the Sahana. A bridge that's too narrow can sit high or pinch; one that's too wide can slide down. Check the bridge number on glasses that already fit you.

The Sarah also has **wider lenses (55 mm vs 52 mm)**, while the Sahana has **slightly longer arms (145 mm vs 142 mm)**.

### 3. Weight

The Sarah weighs **26 g** and the Sahana **30.6 g**. Both are reasonable for large frames, but the Sarah will feel a little lighter on your nose over a long day.

### 4. Price

At the time of writing, the Sarah costs **$14.95** and the Sahana **$19.95**, frame only. Lensmart's **"50% off every 2nd frame"** offer applies if you buy two frames.

## A note on lens height

Lensmart's descriptions for **both** frames say the lens height is **51 mm**, but the spec tables say **45 mm** (Sarah) and **44 mm** (Sahana). Lens height matters most for **progressive** and **bifocal** lenses, so if you need them, ask Lensmart to confirm before ordering.
