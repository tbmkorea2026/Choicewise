## What EMS actually does

An EMS device places electrodes on your skin and sends pulses that make the muscle underneath **contract and relax**, the same way a nerve signal would. You feel a tightening that you can't fully control. Ape Born's Ultra does this around your midsection, with **12 modes** that change the pattern and **19 intensity levels** that change how strong the contraction is.

## What it can do

- **Strengthen the targeted muscle a bit.** Studies of ab-stimulation belts have reported modest improvements in abdominal strength and endurance after several weeks of regular use.
- **Help when exercise is hard.** EMS is used in rehab and for people who can't do much active training, under professional guidance.
- **Add a little extra work** to an existing routine.

## What it can't do

- **Burn fat from your belly.** Spot reduction doesn't work with exercise or electricity.
- **Replace a workout.** A stimulated contraction isn't the same as moving your body under load; Ape Born's "3,000 crunches" figure comes from counting contractions.
- **Deliver "500 calories in 20 minutes".** We found no evidence for it. In 2002, the US Federal Trade Commission took action against several ab-belt sellers over similar weight-loss and six-pack claims.

## Reading Ape Born's numbers

- **"FDA Cleared (Class II)":** no 510(k) number on the page. Ask for it.
- **"121% increase in abdominal endurance" / "59% increase in strength":** shown on product images citing "an 8-week study", without naming it.
- **4.6/5 from 1,501 reviews:** similar counts appear on several listings, which suggests pooled reviews. Trustpilot shows 3.6/5.

## Who should not use EMS

- Pacemaker, defibrillator or other implanted electronic device
- Pregnancy
- Epilepsy or heart conditions
- Broken, irritated or numb skin at the pad site
- Never across the chest, neck or head

If you have a health condition, talk to a doctor first, and always start on a low intensity.

## How to get real value from one

- Use it **alongside** core training (planks, dead bugs, carries) and enough protein.
- Be consistent; studies used regular sessions over several weeks.
- Judge it on **strength and comfort**, not the scale.
- Use the **60-day refund window** if it doesn't suit you, and **cancel the Elite Club trial** if you didn't want it.

## The bottom line

EMS belts like the Ultra can make your muscles work, which has some value as an extra. They can't melt fat or replace exercise.
