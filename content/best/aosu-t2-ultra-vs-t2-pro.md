## Quick verdict

- **Want the sharpest picture and fewer false alarms?** Get the **T2 Ultra** (2-cam kit $399.99).
- **Want two views from each camera for less?** Get the **T2 Pro** (2-cam kit $299.99).

## Resolution vs coverage

The **T2 Ultra** puts all its pixels into one 4K view, better for reading details at a distance, and swings 360° to follow movement. The **T2 Pro** splits its job between **two 3K cameras**, so you can watch a wide area and a close-up at the same time; aosu says the arm gives about **3x the coverage** of a single camera.

## Alerts

The T2 Ultra's **radar + PIR + AI** combination is designed to cut false alarms from things like swaying trees. That matters for battery cameras, since every false alert drains power.

## Before you buy either

- **Event recording only**, not 24/7.
- **Charging** stops below 14°F, per aosu's D1 specs; check panel placement.
- **Returns:** 30 days for any reason, but you pay return shipping; **warranty** 12 months.
- aosu's Trustpilot score is **2.7/5**, so use the return window to test.

## The bottom line

Pick the **T2 Ultra** for 4K detail and smarter detection, or the **T2 Pro** for two simultaneous views per camera at a lower kit price.
