## How to choose

- **Need wireless?** The **K2** or **K8**; compare them in [K2 vs K8](/best/keychron-k2-vs-k8/). For a number pad and 2.4 GHz, the **Q6 Max**.
- **Stays on one desk?** The wired **V1** or **Q1** give you QMK, a knob and nicer builds for less.
- **Want a premium feel on a budget?** The **Q1 Version 2** puts an aluminum, gasket-mount case under $90 while stock lasts.

## Buying tips

- **Check the version:** stock is limited on older models, so confirm the switch and backlight you're getting.
- **Choose carefully:** change-of-mind returns must be requested within 7 days and cost you shipping.
- **Barebone means no switches or keycaps.** Budget for both if you pick the Q1 barebone.
- **Import tax isn't included** outside the US; check before ordering. See our full [Keychron review](/reviews/keychron-review/).
