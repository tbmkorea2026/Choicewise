## How to choose EyeCandys colored contacts

### 1. Start with your prescription

Your eye doctor's contact lens prescription gives the **power**, **base curve** and **diameter** that fit you. These lenses are **8.6 or 8.7 base curve, 14.2 mm** only, and come in **minus powers only**. If your prescription differs, or you have astigmatism, ask your eye doctor and check EyeCandys' toric lenses.

### 2. Pick a wear schedule

- **Daily (Glossy Ivory 1-Day):** a fresh pair each time, no cleaning. Best for occasional wear.
- **3 months (Cashmere, Doe):** cheaper per wear if you use them often; needs solution and a case.
- **6 months (Glossy Blue):** the longest cycle; replace on time even if they look fine.

### 3. Choose how strong a change you want

- **Subtle:** Cashmere Hazel or Satin Olive (no ring, small 12.4 mm design).
- **Lighter but natural:** the Glossy lenses (no ring, 12.7 mm).
- **More definition:** Doe Blue (soft limbal ring).

### 4. Compare materials

**MPC** lenses (Glossy Ivory 1-Day, Cashmere, Doe) list **40% water**; EyeCandys says MPC helps hold moisture. **Glossy Blue** is **HEMA** at **38%**. Comfort varies from person to person, so ask your eye doctor what suits your eyes.
