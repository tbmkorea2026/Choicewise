## Size and fit

The Mini is about **1.2 inches shorter and 1 inch narrower** at the same height, which matters in tight RV bays and under seats. The Group 24 matches the boxes and trays many boats and campers already have.

## Power and monitoring

Continuous power is identical. The Group 24's **300A surge** rating is far higher than the Mini's 150A, useful for loads with big start-up spikes. The Mini adds a **Bluetooth app** to watch state of charge and cell data.

## Before you buy either

- **Charging:** check your charger supports LiFePO4; neither charges below 32°F.
- **Returns:** 30 days, unused and uninstalled only; you pay return shipping.
- **Warranty:** register within 3 months to get 7 years in total.

## The bottom line

Same capacity, same BMS, $10 apart. The **Group 24** is the value pick and the better drop-in for standard boxes and motors; the **Mini Bluetooth** suits tight spaces and anyone who wants app monitoring.
