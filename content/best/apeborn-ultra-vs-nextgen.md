## Quick verdict

- **Ab training several times a week and hate consumables?** Get the **Ultra belt** ($149.99).
- **Want to try EMS on abs and arms for less?** Get the **NextGen gel-pad set** ($99.99), and budget for pads.

## Running cost

Gel pads lose their grip with use and need replacing. At **$29.99 per 14 pads**, a regular user buying a pack every few months adds roughly **$60–$90 a year**, which can make the cheaper NextGen set cost more than the Ultra over time. Ape Born says the Ultra has nothing to replace.

## Comfort and convenience

A **belt** is quicker to put on and doesn't need skin prep, but it fits only the area it's shaped for. **Gel pads** can go on abs or arms and fit different body shapes, but they need clean, dry skin and fresh pads to stay stuck.

## Before you buy either

- **Check your cart** for the 7-day Elite Club trial ($49.99/month afterwards) and pre-ticked add-ons.
- **Safety:** no EMS with a pacemaker, during pregnancy, with epilepsy or heart conditions, or on damaged skin.
- **Expectations:** modest strength gains, not fat loss. See [do EMS ab belts work?](/best/do-ems-ab-belts-work-apeborn-ultra/)
- **60-day refund** window from delivery.
