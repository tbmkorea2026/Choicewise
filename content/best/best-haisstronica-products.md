## Match the gauge

Wire gauge (AWG) matters for every product here. Lower numbers are thicker wires. Connectors are color-coded by gauge range, so match the connector color to your wire and the crimper's jaw position to the connector. Haisstronica's product pages list the gauge for each option; check it before ordering.
