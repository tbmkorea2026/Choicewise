## How to choose

- **Just organizing at home or the office?** The **B1** is the cheapest way in.
- **Want sharper inkless labels, or photo stickers?** The **B21 Pro**.
- **Selling products (candles, cosmetics, coffee, jars) and need labels that last?** The **M2**, or the **M3** for wider labels.
- **Shipping orders?** The **B4** handles 4x6 labels.

## Buying tips

- **Budget for consumables:** all models need NIIMBOT label rolls; the M2 and M3 also need ribbons.
- **Pick carefully:** orders **can't be cancelled**, and returns are **at your cost** within 30 days if unused.
- **Defects:** report within 30 days of arrival; refunds for defects after use come as **store credit**. No warranty period is stated.
- **Allow time for delivery:** NIIMBOT's pages give 5–10 days to the US and UK, but Trustpilot reviewers report some longer waits. See our [NIIMBOT review](/reviews/niimbot-review/).
