## The short answer

- **In the EU or UK:** **DATOUBOSS** is the one that ships to you, at very low prices; accept weaker after-sales.
- **In the US:** **WattCycle**, with clearer specs, a longer warranty and a stronger reputation.

## 12V: 324Ah vs 314Ah

Near-identical capacity (~4kWh) and the same **200A BMS**. DATOUBOSS adds **Bluetooth** and **low-temperature protection** in the base model; WattCycle publishes more **specs (IP65, 6,000 cycles at 80% DOD, size and weight)**. DATOUBOSS's **15,000-cycle** headline is far beyond WattCycle's more conservative figure.

## 48V: one big cabinet vs stackable racks

DATOUBOSS puts **16kWh in one wheeled cabinet** for €1,799.99. WattCycle sells **5.12kWh racks** you stack (up to 20), with **CAN/RS485 ports** for inverter communication. DATOUBOSS is cheaper per kWh; WattCycle is easier to verify and expand.

## Trust and after-sales

This is the biggest gap. WattCycle has **hundreds of mostly positive Trustpilot reviews**; DATOUBOSS's are **split**, with reports of dispatch delays and slow support.

## The bottom line

Buy by region, then by terms: **WattCycle** if you're in the US; **DATOUBOSS** in Europe if the price saving outweighs weaker after-sales. Read our [DATOUBOSS review](/reviews/datouboss-review/) and the [16kWh battery review](/reviews/datouboss-48v-314ah-battery-review/).
