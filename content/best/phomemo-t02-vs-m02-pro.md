- **Want the cheapest, cutest pocket printer?** Get the **T02** (from $39.49).
- **Want sharper prints and more paper options?** Get the **M02 Pro** ($68.99).

## Print quality

**300 dpi** on the M02 Pro means finer lines and smoother photos than the T02's **203 dpi**, which matters for detailed artwork, small text and photos. For simple stickers, labels and to-do lists, 203 dpi is usually enough.

## Paper and speed

The M02 Pro supports **three paper widths** (53, 25 and 15mm), useful for narrow labels as well as wide stickers, and prints about **50% faster** at its top speed. The T02 page doesn't list paper widths, so check compatible rolls before buying.

## Before you buy either

- **Thermal prints** can fade with heat and light; Phomemo sells papers rated for longer life.
- **Returns:** 30 days, but items must be unused and in original packaging, and you pay return shipping unless it's faulty.
- **Warranty:** 1 year. Phomemo's Trustpilot score is **2.9/5**, so test early.

## The bottom line

Pick the **T02** for the lowest price and lightest body, or the **M02 Pro** for sharper 300 dpi prints, faster output and more paper sizes.
