## The neck makes the difference

The **SL-233** has a separate neck section with **4 nodes** that you can raise or lower, so it kneads your neck and the top of your shoulders while the back nodes work on your spine. Snailax says it fits users about **5'3" to 5'11"**. You can also switch between **shiatsu** (kneading) and **rolling** along the spine and adjust the **roller width**.

The **SL-256** has no neck section. Its 4 nodes travel the length of your back, and Snailax says they **flex up to 3 mm** to follow your back's curves.

## Seat and comfort

Both have **vibration in the seat** for the hips and thighs. The SL-256 offers **3 vibration levels** and Snailax notes the **seat isn't heated**. The SL-233 has **2 vibration levels**. Both come with a **detachable flap** to soften the massage if it feels too strong.

- **Keep the box:** returns within 30 days need the original packaging, and Snailax's return pages disagree on who pays return shipping for change-of-mind returns. Confirm with support.

## The bottom line

The **SL-256** is the better buy if you just want a heated back massage on your office chair or sofa.
