## Quick verdict

- **Wrist under about 16.5cm (6.5"), or want the vintage look?** Get the **38mm** (from €335).
- **Average or larger wrist, or want 5 ATM and easy 20mm straps?** Get the **40mm** (€415).

## Fit

Lug-to-lug matters more than diameter. At **47mm**, the 38mm sits inside most wrists; the 40mm's **50.5mm** lugs can overhang a slim wrist. If you want 40mm but have a smaller wrist, the **Icon II** (40mm, 46mm lugs, €537) is the alternative; see our [Icon II Green review](/reviews/seagull1963-icon-ii-green-review/).

## Movement and crystal

Both are hand-wound Seagull chronographs (21 jewels, 21,600 bph, about 42 hours of power reserve). The 38mm lets you choose **acrylic** (vintage look, scratches more easily) or **sapphire**, with or without a swan-neck regulator; the 40mm comes with sapphire.

- **Returns:** 14 days, unused; return shipping is on you and €30 is deducted.

Choose the **38mm** for the classic proportions and lowest price, or the **40mm** for more presence, better water resistance and standard 20mm straps.
