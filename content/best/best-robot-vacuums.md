## Robot vacuum buying guide

### Navigation: LiDAR vs. gyro

**LiDAR** robots build an accurate map of your home, clean in efficient rows and support no-go zones. Cheaper **gyro** robots navigate in simpler patterns and take longer to cover a room.

### Suction power

Suction is measured in pascals (Pa). Around 4,000 Pa is plenty for hard floors, while homes with carpets and pets benefit from 6,000 Pa or more.

### Self-emptying docks

A self-emptying dock is the single biggest convenience upgrade. It means weeks of hands-free cleaning instead of emptying a small bin after every run.

### Mopping

Many robots now include mopping. Rotating or lifting mop pads deliver far better results than simple drag-cloth designs.
