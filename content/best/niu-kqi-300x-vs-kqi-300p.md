## Quick verdict

- **Long commute, hills or heavier rider?** Get the **KQi 300X** ($899) for more range and climbing power.
- **Daily rides under about 25 miles on mostly flat roads?** Get the **KQi 300P** ($749) and save $150 and 2.7 lb.

## Range and power

The **300X**'s **608 Wh** battery gives it about **25% more** rated range than the 300P, and its **500 W** motor handles **steeper hills** (25% vs 20%). If your round trip is long or hilly, that headroom matters, especially because real-world range is always below NIU's maximum.

## Tires and weight

The 300X uses **self-healing tubeless** tires designed to seal small punctures; the 300P uses regular **pneumatic tubeless** tires. The 300P is **2.7 lb lighter** and charges about **an hour faster**.

## Before you buy either

- **Wear a helmet** and follow local scooter laws.
- **Returns:** 30 days; change of mind only with **10 miles or less** on the odometer.
- **Warranty:** 2 years on the main body including the battery.
- **Reliability:** NIU's Trustpilot score is 1.4/5, so test your scooter thoroughly early on.

## The bottom line

Same comfortable, well-equipped platform: choose the **300X** for **range and hills**, or the **300P** to **save $150** if 30 miles is plenty.
