## How to choose

- **Building a wired PoE system?** The **YHC812-P** dome or **YHB812-PA** bullet (see [dome vs bullet](/best/hiseeu-yhc812-p-vs-yhb812-pa/)), plus the **YHD912-P** for areas that need panning.

- **Already own a Hiseeu wireless kit?** The **TZ-HB613** add-on.

## Buying tips

- **Plan power:** PoE cameras need a PoE NVR or switch; WiFi cameras need an outlet.
- **Buy a microSD card** if you won't use an NVR (not included).
- **Secure your system:** change default passwords and update firmware.
- **Test within 30 days:** returns are free in that window. See our full [Hiseeu review](/reviews/hiseeu-review/).
