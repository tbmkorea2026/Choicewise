## The key differences

### 1. Liner vs no liner

This is the deciding factor. The **SprintDry 2-in-1** has a fitted **compression liner** under the outer short; MIER says it is designed to support the thigh muscles, and many runners choose this style to reduce chafing. The **PaceLite Linerless** has no inner layer at all, which gives the most airflow and lets you choose your own underwear or compression shorts.

### 2. How you carry your stuff

The 2-in-1 has a **dedicated phone pocket in the liner**, plus a small hidden pocket inside the right side pocket for keys or cards. The Linerless has **three zip pockets** (two side, one back), which are better for securing keys and cards, though MIER does not say whether they fit a phone.

### 3. Visibility

The PaceLite Linerless lists **reflective elements** for low-light conditions. MIER does not list reflective details on the SprintDry 2-in-1.

### 4. Size range

The 2-in-1 starts at **XS**, the Linerless at **S**. Both go up to **3XL**.

## What they have in common

Both are **5-inch**, quick-drying, stretchy running shorts with an **elastic waistband and internal drawcord**, side splits for movement, and versatile use for running, gym and everyday wear. Both cost **about $33**, and both are covered by MIER's **free 30-day returns**.
