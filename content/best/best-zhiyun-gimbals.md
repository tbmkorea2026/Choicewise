## How to choose a ZHIYUN gimbal

### 1. Start with your camera's weight

Weigh your camera with its lens, battery and memory card. For a **small mirrorless camera with a light lens**, the compact **Crane M3** makes sense; check its compatibility list, as the store page doesn't state a payload figure. For **full-frame mirrorless bodies or lighter DSLRs with standard zooms**, the **Weebill** models are rated at **3 kg**.

### 2. Decide which features you'll use

- **Native vertical shooting** for Reels, Shorts and TikTok: Weebill 3E and 3S.
- **A built-in fill light**: Crane M3 and Weebill 3S.
- **A large screen on the gimbal**: Weebill 2.
- **Image transmission or a follow-focus motor**: Weebill S, Weebill 2 and Weebill 3S support ZHIYUN's modules (sold separately or in bigger kits); the Weebill 3E doesn't.

### 3. Check camera compatibility and kit contents

ZHIYUN publishes camera compatibility and control lists for each gimbal. Most models come in **Standard, Combo or Pro** kits, so check what each kit includes before you order.
