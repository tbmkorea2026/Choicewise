## What the weight actually changes

Fabric weight measures how much wool is in each square meter of fabric. More wool means **more insulation**, but also **less breathability** when you're working hard.

- **250 g/m²** is Merino.tech's midweight: warm enough for most cold-weather activities, while still letting heat and moisture escape when you're moving.
- **320 g/m²** is Merino.tech's heavyweight and its warmest option: ideal when warmth matters more than breathability.

Both versions keep merino's other benefits: **moisture-wicking**, **natural odor resistance** and a soft feel, according to Merino.tech.

| Winter camping, ice fishing, standing around outdoors | 320 |
| Everyday wear in cool weather | 250 |

## The half-zip advantage

Whichever weight you choose, the **half zip** lets you open the neck to dump heat when you warm up and close it when you stop, which makes either version more versatile than a crew neck.
