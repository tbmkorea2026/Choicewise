## How to choose GeekShare accessories

### 1. Pick the right console version

GeekShare sells **separate versions** for the original Switch family and **Switch 2**, and some colors or versions sell out. Double-check the version before you add to cart.

### 2. Silicone case or case with stand?

**Silicone cases** (Star Wings, Cream Strawberry) are soft and easy to fit, with a frosted coating. The **Star Wing case for Switch 2** adds a **built-in kickstand** for tabletop play. See our [Star Wings vs Cream Strawberry](/best/geekshare-star-wings-vs-cream-strawberry-case/) comparison.

### 3. Bundle to get free shipping

Orders over **$40** ship free in most countries. A single case is about $22, so free shipping usually means buying two or three matching pieces.

## Buying tips

- International delivery takes **3–20 business days**; order ahead for gifts.
- **Returns** need prior approval within 30 days, and the policy is unclear on change-of-mind returns; there's a **6-month one-time exchange** for the same style.
