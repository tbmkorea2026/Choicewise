## How to choose

- **Wrist under about 16.5cm (6.5")?** Look at the 38mm or the Icon II (46mm lugs). Compare sizes in [38mm vs 40mm](/best/seagull1963-38mm-vs-40mm/).
- **Want bronze?** Icon II Green, or the Bronze 40mm Lume for less. Read our [Icon II Green review](/reviews/seagull1963-icon-ii-green-review/).
- **Want something dressier?** The Moonphase 40mm.

## Buying tips

- **Ignore urgency banners** ("viewed by…", "last pieces"); they're sales tactics.
- **Decide before you order:** you can only cancel within 30 minutes, and change-of-mind returns cost return shipping plus €30.
- **Wind it daily:** all five are hand-wound.
- **Know the brand:** it's an independent company using Seagull movements. See our full [Seagull 1963 review](/reviews/seagull1963-review/).
