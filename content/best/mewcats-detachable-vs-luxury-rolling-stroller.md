## The key differences

### 1. Clear bubble vs fabric canopy

The **Stylish Detachable** gives your cat an **all-round view** through a clear dome, which confident cats may enjoy and nervous cats may not. The **Luxury Rolling Stroller** has a **fabric body with a canopy and mesh windows**, so your cat can see out but also hide, and it's better shaded.

### 2. Size and weight limit

The **Luxury Rolling Stroller** is **longer and wider** at 39.3" × 18.7" and states a **33 lb** limit. The **Stylish Detachable** is more compact in footprint (27.5" × 14.9") but taller at 40.5", and its "weight within 55lbs" note isn't explained.

### 3. Price

At the time of writing, the gap is just **$17**: **$282.82** vs **$299.68**, before the 10% code.
