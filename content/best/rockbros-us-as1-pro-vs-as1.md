- **Want to set a pressure and let the pump stop by itself?** Get the **AS1 Pro** ($89.99).

## Ease of use

This is the biggest difference. The **AS1 Pro** works like a mini compressor: set the target, attach, and it stops. The **AS1** has no screen or auto shutoff, so you pump in bursts and check pressure with a separate gauge.

## Pressure and battery

The Pro's **120 PSI** and bigger battery suit high-pressure road tires; ROCKBROS also lists it as faster. The AS1's **100 PSI** and single full inflation per charge are fine for MTB, gravel and wide road tires, especially for top-ups.

## Before you buy either

- **Returns:** 30 days if unused, but change-of-mind returns cost you shipping, and sale items are final sale.
- **Warranty:** 2-year limited on direct purchases.
- **Battery claims vary:** the marketing says up to four tires per charge for the Pro; the spec table says two full inflations.

## The bottom line

The **AS1 Pro** is the easier, more capable pump and worth the $30 if you ride road tires or want automatic stopping. The **AS1** is a lighter, cheaper backup for lower pressures.
