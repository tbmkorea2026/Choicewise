## Quick verdict

- **Want the smallest board that still has arrows and a function row, and like linear red switches?** Get the **K2** ($69.99).
- **Want a standard navigation cluster, or blue or brown switches?** Get the **K8** (from $59.99).

## Layout and size

The difference you'll feel every day is the layout. The **K2** squeezes the navigation keys into a single column on the right, so Home, End and Page Up/Down sit next to Enter. The **K8** keeps the standard tenkeyless block with Insert, Delete, Home and End above normally spaced arrows. It's about **40 mm wider** and roughly **80–90 g heavier** depending on the frame.

## Switches and stock

This may decide it for you. The K2 was in stock in **one configuration only**: RGB backlight, aluminum frame, **Keychron Super Red** (linear). The K8 had **ten versions** in stock, including **white or RGB backlight** and **red, blue or brown** switches, with the hot-swappable Keychron Super versions costing $10 more than Gateron G Pro.

## Battery and connectivity

Both pair with **three devices over Bluetooth** and work wired over USB-C with a 1000 Hz polling rate. Both have a **4000 mAh** battery. Keychron lists the K2 at **240 hours** with the backlight off and the K8 at **68 hours**; the K8 page doesn't say whether its figure is with backlight on.

## Ratings and warranty

The K2 is rated **4.87 from 950 reviews**, the K8 **4.78 from 457**. Both are classic K-series boards with a **12-month warranty** under which Keychron may replace the keyboard or its parts. Change-of-mind returns must be requested within 7 days at your cost. See our [Keychron review](/reviews/keychron-review/).

## The bottom line

If the single in-stock K2 suits you, it's the more compact, better-reviewed choice. If you want a standard layout or a different switch feel, the **K8** gives you more options and starts $10 cheaper.
