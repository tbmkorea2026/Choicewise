On APS-C cameras, multiply the focal length by about 1.5 to get the full-frame equivalent. A 35mm lens behaves like a 52.5mm "normal" lens, and a 50mm lens like a 75mm portrait lens.

## Autofocus or manual focus?

- **Choose autofocus** (AF 25/35/50, AF 35 Full-Frame, AF 10mm) if you shoot moving subjects, kids, pets, events or vlogs, or if you simply want point-and-shoot convenience.
- **Choose manual focus** (50mm f/0.95, 7.5mm fisheye) if you enjoy a more deliberate style, want a very specific look such as extreme bokeh or a fisheye effect, and want to spend less.

> **Tip:** On manual lenses, turn on your Sony's **focus peaking** and enable **"Release w/o Lens"** in the menu, otherwise the shutter will not fire with a lens that has no electronic contacts.
