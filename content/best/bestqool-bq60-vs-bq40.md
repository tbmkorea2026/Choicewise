## Quick verdict

- **Treating the back, legs or larger areas?** Get the **BQ60** ($199).
- **Mostly face, hands or travel?** Get the **BQ40** ($149).

## Output and coverage

The BQ60 delivers about **37% more irradiance** at the same distance and covers roughly **2.8 times the area**, so one session can reach a whole back or both thighs. The BQ40's smaller beam suits the face, a hand or a single joint, but larger areas take several sessions.

## Size and setup

The **BQ40** weighs only **2.38 lb** and is under 1.3 inches thick, so it's the easier one to pack. The **BQ60** is still portable at **4.78 lb** and includes a **door hook and rope** for hands-free sessions.

## Before you buy either

- **Health claims are BestQool's own**; its disclaimer says the devices aren't intended to diagnose, treat or prevent disease.
- **Wear the included eyewear** and follow session times. Talk to a doctor first if you're pregnant, have a medical condition or take photosensitizing medication.
- **Returns:** 30 days, but change-of-mind returns cost you shipping. **Register within 14 days** to get the third warranty year.

## The bottom line

Same LEDs, same warranty, different size. The **BQ60** is the better all-round choice for most buyers; the **BQ40** makes sense if you mainly treat your face or want something light for travel.
