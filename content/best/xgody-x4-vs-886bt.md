- **Want the biggest screen?** Get the **X4BT** ($89.90). The 9-inch display is easier to read at a glance, especially in a truck.
- **Want the safer, cheaper pick?** Get the **886BT** ($78.90). It includes Bluetooth, and its **1,108 store reviews** give far more evidence than the X4's 9.

## Screen size vs evidence

The **X4's 9-inch screen** is its main draw: maps and turn arrows are larger, which helps older drivers and anyone in a big cab. But both screens are **800×480**, so you get the same detail, just bigger.

The **886BT** is smaller and lighter (253 g) and has been reviewed **over a thousand times** on XGODY's store at 4.6/5. The X4's 4.8/5 comes from just **9 reviews**, so there's much less to go on.

## Bluetooth and extras

The 886BT includes **Bluetooth hands-free calling** at its base price. For the X4 you need the **X4BT** ($4 more than the X4F). Both have an **FM transmitter** to play sound through your car speakers and support music and video. Bundles with a backup camera were out of stock for both.

## Before you buy either

- **Warranty is short:** 14-day refund or replacement for defects, 30-day replacement, then free repair up to 180 days. XGODY's Trustpilot score is 1.4/5, so pay with buyer protection.
- **Map updates are manual:** you'll need a computer to copy new map files.
- **Speed-camera alerts** are banned in some countries; turn them off if so.
- **Shipping:** free on most items, 4–10 working days to the US, UK and Australia.

## The bottom line

The **X4BT** is the one to get if a **big screen** matters most. The **886BT** is cheaper, includes Bluetooth and has far more customer feedback, making it the **safer budget choice**.
