## The short answer

- **Lowest price and simplest set-up:** **ProtoArc EC200** ($179.99) or **EC300** ($229.99).
- **Lumbar that moves with you, deeper recline or over 220 lb:** **Newtral H003** ($239.99) or **NT001** ($329.99).
- **Worried about returns:** Newtral's free change-of-mind returns are better on paper; ProtoArc's reputation is cleaner.

## Back support: follow vs set

**Newtral's auto-following lumbar** is designed to keep contact as you lean forward, sit upright or recline, which suits people who **move around a lot**. **ProtoArc's** approach is to **set it once**: the EC300 moves the whole backrest to your torso height, while the EC200's lumbar adjusts in height and depth. If you sit in one posture most of the day, a well-set fixed support works fine; if you fidget, recline and lean, auto-following may suit you better.

## Fit and size

ProtoArc lists height ranges of about 5'4"–6'1" and **220 lb max**. Newtral's H003 and H-BPro list **300 lb**, and the H-BPro covers 5'1"–6'3". Shorter or heavier users should start with Newtral's specs; but note one Trustpilot reviewer found a Newtral multi-posture chair too wide for a shorter frame.

## Trust and after-sales

This is where they differ most. Newtral's **2.3/5 Trustpilot** score comes largely from Kickstarter backers and slow-support complaints. ProtoArc has almost **no independent reviews** at all. Neither is ideal: buy with a card or PayPal, keep the box for 30 days, and register the warranty.
