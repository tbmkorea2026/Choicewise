## Why updates matter

A scanner is hardware plus a **software database** of vehicles and procedures. Updates add **new models**, **new protocols** (CAN-FD, DoIP) and **new service functions**. Brands either **include updates for life** or **charge a yearly fee** after a free period. Over 3–5 years, that fee can cost more than the scanner.
