## How to choose

- **Budget whole-yard coverage:** D1 Classic Kit.
- **Need to read faces or plates further away?** T2 Ultra (4K) or T2 Pro (two lenses). Compare them in [T2 Ultra vs T2 Pro](/best/aosu-t2-ultra-vs-t2-pro/).
- **Indoors:** P1 Pro.
- **Front door:** Video Doorbell Pro.

## Buying tips

- **Buy a kit for no-fee storage**; single cameras need a microSD card or cloud plan.
- **These record events, not 24/7.** For continuous recording, look at wired cameras.
- **Test within 30 days:** returns are allowed for any reason, but you pay return shipping. The warranty is 12 months.
- **Place solar panels in good sun**; charging stops below 14°F. See our full [aosu review](/reviews/aosu-review/).
