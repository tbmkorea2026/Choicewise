## How to choose a Brightin Star lens

### 1. Match the format to your camera

- **APS-C** (Fuji X, Sony a6000-series/ZV-E10, Nikon Z50, Canon R50, EOS-M): the 35mm F0.95 and 10mm fisheye are made for you.
- **Full-frame** (Sony a7, Nikon Z5/Z6, Canon R6, L-mount): pick the 35mm F1.4 or the 9mm F5.6.
- **Leica M**: the 28mm F2.8 pancake mounts natively; other systems need the adapter kit.

### 2. Be sure about the mount

Brightin Star charges a **40% restocking fee** (plus shipping both ways) if you order the wrong mount or model. Check twice before checkout.

### 3. Be ready for manual focus

All five are manual focus. Turn on your camera's **"release without lens"** setting and use **focus peaking**. The 10mm fisheye is designed for hyperfocal shooting, so it barely needs focusing.

## Buying tips

- **Free shipping** over $55, but Standard takes **18–25 business days** (Priority 15–18; Express 6–9).
- **Returns:** only for defects or shipping mistakes within 90 days; **no change-of-mind returns**, and orders placed with **discount codes** can't be returned.
- **Warranty:** 2-year limited warranty on manufacturing defects.
