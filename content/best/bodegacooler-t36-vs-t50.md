## Space vs weight

The T50's extra **15 quarts** come from height, not footprint, so both fit the same floor space. The cost is **12.8 lb** more weight, which the T50 offsets with wheels.

## Zones

The T36 splits into **two equal 19-quart zones**. The T50's zones are **30 and 23 quarts**, and the partition comes out if you'd rather have one big fridge or freezer.

## Before you buy either

- **Returns:** 30 days, unused and in the original box; change-of-mind returns cost you shipping both ways.
- **Warranty:** 2 years, +1 year if you register within 10 days.
- **Reviews:** strong on-site, but BodegaCooler's Trustpilot score is 2.6/5 from 16 reviews.

## The bottom line

Same footprint, same power, same cooling: the **T50** is the better buy for most people at nearly the same price, while the **T36** suits small vehicles and anyone who carries the fridge by hand.
