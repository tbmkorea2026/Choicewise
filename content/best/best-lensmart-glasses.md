## How to choose Lensmart glasses

### 1. Start with a pair that fits

Look inside the arm of your current glasses for three numbers, such as **52-16-140**: lens width, bridge width and arm length. Choose a frame whose **frame width** and **lens width** are within a few millimeters of yours. Lensmart measures its frames by hand, so expect slight variation.

### 2. Think about your prescription

- **Progressive lenses** need enough **lens height**. A shallow frame like the **Andrea** (35 mm) leaves less room for the reading zone.
- **Strong prescriptions** look thinner in smaller frames and with **high-index** lenses.

### 3. Pick the material

**TR90** frames (Sarah, Andrea, Minda, Ligeia) are light and flexible. **Acetate** (Sahana) is a classic, slightly heavier material with rich patterns. See our [Sarah vs Sahana](/best/lensmart-sarah-vs-sahana/) comparison.

### 4. Choose lenses deliberately

Some Lensmart descriptions mention "blue light protection", but lenses are sold separately. **Select blue-light, photochromic or polarized lenses** in the lens step if you want them.
