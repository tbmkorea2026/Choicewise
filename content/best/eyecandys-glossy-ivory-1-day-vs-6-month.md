## Quick verdict

- **Choose Glossy Ivory 1-Day** if you'll wear colored lenses **now and then** (events, weekends, photos, travel), want **no cleaning routine**, or want to **try the shade** before committing.
- **Choose the 6-month Glossy Ivory** if you'll wear the color **several times a week** and are happy to **clean and store** lenses properly.

> **Prescription first:** in the US, EyeCandys requires a valid contact lens prescription for both versions, including 0.00. Colored lenses are medical devices, so get your eyes checked and fitted first.

## Material and care

- **1-Day (MPC, 40% water):** EyeCandys says MPC helps retain moisture and resist deposits, and it pitches the 1-Day for **sensitive eyes**. Each lens is used once, so there's **no build-up** from reuse.
- **6-month (HEMA, 38% water):** a classic hydrogel. You'll need to **clean, rinse and store** the lenses in fresh solution after every wear and **replace the case** regularly.

## Stock

At the time of writing, the 6-month Glossy Ivory showed **many powers out of stock**, including **0.00** and most powers from **-1.25 to -5.00**. The 1-Day listed all powers from **0.00 to -8.00**. Check your power before you decide.

## The bottom line

Same color, same size, different routine. For occasional wear, **Glossy Ivory 1-Day** is the easier and more hygienic choice. If you'll wear the color most days, the **6-month pair** costs far less over time, as long as you look after it properly.
