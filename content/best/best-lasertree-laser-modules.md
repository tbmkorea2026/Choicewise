## How to choose

- **Check your machine first:** its frame must carry the weight, and its power supply must deliver the current.
- **Mostly cutting thick wood?** The **K30** or **K40**; compare them in [K30 vs K40](/best/lasertree-k30-vs-k40/).
- **Want the most power and can supply 10A?** The **K60**.
- **On a budget?** The **K20 Pro**, or the **CNC3018 module** for a 3018 router.

## Buying tips

- **Budget for safety:** 450 nm goggles, an enclosure and ventilation aren't optional at these powers.
- **Air assist helps:** K-series bundles with an air pump cost $40–$50 more.
- **Test right away:** replacement is free only within 15 days; after that LaserTree charges 15–20%.
