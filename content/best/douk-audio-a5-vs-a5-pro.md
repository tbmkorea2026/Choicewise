## Quick verdict

- **Building a 2.1 system with a subwoofer, using a DAC or streamer as the source?** Get the **A5** ($89.99): its high-pass filter is the feature the PRO lacks.
- **Want to stream from your phone, adjust bass and treble, or plug in headphones?** Get the **A5 PRO** ($114.99).

## The high-pass filter vs Bluetooth

The **A5's high-pass filter** (30–200Hz) removes deep bass from the speaker outputs. With small bookshelf speakers and a **powered subwoofer** on the AUX output, that lets the speakers play cleaner and louder while the sub handles the low end. It's a feature rarely found at this price.

The **A5 PRO** drops the filter for everyday convenience: **Bluetooth 5.1** for phones, **bass and treble** knobs to tune the sound, and a **headphone jack** for late-night listening. Its AUX output still feeds a sub, but full-range.

## Power and power supplies

Both amps use the same TPA3255 and publish the **same rated power**. The "300W×2" in the product titles is a maximum. The included **32V/5A** adapter gives **78W per channel** into 4 ohms; the **48V/5A** option gives **120W**, for $20 more on either model.
