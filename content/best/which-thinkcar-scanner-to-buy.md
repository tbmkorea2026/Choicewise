## What the jumps in price buy you

- **$57 → $79:** bi-directional control and three times as many resets.
- **$79 → $196:** a dedicated screen, but **fewer systems** (the 662 covers 4 main systems).
- **$196 → $500:** full-system diagnostics on a tablet, 35+ functions, ECU coding and gateway access.

## Avoid these mistakes

- **Buying a tablet to read one code.** A dongle does that.
- **Assuming "120+ brands" covers your car.** Use the compatibility check on the product page.
- **Ignoring renewals.** The ThinkDiag 2 includes only 1 year of updates; see [lifetime updates vs subscription](/best/obd2-scanner-lifetime-updates-vs-subscription/).
