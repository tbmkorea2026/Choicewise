## How each one goes on

**Gel strips** are already partly cured. You stick one on, press it down, file the excess at the tip and cure it under the lamp. Because the top coat is built in, that's it.

**Gel pens** work like a thin marker with a brush tip. NailSala's routine is two thin colour coats, a 60-second cure, a no-wipe top coat and a final cure. No base coat is needed, according to the listing.

**Solid gel** is a thick, paint-pot gel you apply with a brush. It doesn't run, which helps with designs, but you need a base coat underneath and a top coat on top.

## Taking it off

Strips peel off with a wooden stick. Painted gel (pens and solid gel) needs a **gel remover**; see our [step-by-step removal guide](/reviews/nailsala-gel-remover-kit-review/).

## Before you buy

- **All three need a UV/LED lamp**; see the [starter checklist](/best/nailsala-gel-nails-at-home-starter-checklist/) for lamp options and real totals.
- **Opened products can't be returned**, so try one method first.
- **Skin safety:** keep gel off your skin and cuticles, and stop if irritation appears.
