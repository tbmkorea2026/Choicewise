## How we chose and ranked them

Because all four models come from the same brand and share the same core features, we focused on what actually separates them: **driver type, speaker sensitivity match, power handling, size and price**. All information comes from Aperion's official product pages as of October 2026. We ranked them by how well they suit the **typical home listener**, which is why the most affordable PST comes first, and gave each model a "best for" badge for the buyer it suits best.

## What all four have in common

Every Aperion super tweeter shares the same easy-to-use features:

- **5 crossover points:** 8, 10, 12, 14 or 16 kHz, plus OFF, with a 12 dB/octave slope.
- **Treble adjust** from 0 to −5 dB in 1 dB steps.
- **No extra amplifier needed:** each connects to your speaker's existing binding posts.
- **3-year cabinet and driver warranty** with lifetime support, a **30-day in-home audition** and free shipping in the continental U.S.

So the real question is not which one has more features, but **which one matches your speakers**.

## Buying guide: how to choose the right model

### 1. Match your speakers' sensitivity

This is the most important step. A super tweeter needs to play at roughly the same level as your main speakers to blend in. Aperion publishes a matching range for each model:

| Your speaker sensitivity | Recommended model |
| --- | --- |
| 85–94 dB (most bookshelf & tower speakers) | **PST** (best value) or RST |
| 94–96 dB | **RST** or **DST** |
| 98–103 dB (horn & high-efficiency speakers) | **HST** |

You will find sensitivity in your speaker's specifications, usually written as "dB (2.83 V/1 m)" or "dB/W/m".

### 2. Consider the driver type

- **Planar-magnetic ribbon (PST):** a thin film with printed aluminum conductors. Light, detailed and affordable.
- **True aluminum ribbon (RST):** an ultra-thin 20 μm aluminum strip. The classic ribbon design.
- **Air Motion Transformer (DST, HST):** a pleated diaphragm in a dual-firing open-baffle design, which Aperion highlights for fast transients.

### 3. Check your amplifier

The DST is a **4 Ω** load, which is harder for some budget amplifiers to drive. The PST and RST are 6 Ω, and the HST is 8 Ω. If you are unsure, check your amplifier's minimum impedance rating.

### 4. Set your budget

Prices range from **$399 (PST) to $1,599 (HST) per pair**. For most people, the PST delivers the core benefit of a super tweeter at the lowest cost, and the 30-day in-home audition lets you test it before committing.

> **Tip:** Whichever model you choose, start with the crossover at 14 kHz and the treble at −2 dB, then adjust one step at a time. Read our full [Aperion PST review](/reviews/aperion-pst-super-tweeter-review/) for more setup advice.
