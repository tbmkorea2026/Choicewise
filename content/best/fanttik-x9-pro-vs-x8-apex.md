## Battery and speed

The X8 APEX's battery is about **55% bigger** (28.8 vs 18.5 Wh). Fanttik rates it **18 seconds faster** on the same 25-inch top-up test and good for **4 top-ups** per charge instead of 3. It also gives a real-world figure: **56 seconds** to take a 215/55 R17 tire from 28 to 33 PSI. The X9 Pro's page contradicts itself slightly ("1 min" in the feature text, 70 seconds in the table).

## Size and features

The **X9 Pro** weighs **0.96 lb** and has its hose built into the body: pull it out to switch on. The **X8 APEX** weighs **1.74 lb** but adds a **10 W power bank**, a **40 lm light**, a dual LCD, altitude-adaptive accuracy (±1 PSI, per Fanttik) and an operating range of 14 °F to 113 °F. Both have presets, a custom target and auto-stop.

## Ratings

The X8 APEX has **4.8 stars from 1,315 reviews** (only 4 one-star); the X9 Pro has **4.6 stars from 230**. Both are strong; the X8 APEX simply has a much longer track record on the store.

## Before you buy either

- Inflate to the pressure on your **door placard**, not the tire's or the pump's maximum.
- Neither battery is replaceable; charge before long trips.
- Fanttik ships within the US only (no Alaska, Hawaii, Rhode Island, PO boxes or military addresses), returns are accepted within 30 days (you pay return shipping unless it's damaged or wrong) and the warranty is 1 year.

## The bottom line

The **X9 Pro** is the better buy if weight and price matter most and you drive a car rather than an SUV. The **X8 APEX** is the more capable all-rounder for $27 more.
