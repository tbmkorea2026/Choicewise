## How the features differ

- **Recline:** H003 goes to 126°; NT001, NT002 and H-BPro go to 136°.
- **Footrest:** only NT002 and H-BPro (plus the H-BP) among these.
- **Armrests:** 3D (H003), 4D nylon (NT001), 4D chrome (NT002), 4D metal (H-BPro).
- **Headrest:** H-BPro has a 5D auto-following headrest; NT002 lists a 2D headrest.

## What all of them share

- **3-year warranty** and **free 30-day returns** in the US, EU and UK, if the chair is like-new and boxed.
- **Flat-pack delivery**, about 20–25 minutes to assemble (per Newtral, for the H003).
- **Not a medical device:** comfort aids, not treatments.
