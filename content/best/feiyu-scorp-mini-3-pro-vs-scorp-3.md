## Quick verdict

- **Choose the SCORP Mini 3 Pro** if your camera and lens weigh **comfortably under 2 kg**, you want **one gimbal for camera and phone**, need **object tracking**, or travel light.
- **Choose the SCORP 3** if you use a **DSLR, a heavier full-frame body or large-aperture zoom lenses**, where the **3.5 kg payload** matters most.

## The key differences

### 1. Payload

This is the deciding factor. The **SCORP 3's 3.5 kg payload** is designed for DSLRs and large-aperture zoom lenses, while the **Mini 3 Pro's 2 kg payload** suits lighter mirrorless setups. FeiyuTech recommends lenses up to about **90 mm long and 75 mm in diameter** for the Mini 3 Pro.

### 2. Cameras and phones

The **Mini 3 Pro is a 4-in-1 gimbal** that also works with smartphones. The **SCORP 3 is camera-only**.

### 3. Tracking

Both use **AI Tracking 4.0** with gesture control and an 18 m range, but FeiyuTech lists **face and object tracking** on the Mini 3 Pro and **face tracking only** on the SCORP 3.

### 4. Price

The Mini 3 Pro is roughly **$100 cheaper** at the time of writing.

## What they share

Both gimbals have a **1.3-inch OLED touchscreen**, **remote control up to 12 m**, **native vertical shooting**, **up to 14 hours of battery** and the same set of creative shooting modes. Both connect to cameras over **Bluetooth**, and some cameras need an **optional Feiyu control cable**; check the compatibility lists for your model.
