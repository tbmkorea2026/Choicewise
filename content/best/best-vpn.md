## How we chose the best VPNs

We scored each VPN on **privacy and security (30%)**, **speed (25%)**, **streaming (15%)**, **ease of use (15%)** and **value (15%)**. Privacy scores consider independent audits, jurisdiction and logging policies. See our [review methodology](/how-we-review/).

## VPN buying guide

### No-logs policies and audits

Any VPN can claim not to keep logs. Look for providers whose policy has been **independently audited**, and ideally servers that run in RAM only.

### Speed

Modern protocols like WireGuard deliver much faster speeds than older ones. Choose a provider with servers close to you for everyday use.

### Price and plan length

VPNs are cheapest on long-term plans. Use the money-back guarantee to test speeds and streaming before committing.
