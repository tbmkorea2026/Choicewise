## Buying tips

- **Check local rules:** e-scooter rules vary by province and city.
- **Test within 10 km:** change-of-mind returns are free only within 30 days and 10 km on the odometer.
- **Plan for winter:** cold weather cuts battery range, so don't count on the full claim.
