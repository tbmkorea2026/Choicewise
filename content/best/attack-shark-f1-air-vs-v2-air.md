## Shape and grip

The **F1 AIR** is short and narrow, built for **small to medium hands** and **claw or fingertip** grips where low weight matters most for flicks. The **V2 AIR** has a **balanced rear hump, inward-curved waist and thumb groove**, giving **palm** and relaxed claw players more support; one owner compares its shape to the classic Microsoft IntelliMouse Optical 1.1.

## Weight and battery

The F1 AIR saves **13 g** by using a smaller **250 mAh** battery. The V2 AIR's **300 mAh** cell gives more runtime. Owners report around 20 hours of play at 8K on the V2 AIR, far below the 1K headline figures, so most players use 1K–2K day to day.
