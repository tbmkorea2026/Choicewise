## Technology

The **Dermisonic II** adds **radio frequency**, which Opatra describes as heat that works under the skin and helps trigger collagen, plus **blue and green LED** modes. The **LumiQuartz** uses **six red LEDs**, heat and vibration through a rose quartz stone, which Opatra says helps with product absorption and puffiness.

More modes don't guarantee better results. Neither page links to published trials for the device itself.

## Routine

The **LumiQuartz** works like a massage step: apply serum or oil, then glide the wand upward in circles. The **Dermisonic II** uses touchscreen modes that each run for **10 minutes**.

## Ratings and evidence

Both pages show **5.0/5**, but from only **18** and **5** reviews. Opatra's Trustpilot score is **4.5/5 from 1,054 reviews**, with 9% 1-star; see our [Opatra review](/reviews/opatra-review/).

- **Returns:** only unused items within 30 days, and you pay return shipping.
- **Warranty:** UK and EU only, needs registration, and has replacement fees.
- **Safety:** ask Opatra for contraindications, and ask a doctor if you're pregnant, have implants or a skin condition, or use light-sensitizing medicine.
