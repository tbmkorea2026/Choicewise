## How to choose

1. **Length of the vehicle.** Under about 20 ft (cars, SUVs, pickups), 52 ft of range is enough. Trailers and RVs need the Solar 5 series or WF4.
2. **Mount.** Plate brackets stay put; magnets let you move the camera between a truck and a trailer.
3. **Recording.** Want footage of a bump? Pick the Plus Max, 5B or WF4.
4. **More views.** All support a second camera; only the WF4 shows three at once.
