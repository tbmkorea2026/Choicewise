## How to choose

- **Certification matters to you?** Stick to models listing **NSF/ANSI 58** (X12, G3P800, G3P600) and read the performance data sheet. **NSF/ANSI 372** alone only covers low-lead materials.
- **Water waste?** The **3:1** X12 and G3P800 drain less than the 2:1 models.
- **Hot drinks and formula?** The **K6** replaces a kettle.
- **Prefer mineral-added water?** **X12** or **G5P700A** (taste benefits are real for many people; health claims are marketing).
- **Budget?** Compare [G3P600 vs G3P800](/best/waterdrop-g3p600-vs-g3p800/) before stepping up to the X12.

## Buying tips

- **Test your water first**, especially well water, which may need extra treatment.
- **Check the sale code** on each product page; prices are almost always shown as discounts.
- **Plan for filters:** about $111–$173 a year on these models (5% off with a subscription).
- **Returns:** 30 days with no restocking fee, but you pay return shipping unless it's defective; 10% fee after 30 days.
- **Installation:** you need an outlet under the sink and to connect to the cold-water line and drain. See our full [Waterdrop review](/reviews/waterdrop-review/).
