## Quick verdict

- **Small wrist, or love dainty vintage watches?** Get the **2946L** ($64.99): an oval case on a slim 8 mm bracelet, just 32 g.
- **Want a bolder, jewellery-like piece, or a slightly larger fit?** Get the **2166L** ($69.99): a square case on a 16 mm link bracelet that fits up to 180 mm.

## Look and feel

The **2946L** has a soft **oval case** inspired (per Berny) by vintage copper mirrors, on a **thin 8 mm** bracelet. It's light and understated, the kind of tiny watch that's back in fashion.

The **2166L** has a **rectangular case** with gently curved edges on a **wide 16 mm** gold link bracelet. At 52 g it has more presence on the wrist and reads more like a bracelet with a watch in it.

## What's the same

Both are **gold-tone quartz watches** with **mineral glass**, **3 ATM** water resistance, **no lume** and a **solid case back**, and both come with Berny's **12-month warranty**.

## Before you buy either

- **Inspect on delivery:** Berny only accepts defect claims within **24 hours** of delivery.
- **Returns:** 30 days for change of mind, but you pay return shipping and there are no exchanges, so check wrist size first.
- **Splash-resistant only:** take them off before showering or swimming.
- Buy only from **bernywatch.com** or Berny's official marketplace stores.

## The bottom line

Pick the **2946L** for a **dainty oval** look and a lighter feel, or the **2166L** for a **bolder square** watch with a wider bracelet and a slightly bigger fit. Both are good value at under $70.
