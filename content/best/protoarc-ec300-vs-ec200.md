## Back support

On the **EC300**, the lumbar and sacrum support is part of the backrest, so moving the back keeps that support aligned with it. On the **EC200**, the lumbar moves independently and also pushes forward or back by 0.8 inches. Pick the EC300 if your main issue is backrest height, and the EC200 if you want to tune how firmly the lumbar presses.
