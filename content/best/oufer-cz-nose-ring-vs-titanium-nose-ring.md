## Material

This is the main difference. **F-136 titanium** is the alloy used for implants and is the usual pick for nickel-sensitive skin. **F-138 steel** is implant-grade too, but contains some nickel. If you've reacted to cheap earrings before, choose titanium.

## Look and fit

The CZ ring's stones sit along the front for a sparkly line on the nostril or helix. The titanium ring is a plain polished band, and its half sizes help you get a snug fit.
