## Quick verdict

- **PC only and want to save money?** Get the **Plus X** ($179–$229).
- **Own a supported Fanatec or Thrustmaster wheelbase, or want the newer sensors?** Get the **Plus X V2** ($208.50–$258.70), if you can wait for a pre-order.

## Sensors and feel

Both use a 200 kg load cell. The V2 also fits **15-bit angle sensors** and combines pressure and angle data, which SIMSONN says gives more precise, consistent input. SIMSONN also says its stronger throttle spring reduces the sticky feel linked to hydraulic damping. We can't confirm how much difference this makes in practice.

## Compatibility

This is the clearest reason to pay more. The original page says **PC only**. The V2 can connect by **RJ12** to supported Fanatec and Thrustmaster wheelbases, opening a route to select PS5, PS4 and Xbox setups. You'll need the right wheelbase and cable, and PC software features may not carry over. If you race only on PC, this benefit doesn't apply to you.

## Build and baseplate

The V2 has a glass-bead-blasted matte finish and an integrated shaft-bushing design meant to reduce wear. Its optional baseplate is CNC-machined 6000-series aluminum and adds about **$89**, while the original's adds **$50–$59**. The V2 is also heavier: its 3-pedal package with baseplate is listed at 12.8 kg, against 8.84 kg on the original's specs.

## Ratings and risks

The V2 page shows **4.8/5 from 596 reviews**, the same figure as the Plus X with Haptics. The V2 listing is recent, so these reviews **can't all be about the V2**. Treat it as an unproven revision for now. The comparison site OC Racing also reports uneven quality control on the original Plus X, which we haven't verified, and it's unclear whether the V2 changes that. SIMSONN's Trustpilot score is 2.4 from 9 reviews; see our [SIMSONN review](/reviews/simsonn-review/).

## The bottom line

About $30 is a small step up if you need wheelbase compatibility or want the updated design, as long as a pre-order wait is fine. For PC-only racers on a tight budget who want to buy now, the original still has the same load cell rating.
