## Step 1: work out your daily energy

List each device's watts × hours per day. Example: fridge 1.2kWh, lights 0.3kWh, laptop and router 0.5kWh = **2kWh/day**. Add 25% margin → **2.5kWh**. That's a 12V 324Ah battery (~4kWh) on a 24V system, or comfortably within the 16kWh battery for several days of autonomy.

## Step 2: check your peak power

Add up what runs **at the same time**. A kettle (2kW) plus a microwave (1kW) already needs a 3kW+ inverter. Heat pumps, ovens and well pumps push you to **48V and 6kW+**.

## Step 3: choose 24V or 48V

- **24V:** cheaper, simpler, up to ~3kW (DATOUBOSS 3000W, €179.99).
- **48V:** for 6kW+ loads and big batteries (6200W IP54, €349.99; 12kW all-in-one, €3,799.99).
