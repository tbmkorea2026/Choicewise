- **Plan to grow?** Only the **Pro** panels can be linked. Compare the small panels in [BQ60 vs BQ40](/best/bestqool-bq60-vs-bq40/).

## Safety and buying tips

- **Health claims are the brand's own.** BestQool's disclaimer says its products aren't intended to diagnose, treat or prevent disease. See a doctor for pain or skin conditions.
- **Wear eye protection** and follow the session times in the manual.
- **Talk to a doctor first** if you're pregnant, have a medical condition or take photosensitizing medication.
