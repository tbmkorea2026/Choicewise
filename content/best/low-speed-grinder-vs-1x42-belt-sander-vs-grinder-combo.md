## Heat: the thing that ruins edges

A standard bench grinder spins at about 3450 RPM. The TDS-200C4HL runs at **1750 RPM**, and the BD1801DS can switch down to **1750 RPM**. The BG2600 only runs at **3450 RPM**, so it's better for tools that don't mind a bit of heat (axes, mower blades, garden shears) than for fine chisels.

## Shape: round wheel vs flat belt

Round wheels leave a **hollow grind**, which is fine (even preferred) on chisels and plane irons. Belts give a **flatter or convex** edge and follow a knife's curve, which is why knife makers favour narrow belt sanders like a 1x42 or 2x72.

## Power and versatility

The two 3/4 HP machines won't stall as easily under pressure. The BG2600's 1/3 HP is enough for sharpening but the most versatile of the three only because it has both a belt and a wheel.
