## How to choose

- **Replacing a battery?** Match the **mount and plug** first. Down-tube Hailong owners should compare the [Hailong 1-2 vs Hailong 13Ah](/best/yosepower-hailong-1-2-vs-hailong-13ah/), because their plugs differ.
- **Have a Mifa, Rehberg, Zündapp or Victoria bike with an Ansmann system?** Check the **Ansmann rear rack** battery's model list.
- **Building a DIY e-bike?** The **Hailong 1-2** gives the most range; the **rear-carrier** batteries suit frames without down-tube space.
- **Want a rear light built in?** Only the **taillight** model has one.
