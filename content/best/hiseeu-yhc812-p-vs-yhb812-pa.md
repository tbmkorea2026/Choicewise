## Dome vs bullet

**Domes** sit flush against a ceiling or eave, look less obvious and are harder to knock out of position. **Bullets** stick out on an arm, which makes them easy to aim at a specific spot and more noticeable, a plus if you want people to see they're being recorded.

## Before you buy either

- You'll need a **PoE NVR or switch** (or 12V adapter) and Ethernet cable runs.
- Hiseeu says you **may need to change the IP address** when adding the camera to an NVR.
- **Returns:** free within 30 days; **warranty:** 1 year.
- Change default passwords and keep firmware updated.
