## Running costs: belts vs gel pads

The **Ultra** line uses belts, and Ape Born says there's nothing to replace. The **NextGen** sets stick on with **gel pads** that lose their stickiness over time; a 14-pack costs **$29.99** (or $22.49 on subscription). Over a year of regular use, the gel pads can close much of the price gap. See [Ultra vs NextGen](/best/apeborn-ultra-vs-nextgen/).

## Add-ons to skip (or think twice about)

- **Elite Club trial:** bundled with the Ultra offer; **$49.99/month** after 7 days unless cancelled.
- **Lifetime Warranty ($29.99)** and **Shipping Protection ($9.99):** pre-ticked as "free" in the bundle; check what's in your cart.
- **Duplicate listings:** the Ultra stimulator appears at both $149.99 and $169.99. Pick the cheaper one.

## Safety

Don't use EMS with a pacemaker or implanted electronics, during pregnancy, with epilepsy or heart conditions, on damaged skin, or across the chest or head. Start low, and ask a doctor if unsure. Results are modest strength gains, not fat loss; see [do EMS ab belts work?](/best/do-ems-ab-belts-work-apeborn-ultra/).
