## How to choose

- **Need an amp for passive speakers on a budget?** The **A5**, or the **A5 PRO** if you want Bluetooth (see [A5 vs A5 PRO](/best/douk-audio-a5-vs-a5-pro/)).
- **Many sources (TV, PC, phone) and a vintage look?** The **ST-01 PRO**.
- **Want every feature and a screen?** The **Ampapa D1**.
- **Already own several amps or speaker pairs?** The **VU3PRO** switches between them.
- **Your DAC or receiver lacks USB?** The **U2 PRO**.

## Buying tips

- **Check rated power, not "300W":** pick the higher-voltage adapter if your speakers need more power.
- **Returns:** request within 30 days of delivery; approval is required and change-of-mind return shipping is likely on you.
- **Warranty:** 24 months for manufacturing defects per Douk's warranty page.
- **Duties:** included at checkout unless noted. See our full [Douk Audio review](/reviews/douk-audio-review/).
