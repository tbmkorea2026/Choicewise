## Buying guide: what to look for

### Noise cancelling (ANC)

Active noise cancelling uses microphones to reduce background noise. It is most effective against steady, low-frequency sounds like engines and air conditioning. If you commute or travel often, it is worth paying for.

### Fit and comfort

A good seal improves both bass and noise cancelling. Look for multiple ear tip sizes, and consider ear hooks or wings if you plan to exercise.

### Battery life

Most earbuds offer 6–10 hours per charge, with the case adding several more full charges. Wireless charging and fast charging are useful extras.

### Codecs and compatibility

AAC works well with iPhones, while LDAC and aptX can offer higher quality on compatible Android phones. For most listeners, tuning matters more than codec.
