## The key differences

### 1. One cylinder or two

The **Cross Twin** arranges two cylinders in a cross layout around a central flywheel, which is more mechanically interesting to watch. The **Mirror Polished** model is a classic single-cylinder design.

### 2. How output is measured and used

Both measure output with a voltmeter. The Cross Twin adds an **indicator light** and a **power generation device** that NikolaToy says increases voltage and current. The Mirror Polished model integrates its voltmeter into the base and adds an **output port** for powering small external devices.

### 3. Looks and footprint

The Mirror Polished engine, as the name suggests, has a **mirror-polished finish** on a compact black base, making it the neater desk piece. The Cross Twin is wider, with two burners on a clear base.

## What they share

Both convert **heat into mechanical and then electrical energy**, run without batteries, and appear in NikolaToy's photos with **alcohol burners**, so both involve an open flame and should be used on a heat-resistant surface with supervision for children.
