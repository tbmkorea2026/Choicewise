## What each machine can't do

- **Grinders** can't flatten wide surfaces or follow long curves.
- **The BD4603** isn't built for knife bevels; its belt is wide.
- **The BD1801DS** is slow for big flat wood panels; its belt is narrow.
- **The BG2600's** single 1/3 HP motor slows under heavy pressure.
- **The DC50** is for wood dust, not metal sparks.

## Mixing wood and metal: a safety note

If you grind steel and sand wood in the same shop, **clean the sander and collector before switching**. Hot sparks landing in wood dust can smoulder. Wear eye protection on every machine, and a dust mask when sanding.
