## Quick verdict

- **Ride rough roads or long distances, and want GPS anti-theft?** Get the **Engine Pro 3.0 Boost** (€1,699).
- **Mostly smooth roads, want to save €400 and get a 2-year warranty?** Get the **EP-2 3.0 Boost** (€1,299).

## Suspension and power

The **Engine Pro 3.0 Boost**'s **rear suspension** makes a real difference on cobbles, potholes and gravel, and its **90 Nm** motor helps more on hills. The **EP-2 3.0 Boost** relies on its fat tires and front fork, fine for smoother city riding.

## Battery and charging

The Engine Pro's **720 Wh** battery gives a bit more range at low assist, and its **8 A** charger refills faster. At full assist (PAS 5), ENGWE quotes **80 km** for both.

## Security and warranty

The Engine Pro's **IoT module** (GPS, 4G, Bluetooth) enables tracking and anti-theft features through the app. The EP-2 lacks it but gets a **24-month** warranty on its frame, motor and battery, double ENGWE's standard 12 months.

## Before you buy either

- Both are heavy for folding bikes (32–35 kg).
- **Returns:** 30 days; change-of-mind returns under 10 km with **€200** deducted.
- Wear a helmet and keep the bike unmodified.

## The bottom line

Choose the **Engine Pro 3.0 Boost** for **full suspension, more torque and GPS anti-theft**; choose the **EP-2 3.0 Boost** to **save €400** and get a **2-year warranty**.
