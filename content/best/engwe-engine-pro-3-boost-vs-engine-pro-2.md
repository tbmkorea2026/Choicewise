## Power and charging

The **3.0 Boost**'s **90 Nm** hub motor gives noticeably more pull from a stop and on climbs than the 2.0's **75 Nm**. Its **8 A charger** refills the battery in about **2 hours**, versus about **5.5 hours** on the 2.0, handy if you ride twice a day.

## Battery and weight

The **2.0** actually has the **bigger battery** (768 Wh) and weighs **3.1 kg less**, which matters when you fold and lift it. ENGWE still quotes more range for the 3.0 Boost thanks to its newer system, but both are rated at roughly 110–130 km in the lowest assist.
