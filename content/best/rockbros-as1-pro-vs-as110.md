- **Road bike, or you like exact pressures?** Get the **AS1 PRO** (A$139.99): set the PSI, and it stops automatically, up to 120 PSI.

## Screen vs simplicity

The **AS1 PRO's** screen is the main reason to pay more: choose PSI or bar, **set your target** and walk away while it **stops on its own**. That's handy for road tyres where a few PSI matter. The **AS110** keeps it simple with **LED lights** for battery and pressure, and an **aluminium body** with a silicone cover to manage heat.

Note that the AS110's page **contradicts itself** on maximum pressure (100 PSI in the name, 120 PSI in the spec table). Assume 100 PSI if you run high-pressure road tyres.

## What owners say

AS1 PRO reviewers like how small it is and how fast it fills MTB tyres, but several say it's **loud**, one found the **side power button** turns it on in a pocket, and one reported a **valve deformed by heat**. The AS110 has fewer reviews (11) and a lower 4.18/5 average.

## Before you buy either

- Electric mini pumps are slower and noisier than a floor pump at home; they're for **roadside top-ups and flats**.
- **Returns:** 14 days, new condition, you pay return shipping.
- **Warranty:** 12 months on accessories.

## The bottom line

Pick the **AS1 PRO** for **precise, hands-off inflation to 120 PSI**; pick the **AS110** if you mainly ride lower-pressure tyres and want to **save A$40**.
