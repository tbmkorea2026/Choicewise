## How to choose a FeiyuTech gimbal

### 1. Start with payload

- **Up to ~1.2 kg** (small mirrorless body + light prime): SCORP Mini 2.
- **Up to 2 kg** (mirrorless + standard zoom): SCORP Mini 3 Pro.
- **Up to 2.5 kg** (larger mirrorless or DSLR): SCORP 2 or SCORP-C.
- **Heavier rigs:** look at the 3.5 kg SCORP 3. See our [SCORP Mini 3 Pro vs SCORP 3](/best/feiyu-scorp-mini-3-pro-vs-scorp-3/) comparison.

### 2. Decide which features you'll use

**AI tracking** and **native vertical shooting** are most useful for solo creators and social video. A **touchscreen** makes on-the-fly adjustments easier than buttons and an app.

### 3. Check camera compatibility

FeiyuTech publishes Bluetooth, cable and lens compatibility lists for each gimbal. Some cameras need an **optional control cable** for full control.
