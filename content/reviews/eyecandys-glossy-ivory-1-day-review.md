## Overview

**Glossy Ivory 1-Day** tops EyeCandys' best-seller list at the time of writing. It's the daily disposable version of the store's popular **Glossy Ivory** shade: a creamy **beige-taupe** colored lens with **no limbal ring**, designed to lighten **dark brown eyes**. A box has **10 lenses (5 pairs)** for **$29**.

We haven't worn these lenses ourselves, so this review is based on EyeCandys' published specs, policies and customer ratings, and it flags what to check before you buy.

> **Prescription and safety:** colored contacts are medical devices. In the US, EyeCandys requires a **valid contact lens prescription** for every order, including 0.00 lenses. Get your eyes checked and fitted by an eye doctor first.

## Look and color

EyeCandys describes Glossy Ivory as a **single-tone beige-taupe** with a soft gradient and **no dark outer ring**, so it blends into your own iris rather than outlining it. The **12.7 mm graphic diameter** is close to a typical iris size, so it shouldn't make your eyes look much bigger. On brown eyes, EyeCandys says it lightens them by **up to 3–4 shades**.

Buyers in the pooled reviews often describe the effect as **natural and subtle**, while a few note that the color shows more strongly in some lighting than others. Colored lenses always look different on each person, so treat product photos as a guide.

## Who should buy it?

**A good fit if you:**

- Have dark eyes and want a soft, lighter look.
- Wear colored lenses now and then rather than daily.
- Don't want to clean and store reusable lenses.

**Think twice if you:**

- Need a base curve other than 8.7, or plus (farsighted) powers.
- Want US FDA clearance listed on the product page.
- Would wear them every day (a reusable lens costs less).

## The bottom line

Glossy Ivory 1-Day is an easy, low-commitment way to try EyeCandys' most popular natural shade, with clear specs and a fair price per wear. Get a prescription and fitting first, check the listed certifications, and order a single box before stocking up.
