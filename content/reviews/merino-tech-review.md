## Overview

**Merino.tech** makes clothing from **100% superfine Australian merino wool**: base layers, long sleeves, half zips, tees, pants, hoodies, underwear and beanies for men, women and kids. The brand's story is that it set out to replace plastic-based "technical" fabrics after a hiking trip in Australia's Blue Mountains, and it positions merino as nature's own performance fabric.

This review covers the range, fabric weights, prices, customer ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on Merino.tech's website and policies as of October 2026.

## Why merino wool?

Merino.tech highlights the properties merino is known for:

- **Thermoregulation:** it helps keep you warm when it's cold and breathes when you're warm.
- **Odor resistance:** merino naturally resists odor, so you can wear it for multiple days on the trail or while traveling.
- **Moisture-wicking:** it moves sweat away from the skin.
- **Softness:** Merino.tech says its superfine fibers feel soft against the skin, with "no itch".

## The fabric-weight system

Merino.tech labels every garment by **fabric weight** in grams per square meter, which makes choosing easy:

| Weight | Label | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **165** | Lightweight | Warm weather, travel tees, layering |
| **190** | Performance | Running and high-output activity, underwear |
| **200–260** | Midweight | All-round base layers for hiking and cool weather |
| **320** | Heavyweight | Cold weather, skiing, winter camping |

## Prices

Prices are one of Merino.tech's selling points. At the time of writing, the 250 thermal sets (top and bottom) cost **$99.99**, half zips **$59.99–$64.99**, tees about **$45** and boxers about **$27**. "Build your set" bundles let you combine a top and bottom in your preferred weight, and the store runs regular promotions.

## Shipping

- **Free on orders of $49.99 or more**; $5.99 below that.
- **Orders before noon ship the same day.**
- Standard delivery takes **5–7 business days** via UPS or USPS; APO/FPO and PO boxes ship with USPS.

## Returns and warranty

- **30 days** from delivery to return eligible items in original condition with tags and packaging.
- **Free returns** are currently offered as a limited-time promotion, with **one prepaid return label per order**, so try everything on and send it back together. Extra labels are at your cost.
- **Socks, underwear and final-sale/clearance items** can't be returned.
- Refunds or exchanges are processed about **48 hours** after the return is inspected; you can also choose store credit with a 5% bonus.
- **Warranty:** 90 days as standard, extendable for free by **registering your purchase within 15 days**.

## Who should buy Merino.tech?

**A great fit if you:**

- Want to try merino base layers on a reasonable budget.
- Hike, ski, travel or work outdoors and want odor-resistant layers.
- Like clear fabric-weight labels to choose the right warmth.

**Think twice if you:**

- Want a long multi-year warranty.
- Plan to try underwear or socks and might want to return them.

## The bottom line

Merino.tech offers 100% merino wool clothing at accessible prices, with a clear fabric-weight system, strong customer ratings and a generous return offer. The short standard warranty and final-sale underwear are the main caveats. For most people looking for their first merino base layers, it is a smart, low-risk place to start.
