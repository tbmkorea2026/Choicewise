## What's inside: the ingredients

The formula leads with **hyaluronic acid**, a humectant that helps the skin hold on to water. It is then built around three groups of ingredients:

### 1. Seven peptides

The ingredient list contains seven peptides, which matches Le Mieux's "7 peptides" claim:

- **Acetyl hexapeptide-8**, widely known by the trade name *Argireline*
- **Palmitoyl pentapeptide-4**, widely known as *Matrixyl*
- **Acetyl octapeptide-3**
- **Acetyl tetrapeptide-5**
- **Palmitoyl tetrapeptide-7**
- **Palmitoyl tripeptide-1**
- **Dipeptide-2**

Peptides are among the most popular ingredients in anti-aging eye products. Several of these are commonly marketed for the appearance of expression lines, firmness and puffiness. How much difference they make depends on the formula, concentration and your skin.

### 2. Nourishing butters and antioxidants

**Cupuaçu butter** and **shea butter** add nourishing emollients, while **red ginseng**, **green and white tea extracts**, **tocotrienols (vitamin E)** and **superoxide dismutase** provide antioxidant support.

### 3. Soothing ingredients

**Panthenol (vitamin B5)**, **allantoin** and **alpha-bisabolol** are included, ingredients often used to help comfort and condition the skin.

**Notably, no fragrance appears in the ingredient list**, which is a plus for an eye product. The formula does include a long list of botanical extracts, so if your skin is very reactive, patch-test before applying it near your eyes.

## How to use it

Le Mieux recommends applying the corrector **morning and night**, using a fingertip to gently pat it around the eye area. Use your ring finger for the lightest touch, apply after cleansing and toning, and follow with your eye cream or moisturizer, and with sunscreen in the morning.

> **Tip:** Keep it out of the eyes themselves and apply along the orbital bone rather than on the eyelid.
