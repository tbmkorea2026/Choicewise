## Overview

The **Sarah** sits at **#1 on Lensmart's best-seller list** at the time of writing. It's a large, square women's frame with a bold black front and a two-tone tortoiseshell top edge, and it costs **$14.95 for the frame** (listed regular price $29.95). On the Lensmart store it's rated **4.9/5 from 2,226 reviews**, the most reviews of any frame in Lensmart's top five.

We haven't worn these glasses ourselves, so this review is based on Lensmart's published measurements, policies and customer ratings, and it flags what to check before you order.

## Design and materials

The Sarah is an **oversized square** shape with a slight cat-eye lift at the top corners. Lensmart lists it as **TR90 + metal**: TR90 is a lightweight, flexible plastic commonly used for eyeglass frames, and the metal accents sit at the hinges and temples. The color we've pictured is **Black-Brunette/Spots**; other colors are available on the product page.

## Size and fit

| Measurement | Sarah |
| --- | --- |

| Bridge width | 15 mm |
| Frame width | 142 mm |
| Arm length | 142 mm |

At **142 mm wide** with **55 mm lenses**, the Sarah is a **large frame** that suits medium-to-wide faces. The **15 mm bridge** is on the narrow side, which may matter if you have a wider nose bridge.

**Check your measurements first.** Compare these numbers with a pair of glasses that already fits you; frame width and lens width matter most. Lensmart's description says the lens height is **51 mm**, but its spec table says **45 mm**, and Lensmart notes all sizes are measured by hand. If lens height matters for your prescription, for example with progressives, ask Lensmart to confirm before ordering.

## Lens options

Like all Lensmart frames, the Sarah is sold **frame only** and you add lenses:

- **Single vision** for distance or reading
- **Progressive** (no-line) or **bifocal**
- **Non-prescription** lenses, such as blue-light blocking for screens

Lensmart says its lenses have **anti-scratch and anti-reflective coatings** and that prescription lenses start at **$5**. The description mentions "built-in blue light protection", but if you want it, **choose a blue-light lens explicitly**.

## What buyers say

Most of the reviews shown on the Sarah page praise its **style, light weight and price**. Note that these reviews are hosted by Lensmart itself; for an independent view of the store, Lensmart is rated **4.6/5 from about 11,600 reviews on Trustpilot**.

## Who should buy the Sarah?

**Buy it if you:**

- Like a bold, oversized square look.
- Have a medium-to-wide face (or already wear frames around 140 mm wide).
- Want inexpensive prescription or blue-light glasses.

**Look elsewhere if you:**

- Have a narrow face: the smaller **Andrea** (138 mm, 15.5 g) may fit better.
- Prefer an **acetate** frame: see our [Sarah vs Sahana](/best/lensmart-sarah-vs-sahana/) comparison.

Expect **3–5 business days** of processing for single-vision lenses, then **10–16 business days** with US Standard Shipping ($6.95), or pay for faster options. Orders over **$69 ship free**. The Sarah offers a lot of style for $14.95, a light build and excellent ratings on the store. It's a large frame, though, so measure carefully, and remember that a return for fit won't get all your money back.
