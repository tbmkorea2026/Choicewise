## Overview

**Enabot** makes small **rolling camera robots** for homes with pets. Instead of a fixed camera on a shelf, an EBO or ROLA robot drives around your home while you steer it from the app. You can watch in 2K or 3K, talk through it, record around the clock to an SD card and, on most models, play with your cat using a **built-in laser**. Enabot's terms name **WeHome Technology Company Limited**; Trustpilot lists the company in **Shenzhen, China**. Its robots are also sold by retailers like MediaMarkt.

This review covers prices, best sellers, ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on Enabot's store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't used these robots ourselves.

## What the robots do

Per Enabot's product pages, the best sellers share:

- A **2K camera** (3K on the Air 2 Plus, 1080p on the original EBO SE) with **night vision** and a 137° view.
- **Remote driving** from the app, from anywhere with an internet connection.
- **Two-way audio**, **24/7 recording** and **motion alerts**.
- **Local storage** on microSD up to 256 GB. A 32 GB card is included with several models.
- **Auto-recharge** on a dock (EBO models).
- **Pet play:** a built-in laser on the EBO SE 2, Air 2 and Air 2 Plus, plus feather toys.

**Limits:** they're small. The EBO SE 2 and Air 2 cross obstacles up to **6 mm** and slopes up to **4°**; the Air 2 Plus and ROLA Mini manage **10 mm** and **15°**. None climb stairs, and thick carpet can stop them.

## Safety and privacy

- **Laser play:** don't aim the laser at eyes (pets' or people's), and end laser sessions with a treat or toy so cats get something to "catch".
- **A camera that moves around your home** is still a camera. Use a strong app password, turn on any privacy mode when you're home, and keep firmware updated. Enabot says video is sent over encrypted connections.
- Keep the robot away from stairs and water; it isn't waterproof.

## Ratings: mixed on Trustpilot

**Trustpilot** shows **3.3/5 from 74 reviews**: **42% 5-star** and **34% 1-star**. Positive reviews praise the **camera, night vision and playful features**, and some praise support. Negative reviews describe **motors or wheels failing within weeks**, slow or unanswered **return requests**, navigation bumps and dropped connections. Enabot **replies to most negative reviews** (87%, per Trustpilot).

## Shipping

- **Free shipping** on robots and bundles to the **US, Canada, Europe, Asia, Australia and New Zealand**, with **taxes and duties included**. Accessories cost $19.90 to ship on their own.
- **Delivery:** US 5–15 days, Canada about 10, Europe 7–15, Asia-Pacific 7–10, rest of world 10–25.
- Other regions pay $49.90 shipping and local import fees.
- Carriers include DHL, FedEx, UPS and USPS.

## Returns and warranty

- **14-day return for any reason**, counted from delivery. For non-defective returns, **you pay return shipping**.
- **7-day DOA:** damaged, wrong or non-working items can be exchanged or refunded, with Enabot paying shipping.
- Items must be in **original condition without signs of use**, in the **original packaging**, with an **RMA number** first. That's strict for a gadget you need to set up to try.
- **Accessories are non-returnable.**
- **Warranty:** **1 year** (**2 years in Europe**) from delivery, **only in the country of purchase**. Normal wear, accidents and misuse aren't covered.
- Returns go to warehouses in DeSoto, Texas or Düsseldorf, Germany. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of inspection.

## Who should buy Enabot?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want to **check on a pet anywhere in your home** without installing several cameras.
- Have a **cat** that would enjoy laser play while you're out.
- Live in a flat or single-level home with hard floors or thin rugs.

**Think twice if you:**

- Have **stairs, thick carpet or lots of thresholds** to cross.
- Want a long warranty or worry about motor reliability.
- Mainly need fixed, always-on security coverage; a regular indoor camera is cheaper.

## The bottom line

Enabot's robots are a fun, genuinely useful way to see and play with your pet from anywhere, with free shipping, duties included and no required subscription. But the Trustpilot reviews point to motor reliability and return friction, returns cost you shipping, and the warranty is 1 year. Pick the model that fits your floors and budget, use code **TRANTRUNG** for 10% off and test it well within the 14 days. Overall: **7.1/10**.
