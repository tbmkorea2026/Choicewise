## In one minute

Newtral's selling point is **lumbar support that follows your back** instead of staying where you set it. Its chairs are priced in the middle of the market, the **warranty (3 years) and return terms are generous**, and on-site ratings are decent. But the brand's **Trustpilot reputation is poor**, much of it from a **Kickstarter campaign** that left backers waiting. If you buy, buy from the website, keep the box for 30 days, and pay with a card or PayPal.

This review is based on Newtral's product pages, policies, on-site reviews and its Trustpilot profile as of November 2026. We haven't sat in these chairs ourselves.

Reading the Trustpilot reviews, the one-star complaints fall into three groups:

- **Kickstarter backers** saying chairs arrived very late or not at all.
- **Support:** slow email replies, missing items in orders.
- **Expectations:** chairs that look more premium in photos than in person, or don't suit shorter users.

The positive reviews mostly praise comfort and the lumbar support. Store reviews are collected by Newtral itself and aren't independent.

## Things to check before you order

- **Laptop table:** the Magic H-BPro description talks about a "detachable laptop table", but the table is a **separate variant** ($419.99) that was **sold out** when we checked. The $379.99 chair doesn't include it.
- **Discounts:** some pages say 15% is auto-applied at checkout; our code **PARKCHOICE** is also 15%. Use whichever gives the lower total; they may not stack.
- **Fit:** each chair lists a height range (e.g. Standing-Mate Small 5'0"–5'8", Medium/Large 5'8"–6'4"). Check it.
- **Grey and laptop-table versions** of several chairs were out of stock.

## Shipping, returns and warranty

- **Shipping:** free to the contiguous US, the UK and most EU countries, from local warehouses; 1–2 business days to process plus 5–7 to deliver.
- **Returns:** 30 days from delivery. You can assemble and try the chair, but it must be **clean, undamaged, complete and in the original packaging**. **Newtral pays return shipping in the US, EU and UK**, even for change of mind. Clearance/Final Sale items are excluded.
- **Warranty:** 3 years on material and manufacturing defects; Newtral often sends replacement parts first.
- **Contact:** service@newtralchair.com.

## Safety note

Newtral states its chairs aren't medical devices. A supportive chair can help you sit more comfortably, but it won't treat back or neck pain. Take breaks, set up your desk and screen height, and see a professional for persistent pain.
