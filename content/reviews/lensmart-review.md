## Overview

**Lensmart** is an online eyewear store selling prescription **eyeglasses**, **sunglasses**, **kids' glasses**, **sports glasses** (cycling, ski and swimming goggles), **contact lenses** and accessories. It's run by **EYE SMART INC**, a company registered in Delaware, and says it ships to **80+ countries** and sells **1.5M+ pairs a year**.

Like other budget online opticians, Lensmart sells **frames and lenses separately**: you pick a frame, then add lenses and enter your prescription. This review covers prices, the product range, customer ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on Lensmart's store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't ordered glasses ourselves.

## Prices

| Best-selling frames | $9.95–$19.95 (frame only) |
| Flash-sale frames | From $6.95 |
| Prescription lenses | From $5 (per Lensmart) |

Lensmart also runs a **"50% off every 2nd frame"** offer and frequent sales. Upgrades like **blue-light blocking**, **photochromic**, **polarized**, **progressive** or **high-index** lenses cost extra; the final price appears after you choose lenses and enter your prescription.

### Lens options

Lensmart offers **single vision (distance or reading)**, **progressive**, **bifocal** and **non-prescription** lenses, in indexes up to **1.74** for strong prescriptions. Its FAQ says **all lenses have anti-scratch and anti-reflective coatings**, and you can add **prism correction**.

Lensmart's best-seller list is led by women's frames: **Sarah**, **Andrea**, **Sahana**, **Minda** and **Ligeia**, all rated **4.8–4.9/5** on the store. > **Read product descriptions carefully.** Some Lensmart descriptions don't match their own measurement tables. The Sarah frame, for example, lists a 45 mm lens height in its specs but 51 mm in the description, and several descriptions mention "built-in blue light protection" even though lenses are chosen separately. Trust the measurement table, and pick blue-light lenses explicitly if you want them.

## Shipping

Orders are processed in **3–10 business days**, depending on lenses (single vision 3–5 days, progressive or tinted 5–7 days), then shipped:

| US shipping | Cost | Delivery after dispatch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $6.95 | 10–16 business days |
| Advanced | $9.95 | 7–12 business days |
| DHL Express | $29.95 | 3–5 business days |

Orders over **$69 ship free**, except to DHL remote areas. Lensmart ships to the UK, Canada, Australia, much of Europe, Asia and Latin America, with standard delivery typically **14–18 business days** to the UK, Canada and EU countries.

## Returns and warranty: read carefully

- **365-day warranty:** a one-time replacement for **clear defects in material and workmanship**.
- **Non-quality returns within 30 days:** the frame and packaging must be in original condition.
  - **Days 1–15:** refund of **70% of the lens price** and **100% of the frame price**.
  - **Days 16–30:** refund of **50% of the lens price** and **80% of the frame price**.
  - If your order shipped free, the shipping cost is deducted.
- **You pay return shipping** for non-quality returns, and **sale items can't be returned or exchanged**.
- **Style or size:** Lensmart offers a **one-time replacement**, but you pay the express fee.
- **Contact lenses** are **final sale**, except confirmed manufacturing defects reported within 15 days.
- Approved refunds go back to your original payment method in **3–7 business days**.

## Who should buy from Lensmart?

**A great fit if you:**

- Want cheap prescription glasses or a spare pair.
- Have an up-to-date prescription with your PD, and know a frame size that fits you.
- Can wait two to four weeks for standard delivery.

**Think twice if you:**

- Have a complex prescription and want in-person fitting and adjustments.
- Want free, full-refund returns.
- Need glasses quickly without paying for DHL Express.

## The bottom line

Lensmart makes stylish prescription glasses very affordable, and buyers rate it highly. The trade-offs are slow standard shipping and a return policy that refunds only part of your money for non-quality returns. Check your measurements carefully, use the virtual try-on, and treat it as a low-cost purchase rather than one you plan to return.
