## Overview

The **Waterdrop X12** is part of the **X Series**, which Waterdrop says won a **Red Dot design award in 2024**. It's the first product tagged **"Best Seller"** on Waterdrop's homepage RO tab and is aimed at families who want a lot of filtered water from a dedicated faucet without a storage tank.

This review is based on Waterdrop's product page, specs and policies as of October 2026. We haven't installed or tested the X12 ourselves. For the brand overview, see our [Waterdrop review](/reviews/waterdrop-review/).

## Before you buy: water and safety

- **Test your water first** (lab test or your utility's report). Well water may need extra treatment for bacteria, iron or hardness.
- **NSF/ANSI 58** certification covers the claims on the **performance data sheet**; read it for the contaminants you care about.
- The X12 adds minerals and Waterdrop says it adjusts pH to about **7.5**. Claims about "health" and "balanced body pH" are **marketing, not medical evidence**.
- You need a **power outlet** under the sink. If you're not comfortable connecting to the cold-water line and drain, use a plumber.

## Price and running costs

- **List price: $1,299**, in silver, black or gold faucet finishes. During the **Oct 26–Nov 1, 2026** sale the store showed **$1,039** with code **WDX12** at checkout.
- **Filters:** F1A **$32.99** (12 months / 1,100 gal), F2 **$30.99** (6 months / 550 gal), X12-F3 RO **$159.99** (24 months / 2,200 gal). Waterdrop estimates about **$173 a year**; a subscription saves 5%.

## Filtration and flow

- **11 stages** including a **0.0001 μm RO membrane**, an **alkaline remineralization** stage and an **LED** stage that Waterdrop says reduces 99.9% of impurities in the water path.
- Rated at **1,200 gallons per day**; Waterdrop says a **6 oz cup fills in 3 seconds**.
- **3:1 pure-to-drain ratio:** for every 3 cups of filtered water, about 1 cup goes to the drain. That's better than the 2:1 of the G3P600, K6 and G5P700A.

## Design and faucet

- **Tankless**, about **18.2 × 6.25 × 16.72 inches**; Waterdrop says it saves 70% of under-sink space versus tank systems.
- **Digital faucet** with touch controls, adjustable water volumes, real-time **TDS** display and filter-life alerts.
- The box includes the system, 3 filters, faucet, power adapter, feed-water adapter, drain saddle, tubing, Teflon tape and lock clips.

## Shipping, returns and warranty

Free standard shipping (orders over $200) from US warehouses, 3–6 business days. **30-day returns** with no restocking fee, but **you pay return shipping** unless it's defective; after 30 days there's a 10% restocking fee. The X12 gets a **2-year warranty**.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** families who use a lot of drinking and cooking water, want remineralized RO water, and value the longer warranty.
- **Think twice:** if your budget is tight (the G3P600 is less than half the price), if there's no outlet under your sink, or if you can't modify plumbing.

## The bottom line

The X12 is Waterdrop's most complete mainstream RO system: fast, efficient, certified to NSF/ANSI 58 and covered for 2 years. It's priced like a premium appliance and its filters aren't cheap, so it makes the most sense for bigger households. Our verdict: **8.1/10**.
