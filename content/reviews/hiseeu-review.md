## Privacy and security basics

Any internet-connected camera can be a privacy risk. **Change default passwords**, keep **firmware** and the app updated, avoid pointing cameras at neighbors' property, and check local laws on audio recording. Local recording to an NVR or SD card keeps footage off the cloud.
