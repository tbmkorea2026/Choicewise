## Overview

The **Indoor Play Gym** is the first product in Wood and Hearts' "Most Loved by Parents" block on its homepage. It's a **wall and ceiling system** rather than a freestanding frame: a big climbing wall, rungs you mount on the wall or ceiling, and hanging ropes, which together turn a room into a climbing gym while leaving the floor open.

This review is based on Wood and Hearts' product page, policies and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't installed or tested the set ourselves.

## Safety first

This set has children climbing up to wall height and hanging from the ceiling, so installation matters more than anything else:

- Mount it only on **load-bearing walls and ceilings** (brick, concrete, solid wood studs or metal profiles), never drywall alone. Use fixings suited to your wall; if in doubt, hire a professional.
- Put **thick mats** under the whole climbing area. They're not included.
- **Supervise** at all times, keep to the **60 kg** use limit, and check and tighten bolts every two months.

More in the safety section of our [Wood and Hearts review](/reviews/woodandhearts-review/).

## What we like

- **Lots of play in one set:** climbing, traversing the rungs, hanging, swinging and rope climbing.
- **Floor stays free:** unlike freestanding playgrounds, it doesn't take up the middle of the room.
- **Modular:** the store says you can rearrange the rungs and add accessories as your child grows.
- **Warranty:** 2 years on wooden parts and 10 years on wooden rungs. Ropes count as textiles, though, which only get 30 days.

## What to check first

- **Your walls and ceiling:** this is the biggest question. Many rentals and plasterboard walls won't suit it.
- **Room size:** the climbing wall alone needs about 2.2 × 2.4 m.
- **Extra costs:** a proper landing surface is essential, and Wood and Hearts' gymnastics mat starts at $206.99.
- **Weight:** 60 kg is fine for most children but rules out many adults.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** families with a spare room, solid walls and ceilings, and kids who climb everything.
- **Think twice:** renters, homes with plasterboard only, or if you'd rather have a freestanding frame you can move.
