## Before you buy: battery basics and safety

- **LiFePO4 vs lead-acid:** WattCycle's 12V batteries are drop-in replacements in size, but check that your **charger, solar controller and alternator** support LiFePO4 charging (about **14.4–14.6V**).
- **Cold weather:** the built-in BMS **blocks charging below about 32°F (0°C)** on the 12V models we checked; plan for that in winter.
- **Large systems:** a 48V server-rack battery stores over 5 kWh. Use proper fusing and cabling, follow the manual, and use a qualified installer if you aren't sure.
