## Overview

The **LaserTree K30** is a 30W blue diode laser module for **upgrading a hobby laser engraver**. It's the first in-stock module in LaserTree's best-selling sort; the 10W and 20W modules ahead of it were sold out.

This review is based on LaserTree's product page, policies and store ratings as of November 2026. We haven't cut anything with the K30 ourselves. Power and speed figures are LaserTree's.

## Safety first

A 30W laser is a **Class 4** hazard: direct and reflected light can injure eyes instantly and start fires. Wear goggles rated for **450 nm**, use an enclosure with fume extraction, never cut PVC or vinyl, and never leave a job unattended. If you don't already own suitable goggles, the goggles bundle ($439) is worth considering. See the safety section in our [LaserTree review](/reviews/lasertree-review/).

## Key features

- **Power:** six 5.5W diodes combined by beam compression into **30W** optical output.
- **Air assist:** built-in nozzle for cleaner cuts (pump sold separately or in a bundle).
- **Temperature warning:** a display shows the module's temperature, and a buzzer sounds above **55 °C**.
- **Positioning:** a red crosshair marks the work position, helpful for second passes.
- **Focus:** a focus bar for quick setup at the 40 mm focal length.
- **Fit:** adapter board plus a multi-hole sliding plate, with power and signal LEDs to check the wiring.

## Cutting claims

LaserTree lists these one-pass results:

| Material | Speed |
| --- | --- |
| 15 mm basswood plywood | 180 mm/min |
| 15 mm black acrylic | 35 mm/min |
| 20 mm pine | 120 mm/min |

It also claims about **3× the engraving speed** of a 10W module. Treat these as best-case figures. Blue diode lasers don't cut clear or blue acrylic well, and results vary with wood type and setup.

## What to check first

- **Your engraver:** it must handle the K30's 710 g weight and supply 24V 5A, or use LaserTree's adapter.
- **Spot size:** unlike the [K40](/best/lasertree-k30-vs-k40/), the K30's spec table doesn't list one, which matters for fine engraving.
- **Warranty:** the page promises **one year**, but the replacement policy covers **three months**, with a 15% fee from day 15 and 20% from day 30, plus return shipping.

## Ratings

The page shows **4.8/5 from 13 reviews**. That's more than LaserTree's other K-series modules have, but still a small sample. LaserTree's Trustpilot score is **4.2 from 15 reviews**, with some reports of modules failing after short use. Test yours as soon as it arrives.

## Price

- **K30:** $389 (US or EU plug).
- **With air pump** or **with safety goggles:** $439 each.

Code **PARKCHOICE** takes **5% off** at the time of writing.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** hobbyists with a sturdy engraver and a safe setup who want much faster, thicker cuts than a 5–10W head.
- **Think twice:** if you need fine-detail engraving above all, a clear long warranty, or you're new to lasers.

## The bottom line

The K30 offers a lot of cutting power for $389 and sensible features like air assist and a heat alarm. The warranty conflict and limited reviews are the main doubts. Our verdict: **7.2/10**.
