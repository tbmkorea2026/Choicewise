## Overview

**Brightin Star** (styled BrightinStar) is a lens brand selling mostly **manual-focus primes** for mirrorless cameras, plus a few autofocus lenses, filters and accessories. Its contact page lists **Hong Kong Brightin Star Optical Technology Co., Ltd.**, with an office in Central, Hong Kong; Trustpilot lists the company in **China**. Lenses come in **Sony E, Fuji X, Nikon Z, Canon RF, Canon EOS-M, L-mount and Micro Four Thirds** versions, and there's a **Leica M** pancake lens.

This review covers prices, best sellers, shipping, returns, warranty and ratings, based on Brightin Star's store and policies as of October 2026, with prices and policies rechecked in November 2026. We haven't used these lenses ourselves.

## Ratings: very few independent reviews

- **On Brightin Star's store:** most best sellers show **4.7–4.9/5**, but usually from **only 1–8 reviews**. The 28mm pancake is the exception with **93**.
- **On Trustpilot:** just **2 reviews**, both **1-star** (TrustScore 2.9). One describes a lens arriving with dust, smears and possible scratches on the glass; the other a 30% cancellation fee and a return that cost more than $50 to ship.

Two reviews aren't enough to judge a brand, but there's also little independent evidence the other way.

## Shipping

- **Processing:** the FAQ says orders usually ship within **48 hours**; the shipping page says **2–5 business days**.
- **Standard:** about **12–15 days**, **free over $55**.
- **Express (DHL/FedEx):** about **10 days**, extra fee shown at checkout.
- Brightin Star says it covers **import duties for most countries** (including the US, UK and Canada), but not on Express/DHL shipments.
- The address can't be changed once the order has shipped.

## Returns and warranty: read carefully

**The "90-day return" is not a no-questions-asked return.**

- **Full refund or exchange, shipping paid by Brightin Star:** defective item, wrong model shipped, item mismatch or incomplete order, within **90 days** of delivery.
- **You ordered the wrong model or mount:** return it unused within 90 days, but you pay return and replacement shipping. Since a **November 2026** update there's **no restocking fee** (it used to be 40%).
- **Not accepted:** change of mind, misuse or accidents, returns after 90 days and items whose serial number doesn't match. Ask before returning part of a bundle.
- **Discount codes:** the updated policy says orders using discount codes **remain eligible** for returns and warranty (previously they weren't).
- Items must be unused, with all packaging and accessories. Get approval and an **RMA number** first.

**Warranty:** a **2-year limited warranty** on manufacturing defects. Within 30 days, Brightin Star pays shipping both ways; from 30 days to 2 years you pay shipping to its warehouse and it pays the return.

> **Contact details:** the store's contact page lists support and business emails on Gmail addresses rather than a company domain, plus a phone number with a +86 (mainland China) code.

## Who should buy from Brightin Star?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want a **cheap, very fast manual prime** (F0.95 or F1.4) and enjoy manual focusing.
- Shoot **Leica M** and want a tiny 28mm pancake.
- Want a **$69.99 fisheye** for fun.

**Think twice if you:**

- Aren't sure which mount you need (a mistake costs shipping both ways).
- Might want to return the lens just because you don't like it.
- Need your lens within two weeks without paying for Express.

## The bottom line

Brightin Star's lenses are cheap for what they promise, and the 2-year warranty helps. Its updated policy is fairer than before (no restocking fee, and discount-code orders keep return rights), but it still excludes change of mind, delivery takes about two weeks, and there's very little independent feedback. Double-check your mount, use code **KOREA** for 5% off, and keep your expectations realistic.
