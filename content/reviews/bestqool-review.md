## Overview

**BestQool** sells **red light therapy (RLT)** devices that combine **red (630–660 nm)** and **near-infrared (850–940 nm)** LEDs: small and large panels, modular full-body setups, flexible wraps and a mat. It says it has **300,000+ members** and sells worldwide from **bestqool.com**.

This review is based on BestQool's product pages, policies, store reviews and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested BestQool devices ourselves.

## Before you buy: health claims and safety

- **What the claims are:** product pages say the devices relieve pain, smooth skin, reduce wrinkles and support sleep, and cite a survey of 100+ users. These are **BestQool's claims**. Its own disclaimer says the products are **not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease**.
- **Regulatory status:** BestQool says its devices are **Class II, 510(k) exempt** under **21 CFR 890.5500** (infrared lamps). That means no FDA premarket review was required; it isn't FDA approval of any health benefit. The site also says devices are made in "factories approved by FDA", which isn't how FDA oversight works, so treat that as marketing.
- **Eyes:** wear the **protective eyewear** included with the panels and don't stare into the LEDs.
- **Talk to a doctor first** if you're pregnant, have a medical condition, or take photosensitizing medication, and don't use RLT in place of treatment for pain or skin problems.
- **Follow the time limits:** BestQool suggests **15–30 minutes a day, 4–7 times a week** for the BQ60.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** **4.8–4.9/5** across the top 5, from **85 to 493 reviews** per product.
- **Trustpilot:** **4.5/5 from 277 reviews**, claimed profile: **81% 5-star, 5% 1-star**. Praise covers support, build quality and reported pain relief. Complaints mention damaged packaging, slow return replies, failed parts or cords and unclear manuals.

## Shipping

- **Free shipping in the mainland US.**
- **International:** most countries; duties, taxes and brokerage fees are on you. BestQool says delivery usually takes **3–7 business days** but doesn't guarantee it.
- If a package shows as delivered but didn't arrive, report it within **7 days**; BestQool offers a one-time replacement, not a refund.

## Returns and warranty

- **30 days** from delivery, **no restocking fee**; contact help@bestqool.com first.
- **Change of mind:** you pay **all shipping**; the prepaid label cost is deducted from your refund. Original shipping, duties and taxes aren't refunded.
- **Condition:** like-new, with all packaging, manuals and accessories, or the refund may be reduced.
- **Refurbished / open-box:** no returns, 1-year repair warranty only.
- **Warranty:** **BQ and Pro series 2 years**, **Redot 1 year**, each with a **free 1-year extension** if you register within **14 days**. After the first month, you pay shipping on warranty claims.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** people who want a home RLT panel from a brand that publishes measured output and backs panels for up to 3 years.
- **Think twice:** if you expect a medical result, want a risk-free trial (returns cost you shipping), or plan to buy refurbished.

## The bottom line

BestQool is one of the more transparent RLT brands on specs, with strong ratings and a good panel warranty if you register it.
