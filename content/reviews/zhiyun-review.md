ZHIYUN runs frequent promotions, with many products discounted against their listed regular price, and offers most gimbals in **Standard**, **Combo** or **Pro** kits. The store also has a **clearance** section and **certified pre-owned** gear.

Orders are **processed within 2–3 business days** and typically arrive **7–15 business days after dispatch**. ZHIYUN ships to the US, Canada, Australia, the UK and a list of European countries, and can't deliver to P.O. boxes.

## Returns and warranty: read carefully

- The policy opens with a **30-day return and refund window** for new or like-new items in original packaging, but a later section still refers to **14 days for refunds**. **Replacements** are available within **30 days** for transit damage, items not as described or manufacturing defects.
- **No return labels** are provided, and **the buyer pays return shipping** for non-quality returns.
- If a returned product doesn't meet the conditions after testing, the sender bears return shipping and testing costs.
- **Warranty:** a **free 1-year limited warranty** against manufacturing defects for official store purchases, with **paid repair service** available afterwards.

> **Our advice:** if a return might matter to you, email ZHIYUN's after-sales team before ordering to confirm the current return window.

## Who should buy from ZHIYUN?

**A great fit if you:**

- Want a capable camera gimbal and video lights from one brand.
- Shoot with mirrorless or DSLR cameras and want options from compact to full-frame.
- Can wait one to three weeks for delivery.

**Think twice if you:**

- Need fast delivery or easy, free returns.
- Live outside ZHIYUN's shipping countries.

## The bottom line

ZHIYUN's product range and pricing are its biggest strengths, and its popular gimbals are well rated by buyers. Delivery times and return terms are less generous than the products themselves, so check the policies and your camera's compatibility before you buy.
