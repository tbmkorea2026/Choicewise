If you have one bench and want to **sharpen and shape** without buying two machines, a grinder-sander combo makes sense. The BG2600 puts a **2x42 belt** on one side and a **6-inch wheel** on the other.

## What it does well

- **Two jobs, one footprint.** Grind a bevel on the wheel, then refine on the belt.
- **Tilting belt.** Vertical for edges, horizontal for flattening small parts.
- **Ratings.** 4.71/5 from 94 store reviews is the most reviews of any BUCKTOOL machine.

## What to know before buying

- **Power is modest.** 1/3 HP on a single motor shared by belt and wheel is fine for sharpening but not for heavy stock removal.
- **Speed.** One fixed speed (3450 RPM). Tool steel overheats quickly; keep a water pot nearby. A [low-speed grinder](/best/low-speed-grinder-vs-1x42-belt-sander-vs-grinder-combo/) is gentler on edges.
- **Grits.** Only 120-grit belt and 60-grit wheel come with it. Budget for a coarser and a finer belt.
- **Listing errors.** The FAQ on the page quotes 200 W, a 1x30 belt and 6 kg; ignore it.

## Safety

Wear eye protection, keep the tool rest close to the wheel, never grind aluminium on the stone, and don't wear gloves near the belt.
