## Overview

**MIER** is a running and training apparel brand that sells directly through miersports.com. It describes itself as a running brand built around **community, innovation and sustainability**, and its range covers running shorts, tops, pants, hoodies, hydration belts and vests, and gym bags. The company lists an office in **Xiamen, China** and a US office in **Denver, Colorado**.

This review covers MIER's product range, prices, fabric technologies, shipping and returns, based on the brand's website and policies as of October 2026.

## What does MIER sell?

- **Running shorts:** MIER's core product and best sellers, in 3", 4", 5" and 7" lengths, lined, linerless and 2-in-1 styles, for men and women.
- **Tops:** running tees, tanks, polos and UPF 50+ sun-protection hoodies.
- **Bottoms:** running pants and quick-dry cargo pants.
- **Accessories:** running belts and hydration vests.
- **Bags:** convertible backpack-duffels and gym bags with shoe compartments.

The store also groups products by goal, such as **5K**, **5–20K** and **marathon** collections, which is a helpful way to shop for beginners.

### Best sellers

MIER's best-seller list is led by its running shorts, especially the **Men's SprintDry™ 2 in 1 Running Shorts 5"**. ## Fabric technologies

MIER names four in-house fabric technologies:

| Technology | What MIER says it does |
| --- | --- |
| **SprintDry™** | High-performance fabric with moisture-wicking and quick-dry features |
| **X-Sofort™** | Patent-pending fabric described as exceptionally soft, breathable and stretchy |
| **AirDure™** | Lightweight fabric designed for sportswear and T-shirts |
| **RePETek™** | Recycled polyester made from plastic bottles |

MIER says its fabrics undergo laboratory testing and that products are developed with athletes. Some women's shorts use **95% recycled polyester**.

## Prices and value

MIER's prices are its biggest selling point. At the time of writing, its best-selling running shorts cost about **$28–$38**, with frequent discounts. Practical features such as phone pockets in liners, zip pockets, reflective details and, on some styles, UPF 50+ sun protection are common across the range.

## Shipping

- **Free shipping on orders over $60.**
- Orders usually ship within **one to two days**.
- **US:** 3–7 business days standard from US warehouses; 1–3 business days expedited (UPS, FedEx, DHL).
- **Europe:** 7–14 business days. **Asia:** 3–15 business days.
- MIER ships to a limited list of countries, and **does not ship to PO boxes or APO/FPO addresses**.

## Returns

MIER's return policy is one of its strengths:

- **Free returns within 30 days** of delivery, extended to **90 days** for Black Friday sale orders.
- Items must be **brand new with original inner packaging and tags**.
- Once you receive a return label, ship it back within **7 days**; refunds are processed **5–7 business days** after the item reaches the warehouse.
- **Custom products** are final sale.
- The US office address is **not** a return address; email intl@miersports.com for the correct one.

## Sustainability and responsibility

MIER says its life cycle assessments show that most of its environmental impact comes from fabric processing and manufacturing, so it focuses there by using recycled materials and working with suppliers to reduce chemicals, water, energy and waste. It also states that it annually audits its production facilities against social and environmental guidelines. These are the brand's own claims; MIER does not list third-party certifications on these pages.

## Who should buy MIER?

**A great fit if you:**

- Want well-featured running shorts and tops on a budget.
- Like having many colors and sizes to choose from.
- Live in the US and want fast standard shipping and free returns.

**Consider other brands if you:**

- Want a long-established brand with detailed company history and certifications.
- Live outside MIER's shipping countries or need APO/FPO delivery.

## The bottom line

MIER delivers a lot of running gear for the money, with smart, practical features, a wide size range and free 30-day returns that make trying it low-risk. For everyday runners and gym-goers who care more about function than logos, MIER is an easy brand to recommend.
