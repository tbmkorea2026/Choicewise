## Overview

The **1963 Icon II** is seagull1963.com's modern take on its classic pilot's chronograph, sold in two versions: **Green bronze** and **Blue steel**. The Green version is the **highest-ranked in-stock watch** on the store's best-selling list; the two limited editions above it were sold out.

This review is based on the product page, specs, policies and Trustpilot reviews as of November 2026. We haven't handled the watch. For the brand background, see our [Seagull 1963 review](/reviews/seagull1963-review/).

## Case and dial

- **40mm** case in **CuSn8 bronze** with a **stainless steel back**; the store says the bronze develops a unique patina.
- **12.7mm** thick, **46mm lug-to-lug**, 20mm lugs: shorter lugs than the store's 40mm Air Force (50.5mm).
- **Sunburst green dial**, **sapphire crystal**, pull-out crown.
- **Numbered**: each piece is individually numbered and engraved.

## Movement

- **Seagull ST1901** hand-wound **column-wheel chronograph**, 21 jewels, 21,600 beats per hour, visible through the caseback.
- Power reserve: the spec table says **42 hours**, the page text says 48 hours. Plan on winding it daily.

## Listing problems

The Icon II Green page's main description actually describes the **Moonphase cream dial** model, and the two power reserve figures don't match. Neither changes the watch, but read the spec table rather than the marketing text, and ask support if anything matters to you.

## Price, shipping, returns and warranty

- **€537**, free shipping from Hong Kong with **DDP** (duties prepaid) for most countries.
- **14-day returns** for unused watches; you pay return shipping and **€30 is deducted**.
- **Warranty: 2 years.**

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone who likes bronze, wants a compact vintage-style chronograph and enjoys a hand-wound movement.
- **Think twice:** if you want an automatic, need more water resistance, or specifically want a factory Tianjin Seagull watch.

## The bottom line

The Icon II Green brings bronze, sapphire and a column-wheel chronograph together at €537, with a more wearable 46mm lug-to-lug. Sloppy listing details and strict returns hold it back. Our verdict: **7.5/10**.
