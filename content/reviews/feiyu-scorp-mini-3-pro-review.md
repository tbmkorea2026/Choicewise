The **Feiyu SCORP Mini 3 Pro** is FeiyuTech's **best-selling gimbal** at the time of writing. It sells for about **$220** (or about **$254** as a kit) and is rated **4.74/5 from 27 reviews** on the FeiyuTech store.

## Key features

### AI Tracking 4.0

The built-in tracking is the headline feature. FeiyuTech lists a **15% wider tracking view**, a **0.003-second response time**, a **tracking distance of up to 18 m** and **gesture control**. It supports both **face tracking and object tracking**, though it can only follow **one subject at a time**. For solo creators, that means you can keep yourself in frame while walking and talking.

### OLED touchscreen and remote control

A **1.3-inch OLED touchscreen** on the handle lets you change settings without opening an app, and FeiyuTech says you can control the gimbal **from up to 12 m away**.

### Native vertical shooting

The **3rd-generation native vertical switch** lets you rotate into portrait orientation for TikTok, Reels and Shorts without extra brackets.

### Battery life and modes

FeiyuTech rates the battery at **up to 14 hours**, enough for a long shoot day. Built-in modes include **Time-lapse, Panorama, Inception (rotating shots), Portrait and Smart Tracking**.

## Payload and compatibility: check before you buy

This is the most important section for a compact gimbal:

- **Payload: 2 kg (4.4 lb).** That's an increase over the previous Mini generation, but FeiyuTech is clear that it **can't support large cameras or lenses**.
- **Lens size:** for good balance, FeiyuTech recommends lenses **up to about 90 mm long and 75 mm in diameter**.
- **Cameras and phones:** it's a **4-in-1** gimbal that works with lightweight mirrorless cameras and smartphones.
- **Camera control:** many cameras pair over **Bluetooth**; some need an **optional Feiyu control cable**. FeiyuTech publishes separate Bluetooth, cable and lens compatibility lists.

> **Tip:** Weigh your camera with the lens, battery and card before ordering. If you're close to 2 kg or use a heavy zoom, the 3.5 kg [SCORP 3](/best/feiyu-scorp-mini-3-pro-vs-scorp-3/) is the safer choice.

## Who should buy it?

**A great fit if you:**

- Shoot with a **compact mirrorless camera** and a small or mid-size lens.
- Vlog alone and want **AI tracking** to keep you in frame.
- Want one gimbal that also works with your **phone**.

**Look elsewhere if you:**

- Use a full-frame body with a heavy zoom lens, or need more than 2 kg of payload.
- Need to track multiple subjects at once.

## The bottom line

The Feiyu SCORP Mini 3 Pro is a feature-rich compact gimbal at a fair price, combining AI tracking, a touchscreen, native vertical shooting and long battery life in a travel-friendly package. As long as your camera and lens fit within its 2 kg payload, it's one of the easiest ways to get smooth, tracked footage as a solo creator.
