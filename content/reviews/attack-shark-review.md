## Overview

**Attack Shark** makes budget gaming peripherals: ultra-light **wireless mice** with flagship sensors, **Hall Effect (magnetic) keyboards** with rapid trigger, mechanical keyboards and headsets. The company behind it is **Guangzhou Shijunxingcheng Electronics Technology Co., Ltd**, which says it was founded in **2022**; Trustpilot lists a Shenzhen address.

This review is based on Attack Shark's product pages, policies, store ratings and Trustpilot as of October 2026. We haven't tested Attack Shark gear ourselves.

## Prices

Attack Shark undercuts big brands on paper specs. Its best sellers cost **about $51 to $115**: the **F1 AIR** and **V2 AIR** mice are **$67.99** with a PAW3955MAX sensor and 8K polling, and the **X68 HE** rapid-trigger keyboard starts around **$51**.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** **4.5–4.9/5** for the best sellers; the X68 HE has nearly **1,000 reviews**.
- **Trustpilot:** **4.0/5 from 82 reviews** (claimed): **61% 5-star, 26% 1-star**. Praise covers value, performance and quick replacements; complaints mention shipping delays, products failing early, repeated requests for proof videos and slow refunds.

## The 2025 driver malware incident

In late 2025 Attack Shark announced that its **G3PRO software** download had been **infected with malware** on its server. It says the problem was limited to the server copy, removed it, moved all files to a new server, **digitally signed** its software and published **VirusTotal** scan results. Many products also offer a **web-based configurator**, which avoids installing software at all.

## Shipping

- **Free standard shipping** on orders over **$15** to the US, UK, most of the EU, Japan, South Korea and Southeast Asia; **express** via DHL/FedEx costs **$10–$40**.
- **Warehouses:** US (3–4 working days), UK (2–4), Germany (3–5); from China, about 7–12 days to the US.
- **Duties** may apply depending on your country and shipping method.
- **Lost parcels:** after 3 months in transit, orders under $50 get a reship or refund, but orders **over $50** only get a **50% refund**.

## Returns and warranty

- **15-day no-reason returns** for undamaged, complete items; **you pay return shipping and duties**. The same page also mentions a "7-day" no-reason policy for some items, so confirm first.
- **30-day replacement or refund** for problems, after sending a video.
- **1-year warranty** on keyboards, mice and switches; after 7 days, you pay shipping to send a faulty item back.
- Attack Shark strongly recommends recording an **unboxing video**.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** competitive gamers who want high-end sensors, 8K polling and rapid trigger for much less than big brands.
- **Think twice:** if you want generous returns, or prefer brands with long software-security track records.

## The bottom line

Attack Shark offers exceptional specs for the money, strong ratings and local warehouses. The return terms and the 2025 malware incident are reasons to be careful with downloads and keep proof of your unboxing. Our verdict: **7.4/10**.
