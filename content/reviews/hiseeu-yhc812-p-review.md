## Overview

The **Hiseeu YHC812-P** is the #1 best seller on hiseeu.com. It's a wired **PoE dome camera** with a **12MP** sensor and a **wide 121° lens**, built to add to a PoE NVR system or run on its own with a microSD card.

This review is based on Hiseeu's product page and policies as of October 2026. We haven't installed the YHC812-P ourselves.

## Key features

Per Hiseeu:

- **12MP** (3840×2880) at **20 fps**, **H.265/H.265+** compression.
- **2.8mm lens** with a **121°** field of view (wider than typical 3.6mm, 78° lenses).
- **Color night vision** with built-in lights.
- **AI human and vehicle detection** with custom alarm zones, plus a **sound-and-light alarm** and **mobile notifications**.
- **Two-way audio**.
- **IP67** weatherproofing.
- **Power:** **PoE (IEEE 802.3af)** or **12V DC**.
- **Storage:** a **PoE NVR** with a hard drive, or a **microSD card up to 512GB** (not included).

## Setup and compatibility

The YHC812-P works with **Hiseeu PoE NVRs** (including its 4K and 12MP NVRs) and NVRs that support **ONVIF**. Hiseeu notes you **may need to change the camera's IP address** the first time you add it. With one Ethernet cable for power and data, PoE setups are tidy, but you'll need a PoE NVR or switch.

## Privacy

Change the default password, keep firmware updated and avoid recording audio or areas where it isn't legal where you live.
