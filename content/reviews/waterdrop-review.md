## Before you buy: water testing and safety

- **Know what's in your water.** Waterdrop's site links to EWG tap-water data by ZIP code; a lab test (or your utility's report) is more reliable. Well water may need extra treatment for bacteria, iron or hardness.
- **Certifications differ by model.** **NSF/ANSI 58** covers RO performance claims, **42** covers taste and chlorine, **53** covers health-related contaminants and **372** only covers **low lead content** in the materials. Read the **performance data sheet** for each model.
- **Alkaline and mineral claims:** Waterdrop says some models add minerals and raise pH to about 7.5 for taste and "healthier" hydration. Those are marketing claims, not medical ones.
- **Installation:** every system here needs a **power outlet** under the sink and connections to your cold-water line and drain. Waterdrop calls it a 15–30 minute DIY job; hire a plumber if you're unsure, since leaks can damage cabinets.

## Shipping

- **US only**, from US warehouses; no international shipping and no delivery to US territories.
- **Standard (3–6 business days):** free over **$200**, otherwise **$5.99**. **Economy (4–8 days)** is free. **Express (2–3 days)** costs **$10.99–$42.99** by weight.
- Orders are prepared in **24–48 hours**, and most ship within 24 hours.

## Returns, cancellations and warranty

- **Cancellations:** free within **24 hours** of ordering. After that, if the order has shipped, you pay shipping both ways.
- **Returns within 30 days of receipt:** **no restocking fee**, but **you pay return shipping** unless the item is defective. Continental US orders only. Items must be disassembled and repacked in the original packaging.
- **Returns after 30 days:** still possible, minus a **10% restocking fee** and shipping costs.
- **Warranty:** **1-year limited** on most RO systems, **2-year** on eligible X Series systems, with free return, repair or replacement for defects. Lifetime technical support is offered on eligible models.
- **Amazon and other third-party orders** don't qualify for these store policies.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** US households that want RO-filtered drinking water from a dedicated faucet, have a power outlet under the sink and are comfortable with basic plumbing.
- **Think twice:** renters who can't drill or modify plumbing, buyers outside the continental US, or anyone who expects a long warranty on a $1,000 appliance.
