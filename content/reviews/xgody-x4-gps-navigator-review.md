## Overview

The **XGODY X4** is the #1 best seller in XGODY's store: a **9-inch** standalone GPS navigator for cars, trucks, RVs and more. It comes in two versions: the **X4F** ($85.90) and the **X4BT** ($89.90), which adds Bluetooth calling and AV-in. XGODY says its GPS units ranked top on eBay since 2009 (its own claim).

This review is based on XGODY's product page, policies and customer ratings as of October 2026. We haven't used the X4 ourselves.

## Key features

Per XGODY's product page:

- **9-inch capacitive touchscreen**, 800×480 resolution.
- **256 MB RAM, 8 GB storage**, microSD up to 32 GB.
- **Car and truck modes**: set your truck's height, weight, length and width to avoid low bridges and restricted roads.
- **Route options:** fast, short, economical or easy; avoid unpaved roads, ferries, tolls or highways.
- **Lane assist**, 2D/3D maps, POI search (gas, restaurants, hotels) and postcode search.
- **Voice guidance** in many languages and **speed-camera alerts**.
- **FM transmitter** to play sound through your car stereo, plus MP3/MP4 playback.
- **X4BT:** Bluetooth hands-free calling and AV-in for a backup camera.
- Comes with a **car charger** and suction mount.

XGODY doesn't list the X4's battery life, operating system or weight on the page.

## Map updates

XGODY promises **free lifetime map updates**, released about once a year for the X4. You email support for a **download link**, then copy the files to the device yourself. It works, but it's more effort than automatic updates, and you'll need a computer.

## Ratings

The X4 shows **4.8/5**, but from **only 9 reviews** on XGODY's store, which is too few to judge reliability. Its 7-inch siblings have far more feedback: the **886BT** shows 4.6/5 from 1,108 reviews and the **718** 4.8/5 from 1,045. XGODY's Trustpilot profile is **1.4/5 from 125 reviews**, mostly about warranty and failures on other products; see our [XGODY review](/reviews/xgody-review/).

## Things to know

- **Speed-camera alerts** are banned while driving in some countries, such as France and Germany. Turn them off if needed.
- Mount it where it **doesn't block your view**, and set the route before you drive.
- **Warranty:** 14-day refund or replacement for defects, 30-day replacement, then **free repair up to 180 days**. Contact support first and keep the box.
- **Shipping:** free on most items, 4–10 working days to the US, UK and Australia.

## The bottom line

The XGODY X4 gives you a **9-inch** sat nav with truck routing and Bluetooth (on the BT) for under $90, which is cheap. But with only 9 reviews, manual map updates and XGODY's short warranty and poor Trustpilot record, it's a budget gamble. If you want the screen size, the X4BT is the version to get; if you want the safest XGODY bet, the better-reviewed 886BT is worth a look. Our verdict: **6.4/10**.
