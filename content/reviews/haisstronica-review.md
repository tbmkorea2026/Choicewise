## Overview

**Haisstronica** is a brand focused on **wire connection products**: electrical connectors, terminals, heat shrink products, cable lugs, crimping tools and wiring kits for repair, installation and maintenance work. The brand says it has been selling these products **since 2016**. Its website and online store are operated by **AIGREEN TRADING CO., LIMITED**, a company registered in **Hong Kong**.

This review covers what Haisstronica sells, prices, shipping and returns, based on its website and published policies as of October 2026.

## Prices and value

Haisstronica's best-selling products are priced for DIYers: at the time of writing, the ratchet crimper costs about **$31**, the self-adjusting stripper from **$24**, and solder seal and heat shrink connector kits from about **$17–$21**, with larger kits costing more. The store regularly runs promotions of up to 20% off eligible orders.

Orders are processed within **1–2 business days**, and Haisstronica says it uses **Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment** for fast delivery. It also delivers to Canada, several European countries and Japan; rates are shown at checkout.

## Returns

- Request a return within **30 days** of delivery by emailing support with your order number and reason.
- Items must be **unused, uninstalled** and in substantially the same condition, with original packaging and accessories.
- **Sale items are eligible** under the same rules.
- **Defective, damaged or incorrect items:** Haisstronica provides or reimburses return shipping, with no restocking fee.
- **Preference returns:** you pay return shipping, and a **restocking fee of up to 10%** may apply. Haisstronica says any fee is disclosed before you ship.
- Refunds are issued within about 5 business days after inspection.

## Support and business buyers

Support is available by **email and chat**, and Haisstronica publishes a phone number. Business customers can use its **bulk purchasing** and **wholesale/distributor** programs. The site also has a blog with practical how-to guides (for example on crimping insulated vs. non-insulated terminals) and video reviews.

## Who should buy from Haisstronica?

**A good fit if you:**

- Do automotive, marine, RV, trailer or household wiring and want connectors and tools from one place.
- Are building a first wiring kit and like the task-based organization.
- Buy in bulk for a business.

**Think twice if you:**

- Want free returns for any reason. Preference returns carry return shipping and a possible restocking fee.
- Need connectors certified to a specific industry standard; check each product's specifications first.

## The bottom line

Haisstronica is a practical, affordable specialist for wire connectors and crimping tools, with clear product organization, free U.S. shipping and fulfillment through Amazon's network. Its return terms are fair for defective items but less generous for change-of-mind returns, so match the connector gauge and tool to your project before ordering.
