## Overview

The **SIMSONN Plus X Load Cell Pedal with Haptics Reactor** is first in SIMSONN's best-selling sort. It comes as a **2-pedal P2-H2** or a **3-pedal P3-H3** with a clutch, in silver or black.

This review is based on SIMSONN's product page, policies and store ratings as of November 2026. We haven't raced on these pedals ourselves. Feel and durability claims below are SIMSONN's.

## Key features

- **Brake:** load cell supporting up to **200 kg**, with a dual-stage design combining short spring travel and elastomers.
- **Damping:** a hydraulic buffer system on throttle, brake and clutch, per SIMSONN.
- **Build:** 3.5–4 mm laser-cut steel with anti-rust treatment and ball bearings.
- **Adjustment:** pedal angle, travel and force curves.
- **Software:** SIMSONN Manager handles curves, sensitivity and stored profiles. Its changelog shows ongoing updates, now at version 2.1.9.

## The haptics question

This is the biggest thing to check. The product title says "with Haptics Reactor" and it costs more than the [plain Plus X set](/best/simsonn-plus-x-vs-plus-x-v2/) ($179–$229 without a baseplate). Yet its package list names **no motors or power supply**, and its description calls haptics optional. One reviewer says the motors arrived about two weeks after the pedals. Ask SIMSONN exactly what's included before paying the higher price.

## Limits to know

- **PC only.** No PlayStation, Xbox or Mac, according to this page.
- **Not the latest model.** The [Plus X V2](/best/simsonn-plus-x-vs-plus-x-v2/) adds angle sensors and an RJ12 connection for supported wheelbases.
- **Conflicting weights.** The description says about 5.1 and 7.1 kg, while the spec table gives 4.26 and 5.01 kg without a baseplate (7.05 and 8.84 kg with one).
- **Mounting.** Baseplate, heel plate and heightening plate are listed "if purchased". You'll need a rig or a solid way to fix the pedals down.

## Ratings

The page shows **4.8/5 from 596 reviews**, but the same figure appears on the V2 page, so the reviews are **pooled across models**. The star split shown is 74% 5-star and 10% 1-star, and it repeats across other products too.

Separately, the comparison site OC Racing calls Plus X quality control hit-or-miss, citing user reports of dead load cells, faulty control boxes and a fragile plastic throttle spring holder, and mentions grounding the rig to fight electrical interference. We haven't verified these reports, but they're a reason to test your pedals as soon as they arrive. SIMSONN's Trustpilot score is **2.4 from 9 reviews**, mainly about delays and support. See our [SIMSONN review](/reviews/simsonn-review/).

## Price and warranty

- **P2-H2:** $295.90 (silver), $299 (black).
- **P3-H3:** $389 (silver), $392.59 (black). Both are just under the **$399** free-shipping line.
- **Warranty:** 24 months on the pedals. Springs and elastomers are covered only if defective on arrival.
- **Returns:** request within 30 days. Refunds carry a 2.9% processing fee, and return rules need confirming by email.

Code **TRANTRUNG** takes **8% off** at the time of writing.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** PC-only racers who want a high-limit load cell, and who confirm the haptics included.
- **Think twice:** console users, anyone who wants plug-and-play reliability, or buyers who'd rather pay less for the plain set or get the [V2](/best/simsonn-plus-x-vs-plus-x-v2/).

## The bottom line

On paper, the Plus X offers a lot for the money. The doubts are the unclear haptics contents, PC-only support and mixed reports on consistency. Our verdict: **7.2/10**. Also see the [best SIMSONN products](/best/best-simsonn-products/).
