## Overview

**XGODY** is a budget electronics brand. Its About page says founder Ben started selling GPS devices on eBay in **2009** and launched the XGODY brand in **2014**. Today the store sells **GPS sat navs for cars and trucks**, Android TV boxes, projectors, tablets, phones and earbuds. Support is by email (support@xgody.com) and phone numbers in **Germany** and the **US**. The site doesn't give a company name or address.

This review covers prices, best sellers, ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on XGODY's store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't used these devices ourselves.

## What XGODY's sat navs offer

XGODY's best sellers are standalone GPS navigators. Per XGODY's product pages, they typically have:

- **7- or 9-inch touchscreens** at 800×480, with 256 MB RAM and 8 GB storage.
- **Car and truck modes**: enter your truck's height, weight, length and width to avoid restricted roads.
- **Voice guidance**, lane assist, 2D/3D maps, POI search and **speed-camera and red-light warnings**.
- **FM transmitter** to play the navigator's sound through your car stereo (it's not an FM radio).
- **BT models**: Bluetooth hands-free calling and AV-in for a backup camera.
- **Free lifetime map updates** (see below for how they work).

They run Windows CE, an old system, and some listings say the X10 shows "real-time road congestion"; with no data connection, we'd treat that claim with caution.

**Speed-camera alerts are illegal in some countries** (for example, France and Germany restrict them while driving). Check your local rules and turn the feature off if needed.

## Ratings: store vs Trustpilot

- **On XGODY's product pages:** the sat navs show **4.1–4.8/5**. The 886BT (1,108 reviews) and 718 (1,045) have the most; the X4, despite being the top seller, shows just 9.
- **On Trustpilot:** **1.4/5 from 125 reviews**, **82% 1-star**. Recent complaints describe tablets, phones and dash cams **failing within weeks or months**, **warranty claims not honoured** or answered with partial refunds, and slow support. Trustpilot notes XGODY hasn't replied to negative reviews.

That's one of the biggest gaps between store and independent ratings we've seen. Many complaints are about non-GPS products, but they point to weak after-sales support.

## Returns and warranty: short and defect-only

Per XGODY's warranty and refund pages:

- **0–14 days:** refund or replacement if the item has a **quality problem**.
- **15–30 days:** free **replacement** for defects.
- **31–180 days:** free **repair**; you send the item to XGODY's address.
- Contact support first; return in **original packaging** with tracking. Damage in return transit isn't accepted.
- Accidental damage isn't covered (XGODY says it may repair it for a fee).

There's **no clear change-of-mind return** policy, and a **180-day** repair warranty is short for electronics. Pay with a card or PayPal that offers buyer protection.

## How map updates work

XGODY promises **free lifetime map updates**, updated once or twice a year. In practice you **email support or join its Facebook group** to get a download link, then **copy the files onto the device yourself**, deleting the old folders first. It's free, but not as simple as the automatic updates on big-name sat navs.

## Who should buy XGODY?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want a **cheap, big-screen sat nav** for a car, truck or RV and don't want to use your phone.
- Need **truck routing** on a budget.
- Are comfortable copying map files from a computer.

**Think twice if you:**

- Want reliable after-sales support and a long warranty.
- Need a backup camera bundle right now (most are out of stock).
- Drive in a country where speed-camera alerts are banned (turn them off).
