## Overview

**Keychron** designs **mechanical keyboards and mice**, from slim wireless boards to heavy, gasket-mounted aluminum "custom" keyboards. It says it has more than 40 keyboard models, that most run **open-source firmware (QMK or ZMK)**, and that it was first to bring Bluetooth, 2.4 GHz and Hall-effect (magnetic switch) support to QMK. Its store terms are governed by **Hong Kong law**. Prices on keychron.com, the Global & US store, are in **US dollars**.

This review is based on Keychron's product pages, policies, store ratings and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested Keychron keyboards ourselves. Claims about press and AI recognition are Keychron's own.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** the five keyboards above are rated **4.48 to 4.93**, from 21 to 950 reviews each.
- **Trustpilot:** **3.2 from 437 reviews** on a profile claimed in June 2026: 29% 5-star and **60% 1-star**. Recent complaints mention the Launcher web app not detecting keyboards, spongy keys or dim backlight, and orders shipped from China that took weeks. Keychron replies to some reviews.

## Shipping

- **Dispatch:** within **2 business days**.
- **Delivery estimates:** **4–6 business days** to the US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan and Mexico; **10–20** to Europe and Brazil via Yuntrack; 10–15 to parts of Southeast Asia.
- **Taxes:** prices **don't include VAT or import tax**, and Keychron can't say whether you'll be charged. If customs needs information and you don't reply within 48 hours, the parcel goes back and re-shipping costs extra.
- **Not shipped to:** PO boxes, APO/DPO, forwarding addresses, European islands and a long list of countries. Remote US areas such as Puerto Rico pay $35 more.
- **Report missing parcels** within a month of a "Delivered" status.

## Returns and warranty

- **Change of mind:** request a return **within 7 days** of delivery and **pay return shipping**. Keychron refunds the product price only, not shipping either way.
- **EU customers:** a **14-day withdrawal right**; you pay return shipping, but standard delivery charges are refunded.
- **Wrong layout or color:** email a photo with the tracking number within 7 days.
- **Cancellations:** not possible once the order has shipped.
- **Warranty:** **12 months**, and **3 months** for keycaps and palm rests. Classic K and C series boards get a replacement product or parts. **K Pro, K Max, K HE, Q, V and other customizable series get replacement parts only**, and damage from disassembly or mods is repaired at your cost. Proof of purchase is required.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** Mac users, people who type across several devices, and hobbyists who want hot-swap switches and QMK remapping at a fair price.
- **Think twice:** anyone who wants easy returns, buyers in countries with high import tax, or anyone who'd rather buy from a local retailer with a longer return window.

## The bottom line

Keychron makes popular keyboards with strong store ratings, fair prices and genuinely useful features like Mac layouts and open-source firmware, and its older models are on deep discount. But the 7-day, buyer-paid return window, parts-only warranty on its custom series and a weak Trustpilot score mean you should pick your layout and switches carefully before ordering.
