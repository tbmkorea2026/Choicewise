## In one minute

DATOUBOSS is a **budget solar-power brand selling to Europe**. If you're building an **off-grid shed, cabin or backup system**, its prices are hard to beat. The trade-offs are **uneven after-sales service**, **thin product information** (most specs are in images) and **fine print** that's less generous than the banners. Treat it as a value buy for informed DIYers, not a plug-and-play home battery.

This review is based on DATOUBOSS's EU store pages, policies, on-site reviews and its Trustpilot profile as of November 2026. We haven't used the products ourselves. Prices are in euros.

## Things that don't add up

- **Ratings:** every product shows **4.88/5 from 389 reviews**, the same number, so it's a store-wide pool.
- **"30-Day Free Returns"** on product pages vs a policy where **the buyer pays** non-defective return shipping.
- **"5-Year Warranty"** vs a policy that only replaces **free for 18 months**, then prorates.
- **"15,000 cycles"** on the 12V 324Ah title is far above the 6,000-cycle figures most LiFePO4 brands quote; treat it as a best-case claim.
- **"No VAT"** banners: check the checkout total for your country.

Happy buyers report good value and correct state-of-charge readings. Unhappy ones report a **16kWh battery not dispatched for weeks**, **email support not replying**, a **refund waited on for two months** and **a battery terminal that melted** after five months.

## Shipping, returns and warranty

- **Shipping:** free to most EU countries from Germany, Poland or the UK; remote areas may pay extra. Processing 1–2 business days, then 3–15 business days.
- **Returns:** 30 days, resellable with original packaging; **you pay** non-defective return shipping.
- **Warranty:** free replacement up to **18 months**, then a depreciation-based price difference.
- **Discount:** **PARKCHOICE** = 10% off.

## Safety

Large LiFePO4 batteries and hybrid inverters handle dangerous currents. Use correctly rated cables, fuses and breakers, follow the manual, and have grid-connected or home-wired systems installed by a qualified electrician; many countries also require registration with the grid operator.
