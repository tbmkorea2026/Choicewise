## Overview

**NIU** is a Chinese maker of smart electric two-wheelers, from mopeds to kick scooters. It says it has **3 million+ riders** in **52+ countries**. **shop.niu.com** is its **US online store**, focused on **KQi electric kick scooters**, with a US office in **San Jose, California** and its headquarters in **Changzhou, China**.

This review is based on NIU's US product pages, policies, store ratings and Trustpilot as of October 2026. We haven't ridden NIU scooters ourselves.

## Safety first

Electric scooters can reach **15–20 mph**. Always wear a **helmet**, check **local laws** on where scooters may be ridden and at what age, use lights at night and respect the **maximum load** and **hill-grade** limits listed for each model. Charge with the supplied charger and stop using a battery that's swollen, damaged or overheating.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** **4.7–4.8/5**, but from small samples, and several models (the KQi 300X and 300P, the 100P and 100F) show **identical review counts**, suggesting a shared review pool.
- **Trustpilot:** **1.4/5 from 553 reviews** (claimed): **84% 1-star**. Complaints describe dead batteries and error codes, scooters failing early, unanswered emails, complicated warranty claims, a lack of repair centers and slow refunds. The profile covers NIU globally, not only the US store.

## Shipping

- **Free ground shipping** (UPS/FedEx) to the **lower 48 US states** from **California**; no Hawaii, Alaska, Puerto Rico, PO boxes or APO.
- Orders usually ship within **3 working days**; delivery takes **3–10 business days**.
- No import tax; local sales tax may apply. Canada uses **shopca.niu.com**.
- You can cancel until the order ships.

## Returns and warranty

- **30-day returns** with prior authorization from **Global-Service@niu.com**; items must be complete, undamaged and in original packaging.
- **Change-of-mind returns** are accepted if the odometer reads **10 miles or less**; other returns are **at your cost**.
- **Warranty:** **2 years** on the main body (battery, charger, frame, motor, controller, dashboard, fork, harness); **6 months** on accessories (brake parts, throttle); **3 months** on consumables (lights, bell, fender, kickstand). It starts on the **delivery date** for online orders. Water damage, flat tires and collisions aren't covered.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** US riders who want a well-specced scooter with app features and are ready to test it thoroughly within 30 days.
- **Think twice:** if you need dependable local repairs, or the after-sales complaints on Trustpilot worry you.

## The bottom line

NIU's US store offers capable KQi scooters, free shipping, a fair 30-day return window and a 2-year main-body warranty. The 1.4 Trustpilot score is a serious warning about reliability and support, so ride your scooter hard in the first weeks and keep every receipt. Our verdict: **6.2/10**.
