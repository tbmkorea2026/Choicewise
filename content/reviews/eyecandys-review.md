## Safety first: colored contacts are medical devices

In the US, the **FDA** treats colored and decorative contact lenses, even with no vision correction, as **medical devices**, and it advises buying them only **with a prescription** from an eye doctor who has checked your eyes. Poorly fitting or poorly cared-for lenses can cause serious eye infections.

What that means at EyeCandys:

- **Prescription:** EyeCandys says it **requires a valid contact lens prescription for every US order**, including **0.00** lenses. You can upload it, or give your eye doctor's name and phone number so EyeCandys can verify it.
- **Certifications vary by lens.** EyeCandys lists **US FDA** documents for three manufacturers it carries: **GEO Medical, Interojo and NEO Vision**. Its **own-brand best sellers** we checked (Glossy, Cashmere, Doe) list **Korea KGMP, Korea FDA and EU ISO/CE** on their product pages, **not US FDA clearance**. If you're in the US and that matters to you, ask EyeCandys about the specific lens before ordering.
- **Wear them as directed.** Stick to the replacement schedule (daily, 3-month, 6-month and so on), never share lenses, and see an eye doctor if you get redness, pain or blurred vision.

Positive Trustpilot reviews praise comfort, natural looks and helpful support. Negative ones mention **long waits for delivery**, **colors looking different from the photos** and **refund disputes**. EyeCandys replies to many of them.

## Shipping

- **Processing:** 2–4 business days (longer if a product page says so; the **24H Ship** collection dispatches faster).
- **US standard:** **free over $75**, **10–14 business days**, with no import charges on delivery.
- **Express:** about 4–7 working days, at extra cost (roughly $26–$34 below the free threshold).
- EyeCandys ships from **several warehouses**; mixing items from different warehouses can add shipping fees.
- It ships to about **30 countries**, including Canada, the UK, Australia, Germany and Japan.

## Returns: the 30-Day Guarantee

- Only for **first-time customers**.
- Covers **one opened pair** of **EyeCandys or Pink Label** colored lenses, bought at regular price.
- If you don't like it, you can **exchange** it for another pair of the same brand or get a **refund**, **excluding shipping fees**. Unopened pairs from the same order can be handled too.
- **Not covered:** other brands, glasses, press-on nails, sale, sample and gift items, and repeat customers.

Claims go through the **help.eyecandys.com** help desk within **30 days of receipt**.
