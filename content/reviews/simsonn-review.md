## Overview

**SIMSONN** sells **sim racing hardware**: load cell pedals, direct drive wheel bases, steering wheels, H-pattern and sequential shifters, handbrakes and pedal haptic motors. Its pitch is making high-performance gear more accessible.

This review is based on SIMSONN's product pages, policies, store ratings and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested SIMSONN products ourselves.

## Who runs it

The terms list **Suite 9090, 61 Bridge Street, Kington, UK**, a **+86 (China) phone number** and **service@simsonn.com**, while the warranty and shipping pages use support@simsonn.com. They name **no legal company**, and the governing-law clause names no country. Trustpilot lists the business in the United States. That makes it harder to know who you'd be dealing with in a dispute, so pay with a method that has buyer protection.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** **4.6–4.8/5** on the best sellers. The same 4.8 from 596 reviews appears on both the Plus X with Haptics page and the new Plus X V2 page, so ratings are **pooled across models**. The same star split (74% 5-star, 10% 1-star) appears on every product page we checked, so it doesn't look product-specific.
- **Trustpilot:** **2.4 from 9 reviews**, 78% 1-star, on an unclaimed profile. Complaints cover shipping delays, unanswered emails and difficult refunds. It's a small sample.
- **Elsewhere:** the comparison site OC Racing calls Plus X quality control hit-or-miss, citing user reports of dead load cells and faulty control boxes, and says some setups need grounding to fight electrical interference. We haven't verified these reports.

## Shipping

- **Warehouses:** US and Germany; the policy says products may ship from different countries.
- **Free:** orders over **$399**, 5–12 business days. Some free-shipping orders may lack tracking.
- **Paid:** Eco 9–15, Standard 5–12 or Express 3–7 business days. For up to 3 kg, that's $9.90, $14.90 or $29.90. Heavier parcels cost more, and the rate table is unclear on exact prices.
- **Handling:** 1–2 business days on top of delivery time. Trustpilot reviewers report longer waits than listed.
- **Duties:** covered for the **US, UK and EU**, per SIMSONN. Elsewhere you pay them.
- PO boxes and military addresses aren't supported.

## Returns and warranty

- **Window:** request within **30 days** of receipt, then wait for a label. Don't send anything back before SIMSONN replies.
- **Conflict:** one list suggests a manufacturing defect is required, while another line says you pay shipping for personal reasons. Confirm by email before relying on a change-of-mind return.
- **Fee:** all refunds carry a **2.9% processing fee**, with a stated minimum of £5. Refused deliveries have return shipping deducted.
- **Cancellation:** within **12 hours** of ordering.
- **Warranty:** **24 months** on main hardware, 12 on power adapters, 6 on cables. Springs, elastomers, screws and brackets are covered only if defective on arrival. SIMSONN covers shipping on confirmed warranty issues. The terms also say products are "as is", which sits awkwardly beside this warranty.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** PC sim racers who want a load cell brake or a shifter at a low price and can troubleshoot setup problems.
- **Think twice:** if you want a named company, quick support or a simple return, or if you race on console and haven't confirmed compatibility.

SIMSONN offers a lot of hardware for the money, and a 24-month warranty on paper.
