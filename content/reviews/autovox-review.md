## In one minute

AUTO-VOX's pitch is simple: a **backup camera you can fit yourself in minutes**, with no video cable running the length of the car. Its best-known models are **solar-assisted, battery-powered cameras** that clip to a licence plate (Solar 3A series) or stick on magnetically (Solar 5 series, aimed at RVs and trailers).

The products rate well on AUTO-VOX's own site, but **independent reviews are more mixed**. The warranty is standard, and returns cost you shipping unless the camera is faulty.

This review is based on AUTO-VOX's product pages, policies and on-site reviews, plus its Trustpilot profile, as of November 2026. We haven't tested the cameras ourselves.

Happy reviewers on Trustpilot mostly mention **easy installation and a clear picture**, especially on caravans and motorhomes. Unhappy ones mention **cameras or charging failing after a few months** and **slow replies on replacements or warranty claims**. Register your camera for the extra 6 months of warranty, and keep your order emails.

## Claims worth checking

- **"100% truly wireless":** true at the camera end. The monitor still needs USB or 12V power.
- **Viewing angle:** the Solar3A Plus bullet list says 140° wide-angle; its spec table says 110° horizontal.
- **"Ranked #1" badges:** product pages show German test awards (heise, F.A.Z. Kaufkompass). We haven't verified them.
- **Recording:** only some models record (Solar3A Plus Max, Solar 5B, WF4). The Solar3A Plus spec table says storage isn't supported.

More on what to check before buying in our [wireless backup camera checklist](/best/wireless-backup-camera-buying-checklist/).

## Shipping, warranty and returns

- **Shipping:** free on orders over $49; US standard 3–7 business days; express $19.99 in some countries. Ships to the US, Canada, Japan, Australia and parts of Europe; some products are US-only.
- **Warranty:** 12 months, **18 months if you register** your product on AUTO-VOX's site.
- **Returns:** 30 days, unused and in original packaging. **You pay return shipping** unless it's a verified quality issue. **Clearance items can't be refunded.**
- **Discount:** code **PARKCHOICE** gives 12% off.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone who wants a camera at the back without wiring, especially on a trailer or RV where a cable run is a pain.
- **Think twice:** if you want a camera that's always on while driving with zero charging (consider a wired kit), or if you're worried about support after the first few months.

## The bottom line

AUTO-VOX makes the easiest backup cameras to fit, and its on-site ratings are strong. Independent reviews point to patchy durability and support, so register for the extended warranty. Our verdict: **7.1/10**.
