## Safety notes

- **Tire inflators:** inflate to the pressure on your vehicle's door placard, not the tire's maximum and not the inflator's 150 PSI limit. Let the unit cool between long sessions and check the reading with a separate gauge now and then.
- **Rotary tools and vacuums:** wear eye protection when grinding, cutting or polishing, and don't use the vacuums on wet spills, ash or fine construction dust unless the manual says it's safe.
- **Batteries:** the batteries in these tools are built in and not replaceable, so follow the charging instructions and don't leave them in a hot car for long periods.

## Ratings

- **Trustpilot:** **3.7/5 from 131 reviews** (claimed in April 2024): **54% 5-star, 6% 4-star, 3% 3-star, 4% 2-star, 33% 1-star**. Fanttik replies to about a third of negative reviews. Praise covers build quality and products that work as advertised; complaints describe orders that took weeks to ship, parcels that never arrived and slow support or refunds.

The gap between product ratings and Trustpilot suggests the tools themselves are generally liked, while ordering and support from the store are where problems show up.

## Shipping

- **Free shipping** on all products (the policy has an asterisk it doesn't explain).
- **Processing:** 1–2 business days; **delivery:** 2–7 business days.

## Returns and warranty

- **Returns:** within **30 days of delivery**, unused, in the **original packaging**, with proof of purchase. **No restocking fee.** Email **support@fanttik.com** for the return address first.
- **Return shipping:** **you pay** for change-of-mind returns; Fanttik pays if the item arrived **damaged or wrong**.
- **Refunds:** after inspection, usually back to your payment method within 1–5 business days. Custom items can't be returned.
- **Warranty:** **1 year** from receipt on most products, **6 months** on scooters, **2 years** on line lasers. Some products offer a paid extended warranty through **Xcotton** (with AIG). The warranty page doesn't spell out what is excluded or how to file a claim, so contact support.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** US buyers who want a small, rechargeable inflator, car vacuum or precision driver with lots of published reviews and a clear 30-day return window.
- **Think twice:** buyers in excluded states or territories, anyone who needs a fast answer from support, or people who expect pro-grade power from palm-sized tools.
