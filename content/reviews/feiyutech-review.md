## Overview

**FeiyuTech** has been making gimbals for nearly two decades. According to the company, it was **established in 2007**, is headquartered in **Guilin, China**, has **more than 500 employees** and runs an R&D and marketing center in **Shenzhen**. Its official online store sells camera gimbals, phone gimbals and pocket gimbal cameras worldwide.

This review covers the product line-up, prices, customer ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on the official FeiyuTech store as of October 2026.

## Prices

FeiyuTech's prices are accessible for capable gimbals. At the time of writing, the SCORP Mini 2 starts at about **$139**, the SCORP Mini 3 Pro at about **$220**, the SCORP-C at **$229**, the SCORP 3 at about **$319** and the Feiyu Pocket 3 kit at **$329**. Many models come as a base version or a **kit** with extra accessories. New subscribers are offered 10% off their first order by email.

## Returns and warranty

- **30 calendar days** to request a return, **including for no reason**, as long as the product is in resellable condition with all original accessories and packaging.
- Returns are also accepted if the product isn't compatible with a camera or phone that isn't on the official compatibility list.
- **Return shipping is paid by the customer** unless the return is due to a quality issue.
- **Warranty:** one year for products bought from the official store; **two years for orders shipped to the EU**.
- Support: store@feiyu-tech.com for orders, service@ or support@feiyu-tech.com for technical help (Mon–Fri, 9:00–18:00 GMT+8).

## Who should buy FeiyuTech?

**A great fit if you:**

- Shoot with a mirrorless or DSLR camera and want a capable gimbal at an accessible price.
- Vlog solo and want features like AI tracking and native vertical shooting.
- Live in the US, Canada, UK or EU and can benefit from local warehouses.

**Think twice if you:**

- Need a long warranty outside the EU.
- Want free return shipping for change-of-mind returns.

## The bottom line

FeiyuTech combines nearly two decades of gimbal experience with a wide, well-priced line-up and helpful compatibility tools. Strong customer ratings, free shipping over $90 and 30-day returns make it a solid choice for creators. Check the compatibility list for your camera and lens before you buy, and you're likely to be happy with the result.
