## Overview

The **Pironman 5** sits at **#1 on SunFounder's best-seller list** at the time of writing. ## What's in the box, and what you need

![SunFounder Pironman 5: what you get and what you need](/assets/img/products/sunfounder-pironman-5-whats-included.webp)

**Included:** aluminum chassis with acrylic panels, IO board (4 RGB LEDs, IR receiver, GPIO extender), 0.96" OLED, metal power button, tower cooler with PWM fan, two 40 mm PWM RGB fans, NVMe PIP board, HDMI adapter board, power switch converter, micro SD extender, **1220 RTC battery**, screws and tools.

**Not included:** the **Raspberry Pi 5**, a **27W USB-C power supply**, a **micro SD card**, an **M.2 NVMe SSD**, plus a monitor, HDMI cable, keyboard and mouse.

## Storage and PCIe

The NVMe PIP board takes one **M.2 NVMe** drive in **2230, 2242, 2260 or 2280** sizes, or a **Hailo-8L** AI accelerator. SunFounder says the link is **certified for PCIe Gen 2.0 (5 GT/s)** and can be **forced to Gen 3.0 (10 GT/s)**.

> **Check your SSD first.** SunFounder lists drives to avoid, mainly those with **Phison controllers**: WD Green SN350, WD Black SN770 and SN850, Corsair MP600, Samsung PM991, Inland TN446, Kingston OM8SEP4256Q-A0 and Transcend 110Q. It warns that the **SN350 and SN570 stop the Raspberry Pi 5 from booting**.

## Cooling, display and ports

- **Cooling:** a **tower cooler** with its own PWM fan sits on the Pi's CPU, and **two 40 mm RGB fans** with dust filters move air through the case.
- **OLED:** a **0.96" (128×64)** screen shows CPU and RAM use, temperature and IP address.
- **Ports:** **two standard HDMI** ports (no micro-HDMI adapters needed), an **external GPIO extender** and an **IR receiver**.
- **Power:** a **metal power button** with **safe shutdown**, and an **RTC battery** so the Pi keeps time when unplugged.
