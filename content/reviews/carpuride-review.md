## Overview

**Carpuride** makes portable screens that bring **Apple CarPlay and Android Auto** to motorcycles and older cars. Its About page says it has focused on automotive electronics for **12 years**, does OEM work for other car-audio brands, and has 200 engineers and 3,000 workers (per Carpuride). The contact page lists **service@carpuride.com**, a Hong Kong phone number and an address in **North Point, Hong Kong**.

The range covers 5-, 6- and 7-inch **waterproof motorcycle screens**, **BMW-specific models** that use the bike's original controls, car dash screens and double-DIN stereos, plus tire-pressure sensors (TPMS) and mounts.

This review covers prices, best sellers, ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on Carpuride's store and policies as of October 2026 and Trustpilot. We haven't used these devices ourselves.

## Safety first: screens on a motorcycle

- **Set routes before you ride.** Any screen on the handlebars is a distraction. Use voice control or the handlebar button (or Wonder Wheel on BMW models) instead of touching the screen while moving.
- **Never watch video while riding.** The W702 Plus can install apps such as YouTube and Netflix (per Carpuride); keep those for when you're parked.
- **Check local rules** on handlebar screens and dash cameras before you mount one.
- **Wire it correctly.** Carpuride warns that the W702 Plus's Type-C port is not for power, and more than 5 V through it can burn out the unit. If you're not comfortable connecting to the battery, have a shop do it.

## Ratings: store vs Trustpilot

- **On Carpuride's product pages:** every best seller scores **4.73–4.83/5**, and the rating breakdowns show **no 1-, 2- or 3-star reviews at all**, even on the W702BS with 2,142 reviews. That's very unusual and suggests low ratings aren't shown.
- **On Trustpilot:** **4.3/5 from 442 reviews**: 68% 5-star but **21% 1-star**. Many recent reviews praise **fast, helpful support** and firmware fixes. The 1-star reviews describe **units dying within days**, long back-and-forth with support, and being asked to **pay shipping for repairs**. Trustpilot notes the profile is unclaimed and the company doesn't invite reviews.

So: plenty of happy riders, but a real minority with failed units. Judge it on Trustpilot, not the store's stars.

## Shipping

- **Free shipping** on all products to most countries (per Carpuride).
- **Dispatch:** within **1–3 days**, via **DHL, UPS or DPD**, with tracking by email.
- **Delivery:** usually **7–15 business days**; local warehouses in some areas deliver in about 2 days.
- **Brazil:** needs a CPF/CNPJ tax ID; postal shipping takes 30–60 days.

## Returns: 30 days, with conditions

Per Carpuride's returns page:

- Request a return within **30 days of delivery** by emailing **service@carpuride.com**; **unauthorized returns aren't accepted**.
- **No fault (change of mind):** full refund, but **you pay return shipping**, and items that have been **opened or used** and can't be resold may be refused (at support's judgment).
- **Faulty item:** full refund with return shipping covered.
- Items must include all packaging, manuals and accessories.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within **7–15 business days** of approval.

The returns page also suggests declaring a low customs value on returned items; declare according to your country's rules.

## Warranty: 2 years on main parts

- **2 years** on main components (screen, motherboard); **6 months** on chargers, cables and brackets.
- Free repair or part replacement for defects; a same-model replacement if repair isn't possible.
- Carpuride pays shipping to its service center only within **3 months**; after that, **you pay to send it in**. It pays the return trip.
- **Not covered:** misuse, unofficial power sources, drops, unofficial firmware, and units where you kept the item after a partial refund of more than 30%.

That's a longer warranty than many direct-to-consumer gadget brands offer, but the shipping cost after 3 months is a common Trustpilot complaint.

## Who should buy Carpuride?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want **CarPlay or Android Auto** on a motorcycle without mounting your phone.
- Ride a **BMW** with a factory nav mount and want to use the **Wonder Wheel** (check the compatibility list).
- Want **TPMS, intercom audio and an SOS crash call** in one unit.

**Think twice if you:**

- Use a **HarmonyOS** phone or an Android model not on the compatibility list.
- Want easy no-questions returns: opened units may be refused unless faulty.
- Just need navigation: a phone mount is far cheaper.
