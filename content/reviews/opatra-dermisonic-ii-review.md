## Overview

The **Dermisonic II** is the first product in Opatra's **Best Sellers** collection, priced at **£2,999**. It's a handheld device with a silicone cover and touchscreen, combining **LED light therapy, radio frequency (RF), heat and vibration**.

This review is based on Opatra's product page and policies as of October 2026. We haven't used the device and can't confirm its skin results. For more on the company, including the 2026 Hong Kong sales probe, read our [Opatra review](/reviews/opatra-review/).

## What it does, per Opatra

- **Red LED + RF (Anti-Ageing mode):** RF is described as heat that works beneath the skin and helps trigger new collagen.
- **Blue LED (Skin-Nourish mode).**
- **Green LED (Even-Tone mode).**
- **Heat therapy:** for circulation and product absorption.
- **Multi-Facial:** all LED colors together.
- **Vibration:** can be turned on or off on the touchscreen.

Opatra lists these benefits as "helps to" statements, such as supporting cell renewal and reducing the look of fine lines. The page doesn't link to published trial results for this device, so treat them as marketing claims.

## Safety

- **Get the contraindications before buying.** They aren't listed on this product page, so request them from **info@opatra.com**.
- **Ask a doctor first** if you're pregnant, have implanted electronics, have a skin condition or use light-sensitizing medicine. This is general caution, not Opatra's wording.
- **Stop if you notice irritation** or discomfort, and follow the manual's treatment times.

## Ratings

The Dermisonic II shows **5.0/5 from 18 store reviews**, a small sample for a device at this price. Opatra overall has **4.5/5 from 1,054 reviews on Trustpilot**, with 9% 1-star. Some complaints there mention devices failing and warranty fees. See our [Opatra review](/reviews/opatra-review/) for details.

## Warranty and returns

- **Returns:** within 30 days, only **unused** in original packaging; you pay shipping.
- **Warranty:** limited lifetime, **UK and EU only**, with registration.
- **Replacement costs:** a shipping fee in the first 12 months, then **£40** each.
- **Replacements** may be new or refurbished.

## Value

At **£2,999** (about **£2,699** with code **TRUNGTRAN**, 10% off at the time of writing), the question is whether all-in-one convenience justifies the cost. Opatra's own [LumiQuartz wand](/best/opatra-dermisonic-ii-vs-lumiquartz/) costs £499 with red LED, heat and vibration, but no RF, blue or green LED. See how it ranks among the [best-selling Opatra products](/best/best-opatra-products/).

## Who should buy it?

**A possible fit if you:**

- Want **LED and RF in one device**, and the price is comfortable.
- Live in the **UK or EU** and will register the warranty.

**Think twice if you:**

- Want proven results before spending this much.
- Live outside the warranty area.
- Are being pushed to decide quickly in person.

## The bottom line

The Dermisonic II has an impressive feature list on paper, but its price is hard to justify from published information alone. If you still want it, get the contraindications first, register the warranty and remember that you can't normally return it once used. Overall: **6.5/10**.
