## Battery and conversion safety

Replacement batteries and conversion kits carry real risks if they don't match your bike. Please read this before you buy.

- **Match everything:** voltage (24V, 36V or 48V), mount, size and **discharge connector**. Yose Power's own FAQ says you must check size and discharge port yourself, and some listings warn that two similar-looking batteries use different plugs.
- **Use the right charger** for the battery, charge on a hard, non-flammable surface, and don't charge a battery that is damaged, swollen or wet.
- **Second-hand batteries** are also sold on the site. Expect less capacity and life than new.
- **Conversion kits** affect brakes, wheels and wiring. If you're not confident, have a bike mechanic fit or check it.
- **Road rules:** in the EU and UK, a pedelec is limited to a **250W** rated motor with assist cutting out at 25 km/h (15.5 mph in the UK). Some Yose Power kits are **350W**; check local rules before riding one on public roads.
- **Recycling:** used lithium batteries must go to a battery collection point, not household waste.

## Returns and warranty

- **Return window conflict:** the refund policy allows **30 days** from purchase, unused and in original packaging, with proof of purchase. The returns page says to send items back **within 7 days** after the return is confirmed. EU online buyers usually also have a statutory 14-day withdrawal right, which the policy doesn't mention.
- **Costs:** you pay return shipping and carry the risk for change-of-mind returns. Wrong or damaged items get a return label.
- **Sale items** can't be returned, per the refund policy, and several best sellers have discounted variants.
- **Warranty:** **18 months** on batteries and **12 months** on chargers (product pages); the warranty page says 12–18 months, limited. Within 30 days Yose Power covers parts and labor; after that it sends free replacement parts and **you pay shipping**. Inspect your order within **3 days**, and charge a stored battery at least monthly to keep cover.
- **Out-of-warranty repairs** have published prices, such as €30 labor plus €10 for a new battery management board.
