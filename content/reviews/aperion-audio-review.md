## Overview

**Aperion Audio** is one of the original direct-to-consumer speaker brands. It was started in **Portland, Oregon in 1998** by founder **Win Jeanfreau**, who, according to the company's own story, set out to build great speakers after a frustrating hunt for a home stereo on a $1,500 budget. More than 28 years later, Aperion is headquartered in **Wilsonville, Oregon**, and still sells primarily online, cutting out the retail middleman.

This review covers what Aperion sells, how much it costs, its warranty and return policies, and who it is best for. It is based on Aperion's official website and policy pages as of October 2026.

## What makes Aperion different

### Direct-to-consumer value

Aperion sells directly online rather than through retail showrooms. According to the company, cutting out the middleman lets it offer speakers at a far lower price than comparable retail products. Sales are frequent: at the time of writing, several Verus and Bravus models were discounted 20–30%.

### A long, transferable warranty

Most Aperion speaker lines (Grandis, Verus III, Novus, Theatrus and Clearus) carry a **5-year warranty** on cabinets, drivers and crossovers, and Aperion's Energy amplifiers are also covered for 5 years. Subwoofers get 5 years on cabinets and drivers and 2 years on the amplifier, while super tweeters are covered for 3 years. Warranties are **transferable**, and Aperion offers **lifetime support** with repairs, diagnostics and replacement parts where available.

### 30-day in-home audition

You can listen to Aperion speakers in your own room for **30 days**, and Aperion says it will extend the trial if you need more time. That matters, because speakers sound very different at home than in a showroom.

### Shipping and financing

Shipping is **free in the continental U.S.**, and in-stock orders usually ship within **2 business days**, typically arriving 2–7 business days later. Financing is available through **Affirm**, starting at 0% APR for eligible buyers.

## The downsides

- **10% restocking fee:** Aperion applies a 10% restocking fee to returned products, so the home audition is not entirely free if you send something back.
- **Strict return condition:** returns must be like-new, in the original packaging, with all accessories and manuals.
- **Mostly U.S.-focused:** free shipping covers the continental U.S. only, and international buyers are pointed to local dealers.
- **Shorter coverage on some products:** 3 years for super tweeters and 2 years for subwoofer amplifiers.

## Who should buy from Aperion?

**Aperion is a great fit if you:**

- Are building a home theater or stereo system and want a matched set of speakers, subwoofers and amps from one brand.
- Live in the continental U.S. and want free shipping and an in-home trial.
- Value a long, transferable warranty and real phone support.

**Look elsewhere if you:**

- Want completely free returns: the 10% restocking fee may put you off.
- Live outside the U.S. without a local Aperion dealer.

## The bottom line

Aperion Audio combines nearly three decades of experience, a complete product range, strong warranties and direct-to-consumer pricing. The 30-day in-home audition makes it easier to buy speakers online, as long as you are comfortable with the 10% restocking fee if you return them. For U.S. buyers building or upgrading a system, Aperion is one of the safest direct-to-consumer brands to consider.
