## Overview

**NIIMBOT** is a Chinese label-printer brand selling small, battery-powered **Bluetooth label makers** that you design and print from the free **NIIMBOT app** (and a PC driver for many models). Its site, **niimbots.com**, calls itself the NIIMBOT Official Store and ships from **US, UK and EU warehouses**. NIIMBOT says its printing platform has 700,000+ registered users (its own figure). The site doesn't list a company name or address.

This review covers the range, prices, best sellers, ratings, shipping and returns, based on NIIMBOT's store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't used these printers ourselves.

## Direct thermal vs thermal transfer

NIIMBOT sells two kinds of printer, and the difference matters:

- **Direct thermal (B1, B21 Pro, B4 and most B/D models):** prints with heat on special labels. **No ink, toner or ribbon** to buy. Labels can fade over time with heat and sunlight.
- **Thermal transfer (M2, M3):** uses a **ribbon** that melts onto the label. Labels last longer and resist water, oil, scratches and sun (NIIMBOT claims 8–10 years for M2 labels), and you can print in **colors** by changing ribbons. Ribbons are an extra running cost.

All of them need **NIIMBOT label rolls**, which the printers recognize automatically.

## Ratings: store vs Trustpilot

- **On NIIMBOT's store:** best sellers show **4.7–4.9/5**, with **343** reviews on the M2 and **141** on the B1.
- **On Trustpilot:** **2.8/5 from just 10 reviews**, and the profile is **unclaimed**. Recent complaints describe **orders taking over a month**, **automated replies with no follow-up**, **wrong or missing items** and trouble getting refunds. Older reviews praise the **easy app and print quality**.

Ten reviews is a tiny sample, but the complaints are about the store's service rather than the printers.

## Shipping

- **Processing:** 1–3 business days.
- **Delivery (shipping page):** **5–10 days** to the US and UK, **6–10 days** to the EU with **customs fees covered** on standard shipping, 6–15 days to the Middle East and Asia, **3–5 days** with express.
- NIIMBOT ships from local warehouses when in stock, otherwise from **Hong Kong**. Its return page gives a wider 3–15 days (up to 25), so allow extra time.
- If an order hasn't arrived within **45 business days**, NIIMBOT offers a refund or replacement.

## Returns and warranty: strict

- **No cancellations** once an order is placed.
- **Change of mind:** return within **30 days** of delivery, **unused and in original packaging**, **at your own cost** (no restocking fee). Exchanges are also at your cost.
- **Damaged on arrival:** send photos and video; NIIMBOT sends a replacement after verification.
- **Defective after use:** report within 30 days of arrival; you **return it at your expense** and receive **store credit**.
- **Warranty:** the refund policy mentions printers "under warranty" (serial number and all accessories required) but **doesn't state how long** the warranty lasts.

## Who should buy NIIMBOT?

**A good fit if you:**

- Run a **small business** and want cheap, phone-based printing for product, price, barcode or QR labels.
- Want to **organize your home or office** without ink or tape cartridges.
- Need a **compact 4x6 shipping printer** (B4) or durable, colored labels (M2, M3).

**Think twice if you:**

- Might change your mind (no cancellations, returns at your cost).
- Want a clearly stated warranty and responsive support.
- Need labels from other brands; NIIMBOT printers are designed around NIIMBOT rolls.

## The bottom line

NIIMBOT's printers are affordable, well-rated on its store and cover everything from a $26.99 home labeler to color thermal transfer and shipping-label printers. The weak points are the store's strict, buyer-pays return rules, no stated warranty length, mixed delivery estimates and thin, negative Trustpilot feedback. Choose your model and label size carefully before ordering. Overall: **7.0/10**.
