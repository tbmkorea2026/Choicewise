## Overview

The **Keychron K2 (Version 2)** comes first in Keychron's best-selling sort. It's a compact **75% wireless mechanical keyboard** with 84 keys, so you keep the function row, arrows and Page Up/Down/Home/End while saving space. It connects to **up to three devices over Bluetooth 5.1** or by **USB-C**, and Keychron says the Version 2 adds QMK firmware support, its Launcher web app for remapping, foam for a better sound, and upgraded Bluetooth.

This review is based on Keychron's product page, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the K2 ourselves. Battery and switch figures are Keychron's.

## Key features

- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.1 for **three devices**, plus **USB-C** wired. Polling is **1000 Hz wired** and **90 Hz over Bluetooth**. N-key rollover works in wired mode.
- **Battery:** **4000 mAh**, up to **240 hours** with the backlight off.
- **Switches:** **Keychron Super** red (linear), blue (clicky) or brown (tactile), **hot-swappable**, rated for 50 million clicks.
- **Keycaps:** **ABS**, with Mac and Windows keys.
- **Build:** ABS case, tray mount; the aluminum-frame version weighs **794 g**.
- **Backlight:** RGB with four brightness levels.

## What to check first

1. **Stock:** only the **RGB backlight, aluminum frame, Super Red** version was available. If you want clicky or tactile switches, you'd need to buy them separately and swap them in.
2. **Layout:** 75% suits most typists, but if you want a separate Insert/Delete cluster, look at the [K8](/best/keychron-k2-vs-k8/).
3. **Gaming:** Bluetooth runs at 90 Hz. Gamers should use wired mode or look at Keychron's newer 8K models.

## Ratings

The K2 is rated **4.87/5 from 950 reviews** on Keychron's store, the most of any model in the best-selling list. Keychron itself scores **3.2 on Trustpilot** from 437 reviews, 60% 1-star, with complaints about software, quality and shipping times.

## Price

**$69.99** (was $94.99) for the RGB aluminum-frame version. Other versions listed from **$49.99** were sold out. Shipping and any import tax are shown at checkout. We don't have a discount code for Keychron.

## Warranty and returns

The K2 has a **12-month warranty**; keycaps are covered for **3 months**. As a classic K-series board, Keychron may replace the keyboard or the faulty parts. **Change-of-mind returns** must be requested within **7 days** of delivery, and you pay return shipping. EU buyers have 14 days. See our [Keychron review](/reviews/keychron-review/) for details.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone wanting an affordable, compact wireless mechanical keyboard for work across a Mac, iPad and phone, and who's happy with linear red switches.
- **Think twice:** gamers, anyone set on PBT keycaps or a 2.4 GHz dongle, or buyers who want blue or brown switches out of the box.

## The bottom line

The K2 earned its place as Keychron's best seller: a sensible layout, multi-device Bluetooth, a big battery and hot-swap switches for **$69.99**. Just know you're buying an older design in the one configuration that's left, with ABS keycaps and a short return window. Our verdict: **7.6/10**.
