## Overview

The **ROCKBROS AS1 Pro** is first on rockbrosbike.us's best-selling list. It's a **pocket-size electric pump** meant to replace a mini hand pump or CO₂ cartridges for road, gravel and mountain bikes.

This review is based on ROCKBROS USA's product page, specs, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the AS1 Pro ourselves. For the store overview, see our [ROCKBROS USA review](/reviews/rockbros-us-review/).

## How it works

Turn it on, choose the unit, **set the target pressure**, attach the valve, and it **stops automatically** at the preset. An **LED screen** shows the reading, and ROCKBROS lists a pressure sensor and **multiple nozzle adapters**.

## Pressure, speed and battery

- **120 PSI** maximum, enough for high-pressure road tires.
- **95–105 seconds** to 120 PSI, per ROCKBROS.
- **7.4 V 450 mAh** battery, **USB-C** charging in about **30 minutes**.
- The spec table lists **2 inflations (0–120 PSI) per charge**; the marketing says **up to four tires**. Expect fewer full road inflations and more top-ups.

## Size and build

- **124 g**, aluminum alloy body.
- Dimensions are given as **64.5 × 48 × 28 mm** in the spec table and **64.5 × 63 × 28 mm** in the comparison table.

## Price, returns and warranty

- **$89.99**; code **TRANTRUNG** takes 10% off at the time of writing, and the store shows $8 off orders over $69.
- **Free shipping** over $50 in the contiguous US.
- **30-day returns** if unused; **you pay shipping** if you change your mind.
- **Warranty:** 2-year limited.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** riders who want set-and-forget inflation to road pressures in a jersey pocket.
- **Think twice:** if you need several full inflations between charges, or mostly run low-pressure MTB tires (the $59.99 AS1 may do).

## The bottom line

The AS1 Pro brings the features that make a mini electric pump easy to use (presets, auto stop, a screen) in a 124 g package. Its battery is small, so treat it as a ride-saver rather than a garage pump. Our verdict: **7.6/10**.
