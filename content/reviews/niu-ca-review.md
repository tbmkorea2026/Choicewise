## Overview

**NIU** is a Chinese electric two-wheeler maker that says it has more than 3 million riders in over 52 countries. Its **Canadian store, shopca.niu.com,** sells **KQi electric kick scooters**, the **BQi-C3 Pro e-bike**, chargers, tires and accessories, priced in **Canadian dollars**. The contact page lists **NIU International Co., Ltd.** in Changzhou, Jiangsu, and **NIU Technologies Group Limited** in Hong Kong. Orders ship from a **Montreal** warehouse.

This review is based on NIU Canada's product pages, policies, store ratings and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested NIU scooters ourselves. Range and speed figures are NIU's own. Shopping in the US? See our separate [NIU US store review](/reviews/niu-review/).

## Scooter safety and local rules

- **Check provincial and city rules.** E-scooter rules vary across Canada: some places allow them on certain roads or bike lanes, others restrict them. NIU says top speed varies by provincial regulation.
- **Wear a helmet** and use lights and turn signals at night. NIU sells a C$79.99 helmet with an LED light.
- **Range claims are best-case.** Rider weight, cold weather, hills and speed all cut range, and lithium batteries lose range in Canadian winters.
- **Charge with the supplied charger** and don't ride through deep water: water damage to electrical parts isn't covered.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** each in-stock vehicle has only **2 to 11 reviews**, rated **4.4 to 5.0**. Each product shows its own star split.
- **Trustpilot:** NIU's single profile for niu.com shows **1.4 from 560 reviews**, **85% 1-star**, claimed since April 2024. Complaints describe scooters failing early, range and power well below claims, error codes returning after repairs, slow or automated replies and limited repair options. Two recent 1-star reviewers based in Canada describe a battery failing after about two years and range far below the advertised figure.

## Shipping

- **Free** to Canada, except Nunavik, Nunavut, the Northwest Territories and Yukon. No PO boxes or APO.
- **From Montreal** by **UPS or FedEx**, ground only because of the lithium batteries.
- **Processing** within about 3 working days, then **3–7 business days** in transit.
- **No import tax**, but GST or provincial tax may apply.

## Returns and warranty

- **30-day returns**, after emailing Global-Service@niu.com for authorization. The policy says both "30 days from the date ordered" and "30 days after you receive them", so act early.
- **Free change-of-mind returns** if the scooter has **10 km or less** on the odometer. Otherwise you pay return shipping. No restocking fee.
- **Refunds** within 2–3 working days after inspection, which takes 3–5 working days.
- **Warranty:** **2 years** on the main body (battery, charger, frame, motor, controller, dashboard, fork), **6 months** on accessories such as brake levers and throttle, **3 months** on consumables such as lights, fenders and kickstand. Flat tires, water damage, overloading and modifications aren't covered.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** Canadian riders who want a well-equipped scooter with an app, turn signals and suspension at a deep discount, and who will test it in the first 10 km.
- **Think twice:** anyone who needs fast, reliable repairs, riders in areas where e-scooters aren't allowed, or buyers counting on the full advertised range in winter.

## The bottom line

NIU Canada's discounts are steep, and free shipping, free change-of-mind returns and a 2-year warranty on the main parts are solid on paper. The problem is what happens when something breaks: NIU's Trustpilot score is among the lowest we've seen. Buy only if you can live with that risk, test your scooter right away, and keep every receipt. Our verdict: **6.3/10**. See our [KQi 300X review](/reviews/niu-ca-kqi-300x-review/).
