## Why removal matters

Most nail damage from gel happens at removal: peeling or picking the gel lifts away the top layers of your natural nail. A proper remover softens the gel so it slides off instead. This guide covers how NailSala says to use its kit, and whether it's worth $23.99.

It's based on NailSala's product page, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't used the kit ourselves.

## Step by step

1. **Buff the shiny top coat** with the coarser file. The remover can't get through an intact top coat.
2. **Apply the remover** over each nail. Avoid flooding the skin around the nail.
3. **Wait 5–10 minutes** (NailSala's estimate).
4. **Gently push the softened gel off** with the stainless pusher, from the cuticle towards the tip.
5. **Repeat** on any stubborn spots instead of forcing them.
6. **Smooth and hydrate:** file the surface lightly and use cuticle oil.
