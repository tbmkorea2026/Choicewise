## Overview

The **ZHIYUN Weebill S** is a compact 3-axis camera gimbal with an underslung "sling" handle. It first launched several years ago and has since been followed by the Weebill 2, 3E, 3S and 5, yet it still sits at **#1 on ZHIYUN's best-seller list** at the time of writing, priced at **$279**.

This review covers the Weebill S's design, payload, features, battery life and box contents, based on ZHIYUN's official specifications. We haven't tested the gimbal ourselves, so we stick to what ZHIYUN publishes and flag what to check before buying.

## Design: compact and built for sling mode

ZHIYUN says the Weebill S is roughly **the size of a sheet of A4 paper** when folded down, at **190 × 140 × 298 mm** and **about 895 g**. Its **underslung handle** lets you hold it like a briefcase for low-angle shots, or tilt it up for a standard grip, which can take some strain off your wrist on longer shoots.

The **two-in-one quick release plate** fits both **Manfrotto and Arca-Swiss** systems, so moving the camera between gimbal and tripod is quick without having to rebalance.

## Payload and compatibility

ZHIYUN rates the Weebill S at **up to 3 kg** and says combos like a **Sony A7 III + FE 24-70mm f/2.8** and a **Canon 5D Mark IV + EF 24-70mm f/2.8** can be balanced on it. Its compatibility list includes:

- **Sony:** A9 II, A9, A7R IV, A7R III, A7 III, A7S III, A7C, A6600, A6400, A6000 series and more
- **Canon:** 5D III/IV, 6D/6D II, 80D, 90D, EOS R, RP, R5, R6, M50
- **Nikon:** D850, D780, Z5, Z6/Z6 II, Z7/Z7 II, Z50
- **Panasonic:** GH5, GH5S, S1, S1H, S5, BGH1
- **Others:** Fujifilm X-T3/X-T4/X-T30, Blackmagic Pocket 4K/6K, Z CAM E2, Sigma fp, Olympus E-M1 Mark II

Because the Weebill S is an older model, **newer camera bodies may not be on the list**. Check ZHIYUN's full compatibility list for your exact camera and lens before ordering.

## Features

- **Upgraded motors and auto tune:** ZHIYUN says a self-adaptive algorithm recognizes the load's weight and tunes the motors to match.
- **On-handle OLED display:** adjust motor strength, follow speed, smoothness, deadband and button customization **without opening the app**.
- **Focus and zoom control:** electronic focus control, plus mechanical focus/zoom with the optional **TransMount Focus/Zoom Control Motor**.
- **ViaTouch 2.0:** connects your phone, camera and gimbal so you can adjust settings like follow speed, aperture, ISO and shutter speed from the app.
- **Optional image transmission:** the **TransMount transmitter** (sold in the Image Transmission package) sends **1080p30 video up to 100 m** with monitoring tools like LUTs, false color, focus peaking and zebras, and enables **SmartFollow 2.0** object tracking.

## Battery life

ZHIYUN rates the Weebill S at **12 hours minimum and up to 14 hours**, which is enough for a full day of shooting for most people. Runtime depends on your camera's weight, balance and settings.

## Who should buy the Weebill S?

**Buy it if you:**

- Want a capable, compact gimbal for a mirrorless or lighter DSLR rig.
- Like shooting low angles in sling mode.
- Value long battery life and a simple on-handle display over newer extras.

**Look elsewhere if you:**

- Want **native vertical shooting**, a **fill light** or **Bluetooth shutter control**: consider the [Weebill 3E or 3S](/best/zhiyun-weebill-3e-vs-weebill-3s/).
- Want a **large flip-out touchscreen**: consider the Weebill 2.
- Shoot with a small camera and want the lightest option: consider the Crane M3.
