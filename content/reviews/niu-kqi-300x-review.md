## Overview

The **NIU KQi 300X** is the #1 best seller on shop.niu.com. It's NIU's top commuter kick scooter: **20 mph**, a big **608 Wh** battery, **suspension**, **disc brakes** and **turn signals**, aimed at riders who want comfort and range over portability.

This review is based on NIU's US product page, policies and ratings as of October 2026. We haven't ridden the KQi 300X ourselves.

## Key specs

Per NIU:

- **Speed and range:** **20 mph** top speed on road, **up to 37.3 miles** per charge.
- **Battery and motor:** **608.4 Wh / 13 Ah**, about **6 hours** to charge; **500 W** rated motor with an FOC sine-wave controller; **25%** hill grade.
- **Comfort:** **all-terrain suspension**, **10.5-inch self-healing tubeless tires** 2.5 inches wide, **21.3-inch** handlebars and a **6.47 × 22.4-inch** deck.
- **Safety:** **triple braking** (front and rear disc plus rear electric brake), **halo headlight**, brake lights and **turn signals**.
- **Smart features:** NIU app over Bluetooth to lock the scooter, view ride stats, customize speed, use cruise control and set a charging limit.
- **Water resistance:** **IP55**.
- **Weight:** **48.7 lb** with battery.

## Safety

Wear a helmet, follow local scooter laws and check your weight is within NIU's load limit. Range and hill climbing drop with heavier riders and steep climbs.

## Ratings

The KQi 300X shows **4.7/5** on NIU's store, but with the same **29** review count as the KQi 300P, so the reviews appear to be shared. NIU's **Trustpilot** score is **1.4/5 from 553 reviews**, with many complaints about batteries and after-sales support; see our [NIU review](/reviews/niu-review/).

## Price, shipping, returns and warranty

- **Price:** **$899** (compare-at $1,299).
- **Shipping:** free ground shipping to the lower 48 states, 3–10 business days.
- **Returns:** 30 days; change of mind accepted with **10 miles or less** on the odometer.
- **Warranty:** **2 years** on the main body (battery, motor, frame, controller), 6 months on accessories, 3 months on consumables.

## Alternatives

- **NIU KQi 300P** ($749): same frame and brakes, smaller 486 Wh battery (up to 30 miles). See [KQi 300X vs KQi 300P](/best/niu-kqi-300x-vs-kqi-300p/).
- **NIU KQi 200P** ($549): 20 mph, up to 33.6 miles, front suspension, lighter at 43.4 lb.

## The bottom line

The KQi 300X packs **long range, suspension, disc brakes and turn signals** into an **$899** scooter. It's a strong commuter on paper, held back by its **weight** and NIU's **weak after-sales reputation**. Test it thoroughly within the 10-mile change-of-mind limit. Our verdict: **7.4/10**.
