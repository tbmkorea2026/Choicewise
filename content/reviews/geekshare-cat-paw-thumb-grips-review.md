## Design

Each cap is a little **cat paw**, with toe beans and a heart-shaped pad in a contrasting color, in pastel shades like **pink, blue, grey, green, brown, yellow-pink and purple-green**. Because they're silicone, they add a soft surface on top of the Joy-Con analog sticks.

## What they do

GeekShare lists two practical benefits besides the looks:

- **Comfort:** soft silicone for a comfortable feel during everyday gaming.
- **Protection:** the caps help protect the analog sticks from **scratches and dirt**.

The raised paw shape also gives your thumb a slightly different surface to rest on than the stock stick.

They don't change how the joystick works internally; they're a cover, not a fix for stick drift.

## Compatibility

GeekShare sells **two versions**:

| Version | Fits |
| --- | --- |
| For Switch/Lite/OLED | Nintendo Switch, Switch Lite, Switch OLED |
| For Switch2 | Nintendo Switch 2 |

At the time of writing, **pink and blue** are in stock for the Switch/Lite/OLED version, and **pink and brown** for Switch 2; other colors are sold out.

## Installing them

GeekShare's instructions: **turn the cap inside out, align it with the joystick, then press it into place** until it fully wraps the stick. If a cap isn't seated all the way around, it's more likely to pop off mid-game.

## Who should buy them?

**Buy them if you:**

- Want a cheap, cute upgrade for your Switch.
- Want to keep your analog sticks cleaner and scratch-free.
- Are looking for a small gift for a Switch player.

**Skip them if you:**

- Prefer the bare feel of the stock sticks.
- Want caps for a Pro Controller or other gamepads.
