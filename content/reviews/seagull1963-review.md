## Overview

**seagull1963.com** sells its own takes on the "**Seagull 1963**", a vintage-style pilot's chronograph with a famous history. Every watch uses a **hand-wound Seagull ST19 or ST1901 column-wheel chronograph movement**, in 38, 40 or 42mm cases of steel or bronze.

This review is based on the store's product pages, policies, terms and Trustpilot reviews as of November 2026. We haven't handled the watches ourselves. Prices are shown in euros as the store displayed them to us.

## Who runs this store

This matters with this brand. The site's own pages say:

- It is **operated by Hobby Hour Limited**, a company registered in **Hong Kong**.
- It uses **genuine Seagull ST19 movements** sourced from the **Tianjin Seagull Watch Group**.
- It is **not affiliated with, authorised by, or an official partner of** Tianjin Seagull.
- It holds **Registered EU Design rights** for several of its own models.

So you're buying an independent brand's watch built around a Seagull movement, not a factory Tianjin Seagull product.

## Ratings

**Trustpilot: 4.0/5 from 377 reviews**, with 73% 5-star and 16% 1-star. Its AI summary says reviewers praise the retro design, materials and helpful support, while delivery experiences are mixed. One reviewer mentioned having to send a watch to a repair partner in Hong Kong. Product pages don't show review counts.

## Watch out for

- **Pressure tactics:** product pages show counters like "Viewed by 1,584 watch lovers in the last 24 hours" and "LAST PIECES AVAILABLE" banners. Ignore them.
- **Mixed messages on returns:** banners say "free exchanges & returns", but the policy says you pay return shipping and €30 is deducted.
- **Copy errors:** the Icon II Green listing's description describes a different watch (the Moonphase cream dial), and its page lists both 48 and 42 hours of power reserve.

## Shipping

- Orders are processed in **1–3 business days** and ship from **Hong Kong**.
- Delivery: **5–9 business days** (Asia/Oceania), **6–10** (Europe, US/Canada), **8–12** (Middle East/Africa).
- **Free shipping** with **DDP** (duties prepaid) for most destinations.

## Returns and warranty

- **14 days** from delivery for change-of-mind returns; the watch must be **unused** and in original packaging.
- **You pay return shipping**, and the store **deducts €30** (its DHL Express cost) from the refund.
- **Cancel within 30 minutes** of ordering, after that the order is in processing.
- **Warranty: 2 years.**

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone who wants an affordable mechanical chronograph with a vintage look and doesn't mind hand-winding.
- **Think twice:** if you specifically want a factory Tianjin Seagull watch, need an automatic, or want an easy, free return.

## The bottom line

seagull1963.com offers real mechanical chronographs at fair prices, with DDP shipping, a 2-year warranty and good Trustpilot reviews. Its independent status, short and costly returns and pushy sales banners hold it back. Our verdict: **7.2/10**.
