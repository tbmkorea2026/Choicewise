## Who the Standing-Mate is for

Standing desks have a problem: **standing all day is tiring**, and many people end up sitting anyway. The Standing-Mate tries to fill the gap with **in-between positions**: leaning or perching takes weight off your feet while keeping you upright and active.

This review is based on Newtral's product page, policies and on-site reviews as of November 2026. We haven't used it ourselves.

## The six positions

| Position | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Upright sitting | Normal desk work |
| Reclined sitting | Calls, reading (backrest 104°–125°) |
| Straddle sitting | Sitting backwards, chest against the backrest |
| Leaning | Half-standing at a standing desk |
| Perching | Higher seat, legs taking more weight |
| Chest-supported standing | Backrest flipped to 167° as a chest rest |

The **built-in memory-foam mat** gives your feet a cushioned surface in the standing positions.

## What to know before buying

- **It's not a full task chair.** There are no armrests listed and no adjustable lumbar, so for 8 hours of seated typing a conventional ergonomic chair is the better tool. See [which Newtral chair to buy](/best/which-newtral-chair-to-buy/).
- **Leaning takes practice.** Perching and leaning positions use your legs; expect to switch positions rather than stay in one.
- **Brand reputation.** Newtral rates 2.3/5 on Trustpilot, mostly from Kickstarter delivery complaints and slow support. Details in our [Newtral review](/reviews/newtral-review/).

## Returns and warranty

Return within 30 days if it's like-new and in the original box; **Newtral pays return shipping in the US, EU and UK**. Warranty: 3 years. Keep the packaging until you're sure.

## Safety

Changing position through the day is generally good for comfort, but this chair isn't a treatment for back or joint problems. Lock the casters or use it on a stable floor when leaning, and see a professional if you have pain.

## The bottom line

A clever companion for a standing desk with genuinely flexible positions, but pricey and backed by a brand with a weak independent reputation. Our verdict: **6.8/10**.
