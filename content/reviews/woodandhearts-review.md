## Overview

**Wood and Hearts** (woodandhearts.com, also written WoodandHearts or Wood&Hearts) sells **indoor climbing gyms** for children: climbing walls, Swedish ladders, monkey bars, ceiling ropes and swings, Montessori climbers, playhouses, foam blocks and full "playroom" bundles. Its About page says the brand started in **2019** with Pikler-style triangles and arches, and that every piece is **made in Ukraine** from **FSC-certified birch plywood and beech wood** by more than 300 craftspeople. It names the owner, **Roman Kondratiuk**.

This review is based on Wood and Hearts' product pages, policies and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested the products ourselves. Material, certification and load claims are the store's own.

## Safety first

These are climbing structures, so falls are the main risk. Wood and Hearts' own safety page asks for:

- **Adult assembly** and **adult supervision** at all times. Loose parts are a choking hazard before assembly.
- **Anchoring to a load-bearing surface** (brick, concrete, solid wood studs or metal profiles) for wall-mounted items. Not drywall alone, and not a sloping wall.
- **Safety mats** under and around climbing areas, and space away from windows and hard furniture.
- **Tightening bolts every two months** and stopping use if anything is damaged.
- Respecting the **60 kg (132 lbs) use limit**. Products are tested to 100 kg, but 60 kg is the stated maximum for use.

If you rent, or your walls are plasterboard only, look at the **two-tower playgrounds** instead of wall systems, and ask support to confirm they need no wall fixing.

## Prices

Wood and Hearts isn't cheap. Single pieces start around **$50–$170** (monkey bar kits, a baby climbing gym), wall-mounted ladders and monkey bars run **$700–$1,000**, and the best-loved complete sets cost **$1,243 to $2,547**. Big bundles go past **$3,000**. Product cards say "Free Shipping", but the shipping policy says the recipient pays the shipping fee in advance, so check the total at checkout. ## Ratings

- **On the store:** we couldn't find per-product star ratings or review counts on the product pages we checked, so we rely on Trustpilot.
- **Trustpilot:** **4.5 from 77 reviews** (68% 5-star, 13% 4-star, 10% 1-star) on a **claimed** profile with a paid subscription. The company invites customers to review and says it replied to every negative review. Praise centers on sturdy, well-finished wood and easy assembly. The weaker reviews mention long delivery times; one 4-star buyer said the order took over a month because they hadn't realized it shipped from Ukraine.

## Shipping

- **From:** mostly **Ukraine**, by UPS, DHL Express, DPD or FedEx. Some items are stocked in the US or EU.
- **Production:** in-stock items ship within 1 business day; made-to-order items take **1–7 business days** (up to **18** in the holiday season). No work on weekends.
- **Delivery:** **6–18 business days** from Ukraine, **3–10** from US/EU stock, **3–25 business days** in total.
- **Duties:** the FAQ says the USA and EU (except Norway and Switzerland) pay none; product pages say the USA and **Australia**. Ask before ordering elsewhere.
- **Lost or damaged in transit:** a new item is sent free.
- **Refusing delivery:** refusing a parcel without telling them first triggers a fee covering shipping both ways and handling.

## Returns and warranty

- **Returns:** **30 days from delivery**, for any reason. The item must be **disassembled** and in its original condition and packaging.
- **Costs:** for change-of-mind returns **you pay all shipping**, the original and the return. On furniture-size boxes from Ukraine that can be a lot.
- **Cancellation:** only while the order is in production; not after a tracking number is issued. Custom-color orders can't be cancelled.
- **Warranty:** **2 years** on wooden products, **10 years** on beech rungs, **30 days** on textiles. Not covered: outdoor use, wrong assembly, modifications, commercial use, and items that were **gifted** or bought second-hand.
- **Price match:** price-difference compensation within 10 days of purchase.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** families with a solid wall (or a big free floor) who want a long-lasting wooden climbing setup and can wait a few weeks.
- **Think twice:** renters with plasterboard walls, anyone who needs it fast, or buyers who may change their mind, since return shipping is on you.

## The bottom line

Wood and Hearts publishes more detail than most play-gym sellers: dimensions, load limits, materials, safety rules and a long rung warranty. Trustpilot feedback is mostly strong. But you're paying furniture prices, waiting for shipping from Ukraine, and taking on return costs if it doesn't fit, so measure your wall and ceiling first. Use **PARK** for 5% off.
