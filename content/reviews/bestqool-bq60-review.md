## Overview

The **BestQool BQ60** is the first product on BestQool's "All products" page sorted by best-selling, and the brand calls it its "entry-level, best-selling red light lamp". It's a compact panel for targeted sessions on the back, legs or face.

This review is based on BestQool's product page, specs, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the BQ60 ourselves. For the brand overview, see our [BestQool review](/reviews/bestqool-review/).

## Health claims and safety

- BestQool says the BQ60 relieves pain, smooths skin, reduces wrinkles and supports sleep. Those are **the brand's claims**, and its disclaimer says the products are **not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease**.
- BestQool lists it as a **Class II, 510(k) exempt** device, which means no FDA premarket review was needed. It isn't FDA approval of any benefit.
- **Wear the included eyewear**, follow the session times in the manual, and talk to a doctor first if you're pregnant, have a medical condition or take photosensitizing medication.

## Output and coverage

- **60 dual-chip LEDs** at **660 nm** (red) and **850 nm** (near-infrared).
- **105 W** power draw and **95.6 mW/cm² at 3 inches** (7.62 cm), which BestQool says it measured directly.
- **Dose:** 172.1 J/cm² after 30 minutes at 3 inches; about **80,136 J** delivered in total.
- **Coverage:** about **17.2 × 11.8 inches at 6 inches**, roughly a back or a pair of legs per session.

## Design and controls

- **Smart controller** with a timer and three modes: red, NIR or both.
- Measures **8.35 × 2.05 × 13.07 inches** and weighs **4.78 lb**; stands on its kickstand or hangs from a door with the included hook and rope.
- BestQool lists **0 EMF at 6 inches**.
- **Can't be linked** with other panels; for a modular setup, look at the Pro series.

## Price, returns and warranty

- **$199**, or **$159** open-box refurbished in the US (no returns, 1-year warranty).
- **Free shipping** in the mainland US.
- **30-day returns** with no restocking fee, but **you pay shipping** if you change your mind.
- **Warranty:** 2 years, plus a **free 1-year extension** if you register within 14 days.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** first-time RLT buyers who want a reasonably strong panel for one body area at a time.
- **Think twice:** if you want full-body coverage or plan to expand later (get a Pro panel), or if you expect a medical treatment.

## The bottom line

The BQ60 offers solid published output, useful red/NIR modes and a long warranty for $199, backed by nearly 500 store reviews. It's a sensible starter panel as long as you treat the health claims with caution. Our verdict: **8.0/10**.
