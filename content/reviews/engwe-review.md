## Safety and local rules

In the EU, e-bikes sold as pedelecs are limited to **250 W** and **25 km/h**. Wear a **helmet**, check your country's rules on **throttles and boost buttons**, respect each bike's **payload limit** (120–150 kg on these models) and charge batteries with the supplied charger.

## Returns, cancellations and warranty

- **Cancellation:** before shipping, **5%** is deducted; after shipping, **€200 per bike**.
- **Returns:** within **30 days** of delivery, with **under 10 km** on the display, complete and in original packaging. For **non-quality returns**, **€200 per bike** is deducted. Returned batteries cost €100. Request a return label first.
- **Defects:** within 30 days, free return or exchange (the €100 shipping fee isn't refunded); after 30 days, repair with ENGWE's guidance. ENGWE can **reimburse local repair shops** (with a printed invoice, up to twice the part cost).
- **Warranty:** generally **12 months** on main parts (frame, motor, battery, electronics), shorter on some components; **consumables** like tires, brake pads and saddles aren't covered. The **EP-2 3.0 Boost** gets **24 months** on core parts and the battery (or 10,000 km). Extended warranty can be added at purchase.
