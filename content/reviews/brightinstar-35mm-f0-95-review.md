## Overview

The **MF 35mm F0.95** tops **Brightin Star's best-seller list** at the time of writing. It's a compact, all-metal, **manual-focus** prime for **APS-C** (and Micro Four Thirds) mirrorless cameras, with an extremely bright **F0.95** maximum aperture, for **$189.99**. It's rated **4.9/5** on Brightin Star's store, from **8 reviews**.

We haven't used this lens ourselves, so this review is based on Brightin Star's published specs and policies and the buyer reviews on its store.

## Design and build

Brightin Star describes an **all-metal CNC body** with a retro look, a manual focus ring and an aperture ring from **F0.95 to F16**. It comes in a standard **Silver** version and a version with **glow-in-the-dark** markings. Brightin Star doesn't list electronic contacts, so expect to set aperture and focus by hand.

## Optics and what to expect

- **Field of view:** on Fuji X, Sony E, Nikon Z and Canon RF APS-C bodies, 35mm frames like about **52.5mm** on full-frame, a classic "normal" view for street and portraits. On Micro Four Thirds it's closer to **70mm**.
- **F0.95:** lets in a lot of light for night shooting and gives very shallow depth of field. That also makes **focus critical**: use focus peaking and magnification.
- **Glass:** Brightin Star lists **ED glass**. Its page says "8 elements in 11 groups", which can't be right (a lens can't have more groups than elements), so treat the exact construction as unconfirmed.
- **Buyer feedback:** one store reviewer found close focusing at F0.95 difficult and saw **chromatic aberration in backlit scenes** that faded by about **F2**. That's typical of very fast budget lenses.

The product page doesn't list **weight, filter thread or minimum focus distance**, so check with Brightin Star if those matter to you.

## Mounts

Fuji X, Sony E, Micro Four Thirds, Canon RF, Nikon Z and Canon EOS-M. Order the right one: a wrong-mount return costs a **40% restocking fee** plus shipping both ways.

## Price and policies

- **$189.99** for every mount and version at the time of writing.
- **Free shipping** over $55, but Standard takes **18–25 business days** (Priority 15–18).
- **Returns:** 90 days for defects or shipping mistakes only; **no change-of-mind returns**, and orders placed with **discount codes** can't be returned.
- **Warranty:** 2-year limited warranty on manufacturing defects.

## Who should buy it?

**A good fit if you:**

- Shoot APS-C or M43 and want the **brightest aperture** for the money.
- Enjoy manual focusing and a retro all-metal lens.
- Shoot street, portraits or night scenes.

**Think twice if you:**

- Need autofocus or shoot fast-moving subjects.
- Use a full-frame camera (see the [35mm F0.95 vs F1.4](/best/brightinstar-35mm-f0-95-vs-35mm-f1-4/) comparison).
- Want easy returns.

## The bottom line

The Brightin Star 35mm F0.95 offers a lot of aperture for $189.99 and seems popular with buyers. Go in expecting a manual, character lens rather than a clinical one, double-check your mount, and read the return rules first.
