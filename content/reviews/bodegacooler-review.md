## Overview

**BodegaCooler** (also styled BODEGA cooler) sells **12V/24V compressor fridges** for cars, trucks, boats and RVs, plus wine coolers, beverage fridges, commercial display refrigerators and power stations. Orders ship from **US warehouses**; returns go to **Rancho Cucamonga, California**. Its products are also sold on Amazon and Walmart.

This review is based on BodegaCooler's product pages, policies, on-site reviews and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested BodegaCooler fridges ourselves.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** **4.59–4.84/5** across the top 5, from **86 to 264 reviews** per product. Several reviewers praise support that replaced parts or units.
- **Trustpilot:** **2.6/5 from only 16 reviews** on a claimed profile listed in China: **25% 5-star, 69% 1-star**. Complaints mention unanswered emails and calls, hard warranty and return claims, products failing early or arriving damaged, and a misleading spin-to-win discount.

That's a big gap. The Trustpilot sample is small, but it's the only independent rating we found.

## Shipping

- **Free standard shipping** in the mainland US from US warehouses; product pages say orders process in 1–3 business days and arrive in 3–7.
- **Canada, Germany, Italy:** +$35, 8–15 business days. **UK:** +$60, 8–15 business days.
- **No PO, APO or FPO** addresses.

## Returns, cancellations and warranty

- **Cancel within 24 hours.** A 2% payment fee may apply; if the order already shipped, double shipping is deducted.
- **Returns within 30 days of purchase**, with a Return Authorization by email. Items must be **unused, uninstalled** and in the **original packaging**; returns without the box aren't accepted.
- **Change of mind:** you pay **shipping both ways**. **Defective:** you pay return shipping first and BodegaCooler reimburses it.
- **Exchanges:** within 30 days of delivery, with a **restocking fee and shipping fee**; the policy doesn't state the restocking amount.
- **Warranty:** **24 months** on the compressor and accessories, **+1 year** if you register within 10 days. Only purchases from bodegacooler.com qualify; Amazon and Walmart orders go through those stores.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** campers, overlanders and RV owners who want an affordable dual-zone compressor fridge and are happy to register the warranty and keep the box.
- **Think twice:** if smooth customer service matters most to you, or you might change your mind (shipping a 30–57 lb fridge both ways is expensive).
