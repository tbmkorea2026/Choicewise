NailSala sells the pieces you need to do **gel nails at home**: colour, a lamp to cure it, and tools to take it off. Prices are low and shipping is free. ## Things that don't add up

- **Copied rating lines:** several descriptions say "Rated 4.8/5 by thousands", while the review widgets show 80–229 reviews per product.
- **Lamp details:** the Mini UV LED lamp page says "not rechargeable", but its main image is labelled "Rechargeable" and "Cordless", and one variant is called "Cordless Lamp". Check which version you're buying.
- **"Does not harm eyes and skin":** the same lamp page claims this for a 365+405 nm UV/LED light. UV nail lamps do emit UV; protect your hands.
- **Resold brands:** the "Professional Nail Lamp" is a SUNUV model (SUN X5 Plus / X11 Max).
- **"No harsh chemicals"** on the remover, with no ingredient list.

## Safety for at-home gel nails

- Keep gel off your skin and cuticles; repeated contact can cause **allergic reactions** to gel ingredients. Stop if you get itching, redness or swelling around the nails.
- Cure fully under the lamp; under-cured gel is more likely to irritate skin.
- Use **fingerless UV gloves or sunscreen** on your hands during curing.
- Use removers in a ventilated room and keep them away from eyes.
