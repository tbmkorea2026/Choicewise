## Overview

The **SolarCam D1 Classic Kit** is first on aosu's best-selling list. It bundles two, four or six solar pan-and-tilt cameras with an **aosuBase** hub that stores video locally, so there's **no required subscription**.

This review is based on aosu's product page, specs, FAQ, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the kit ourselves. For the brand overview, see our [aosu review](/reviews/aosu-review/).

## Cameras

- **2K (2560×1440, 3MP)** video; the 6-cam kit option is labeled 3K.
- **360° pan and tilt** with **auto tracking**, and **6x digital zoom**.
- **Color night vision** up to **33 ft**.
- **Two-way talk** with preset messages and voice disguise, **IP65** weatherproofing.
- Works with **Alexa, Google Assistant and SmartThings**.

## aosuBase and storage

- The base has **32GB** of storage and supports up to **4 cameras** (aosuBase 2.0 supports 6).
- It **extends Wi-Fi** to the cameras and powers **cross-camera tracking**, merging clips of the same event.
- aosu estimates **up to 120 days** of motion events on a 2-cam kit and **up to 60 days** on a 4-cam kit.
- One store reviewer reported having to clear storage manually, despite aosu describing it as loop recording.

## Solar and battery

The cameras run on built-in batteries topped up by solar panels. aosu says they charge in low light and partial shade, but **only above 14°F (-10°C)**. Battery life is the most common complaint about aosu on Trustpilot, so check panel placement during the 30-day return window.

## Price, returns and warranty

- **2-cam $299.99**, **4-cam $369.99**, **6-cam $499.99** (with an upgraded base), plus a G11 suite option.
- **Free shipping**, 2–5 business days in the US.
- **30-day returns** for any reason, but **you pay return shipping**.
- **Warranty:** 12 months.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone covering a yard, driveway or outbuilding without outdoor power who wants local storage and no fees.
- **Think twice:** if you need 24/7 recording, live with long freezing winters, or want more than 2K detail (see the T2 models).

## The bottom line

The D1 Classic Kit packs pan-and-tilt solar cameras and subscription-free local storage into an affordable kit, which explains its popularity. Battery-based event recording and a short warranty are the main limits. Our verdict: **7.4/10**.
