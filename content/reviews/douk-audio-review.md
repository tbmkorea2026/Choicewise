## Overview

**Douk Audio** sells budget hi-fi gear direct from its own store, alongside its sister brands **Nobsound** and **Little Bear**. According to its About page, the team launched Nobsound's NS-01G mini amp in 2013, then started Douk Audio and built its own manufacturing. The store sells **class D and tube amplifiers, preamps, VU meters, speaker and amp switchers, USB digital interfaces** and power supplies, plus a collaboration brand, **Ampapa**.

This review is based on Douk Audio's product pages, policies and store ratings as of October 2026. We haven't tested Douk Audio gear ourselves.

## Prices

Douk Audio is a budget brand. Its five best sellers range from **$69.99** (U2 PRO digital interface) to **$199.99** (Ampapa D1 amp). Several amps come in **two power-supply versions**: the A5 is $89.99 with a 32V/5A adapter or $109.99 with a 48V/5A adapter, and the bigger adapter gives noticeably more power (see below).

## About those power numbers

Many Douk Audio amps are headlined at **"300W×2"**. That's the chip's **maximum** output. Douk's own power tables are more useful: the A5 delivers **78W per channel into 4 ohms with the included 32V/5A adapter**, **107W with a 36V/6A** supply and **120W with a 48V/5A** supply. That's still plenty for typical bookshelf speakers, but compare the rated numbers, not the headline, when you shop.

## Ratings

Store reviews (Judge.me) are good across the best sellers: **4.4 to 4.8 out of 5**, with **17 to 53 reviews** per product. Douk's **Trustpilot** profile is unclaimed and has only **two reviews** (both 5-star, TrustScore 3.8), so there's little independent feedback on the store itself.

## Shipping

- Ships to **nearly 200 countries** from multiple warehouses.
- Orders are usually processed by the **next business day**.
- International delivery is typically **2–8 business days**.
- **Duties, taxes and customs fees are included** at checkout unless otherwise noted.
- Douk isn't responsible for packages marked delivered without third-party confirmation, so give a complete address.

## Returns and warranty

- **Returns:** request within **30 days of delivery**; returns need **approval first** and must ship back **within 7 days**. Items must be complete, in original packaging and in good condition, or you may get a **partial refund**.
- **Fees:** original shipping, duties and taxes aren't refunded unless Douk made an error. The returns page doesn't say who pays return shipping on change-of-mind returns; an older refund page lists "don't like the item" as outside free after-sale service, so plan to pay it.
- **Warranty:** the **24-month warranty** page covers manufacturing defects with free repair or replacement and **free two-way shipping**. Accidental damage, DIY modifications and misuse aren't covered. The older refund page still says **1-year warranty**, which is inconsistent.
- **Purchases elsewhere** (Amazon, dealers) follow that seller's policy.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone building an affordable stereo with passive speakers, adding Bluetooth or tubes to a desk or turntable setup, or who enjoys swapping op-amps and tubes.
- **Think twice:** if you want a big-brand dealer network, local returns, or official specs measured by a third party.

## The bottom line

Douk Audio offers a lot of hi-fi features for the money, with good store ratings, listed company details, duties included and a 24-month warranty page. Read power ratings carefully, and know that change-of-mind returns likely cost you shipping. Our verdict: **7.6/10**.
