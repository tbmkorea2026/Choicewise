## Overview

The **Snailax Shiatsu Neck & Shoulder Massager (SL-632)** is the #1 best seller in Snailax's store. It's a U-shaped massage pillow you rest over your neck and shoulders. Inside are **8 rotating 3D shiatsu nodes** that knead deeply, with **optional heat**. You slip your arms through the straps and pull to press the nodes deeper, or relax your arms for a lighter massage.

This review is based on Snailax's product page, its policies and customer ratings as of October 2026. We haven't used the massager ourselves.

## What you get

- **8 shiatsu nodes** with heat (switchable).
- **Arm straps** for hands-free use and pressure control.
- **15-minute auto shut-off.**
- **Home AC adapter and car charger**, so you can use it at home, in the office or in a parked car.
- Simple button controls.

Snailax doesn't list the weight, dimensions or heat temperature on the product page.

## Where you can use it

Snailax says the SL-632 works on:

- **Neck and shoulders**, the main use.
- **Upper and lower back**, wedged between you and a chair.
- **Calves and legs**, placed on the floor.
- **Feet**, while you're seated.

That flexibility is a big part of its appeal compared with chair pads, which only do your back.

## Safety

- Keep sessions to about **15–20 minutes** (it switches off at 15) and **stop if it feels too intense** or painful.
- Don't use heat on numb or broken skin, and don't fall asleep with it on.
- Talk to a doctor first if you're pregnant, have a pacemaker or implant, a recent neck injury or a condition that affects how you feel heat.
- Snailax's page calls it "professional-level" pain relief. Treat that as marketing: it's a comfort device, not a treatment.
