## Overview

The **NIU KQi 300X** is the first scooter with stock in NIU Canada's best-selling sort. It's NIU's all-terrain commuter: a big, heavy scooter with **front suspension**, **wide 10.5-inch tubeless tires**, **wider handlebars and deck** than NIU's smaller models, and **1000 W of max power**. It sells for **C$799**, down from C$1,399, in Space Gray or Black.

This review is based on NIU Canada's product page, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the KQi 300X ourselves. Power, range and climbing figures are NIU's.

## Safety first

- **Check local rules:** e-scooter rules vary by province and city in Canada, and NIU says top speed varies by provincial regulation.
- **Wear a helmet** and use the turn signals and lights.
- **Expect less range in the cold:** lithium batteries lose capacity in winter.

## Key features

- **Power:** **1000 W max**, FOC sine-wave controller, **25% hill grade** claim.
- **Range:** up to **60 km** per charge from a **13 Ah** battery (claimed).
- **Comfort:** **all-terrain front suspension** and **10.5" × 2.5" self-healing tubeless tires**.
- **Brakes:** **front and rear disc brakes** plus a **rear electric brake**.
- **Lights:** Halo headlight, brake lights and **turn signals**.
- **App:** Bluetooth lock, ride stats, speed settings, **cruise control** and a **charge limit** to extend battery life.
- **Size:** **22.1 kg**; handlebar 21.3" wide, deck 6.47" × 22.4".

## What to check first

1. **Weight:** at 22.1 kg it's a lot to carry. Folding helps, but stairs will be hard.
2. **Speed:** capped at **25 km/h**. If you want more speed and much less weight, look at the [KQi Air](/best/niu-ca-kqi-air-vs-kqi-300x/), which lists 32 km/h.
3. **Local rules:** make sure e-scooters are allowed where you'll ride.

## Ratings

The KQi 300X is rated **4.5/5 from 11 reviews** on NIU Canada's store: 9 five-star, 1 four-star and 1 one-star. That's a small sample. NIU as a company scores **1.4 on Trustpilot** from 560 reviews, 85% 1-star, mostly about failures, range shortfalls and slow after-sales service.

## Price

**C$799** (was C$1,399) with free shipping from Montreal, 3–7 business days. GST or provincial tax may apply. We don't have a discount code for NIU Canada.

## Warranty and returns

**2 years** on the main body, including battery, charger, motor, controller, frame and dashboard. **6 months** on accessories and **3 months** on consumables. Change-of-mind returns are **free within 30 days** if the scooter has **10 km or less** on it, so test it straight away. See our [NIU Canada review](/reviews/niu-ca-review/) for details.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** commuters on rough or hilly roads who want suspension, strong brakes and turn signals, and don't need to carry the scooter far.
- **Think twice:** anyone who climbs stairs with their scooter, riders who want more than 25 km/h, or buyers worried about NIU's after-sales record.

## The bottom line

At **C$799**, the KQi 300X packs suspension, big tubeless tires, triple braking and 1000 W of power for a fair price. The weight and NIU's poor service reputation are the trade-offs. Use the 10 km free-return window to make sure it suits you. Our verdict: **7.3/10**.
