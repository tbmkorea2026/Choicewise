## Safety first

Massagers are generally simple to use, but they aren't medical devices you should rely on for treatment:

- Snailax's own product pages say to **limit sessions to about 15–20 minutes**, **stop if the massage feels too intense**, and **talk to your doctor first if you have a medical condition**.
- Many models have a **15-minute auto shut-off** and **overheat protection** (per Snailax).
- As general advice: if you're pregnant, have a pacemaker or other implant, a recent injury or surgery, or a condition like diabetes that affects how you feel heat, ask a healthcare professional before using any heated massager.
- Product pages describe the massagers as "medical-grade" and good for pain or arthritis relief. These are **marketing claims** we can't verify; treat a massager as a comfort device, not a cure.

## Returns and warranty: read carefully

**Returns (per Snailax's policies):**

- **Days 0–30:** refund for most products if returned **unused and resellable, in the original packaging** with all accessories. The 30-day no-reason return applies only to the **contiguous US**.
- **Days 31–90:** refund only for a **defect Snailax can't repair or replace**; it may choose repair or replacement instead. Snailax pays shipping for verified defects or wrong items within 90 days.
- **Heavy or oversized items** (Snailax says these are marked on the product page): you **prepay return freight** for change-of-mind returns.
- A **15% restocking fee** may apply if the item isn't resellable or a delivery was refused.

**Where the policies disagree:**

- The **warranty page** counts 30 days from **delivery**; the **refund page** counts from **purchase**.
- The **shipping page** says Snailax **pays return shipping on change-of-mind returns** for standard items; the **refund page** says the **customer pays** for change-of-mind returns.

Ask support to confirm in writing before you send anything back.

**Warranty:** **12 months** on all products and accessories, extendable to **24 months for the main unit** if you **register within 30 days** of purchase. Claims go to support@snailax.com with proof of purchase, photos or video and the serial number; Snailax issues an **RMA** first. After 90 days you pay shipping for warranty repairs. Normal wear (like fabric fraying), misuse and commercial use aren't covered.
