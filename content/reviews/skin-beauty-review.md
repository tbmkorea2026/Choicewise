## Overview

**Skin Beauty** (skin-beauty.com) is an online retailer specializing in **professional, spa and luxury skincare**. Based in **Everett, Washington**, it stocks hundreds of brands in one place, from clinical lines such as **Obagi, PCA Skin and EltaMD** to spa favorites like **Sothys and Eminence** and popular K-beauty names like **COSRX and Beauty of Joseon**.

This review explains what it is like to shop at Skin Beauty: the selection, prices, shipping and, most importantly, the return policy. It is based on the store's website and published policies as of October 2026. We could not independently verify customer review platforms for this store, so we focus on the facts Skin Beauty publishes.

## What can you buy at Skin Beauty?

The store's catalog is organized three ways, which makes it easy to browse:

- **By brand:** an A–Z list of several hundred brands, including professional lines that are not always easy to find online.
- **By concern:** acne, aging skin, dark circles, large pores, discoloration, sensitive skin and more.
- **By product type:** cleansers, toners, serums, moisturizers, eye products, masks and peels, sun care, body care and skincare kits.

There are also sections for **gift sets**, **new arrivals**, a **K-Beauty collection**, makeup and a **sale section with discounts of up to 60%**.

### What's popular

At the time of writing, Skin Beauty's best-selling skin care list is dominated by **Le Mieux**, led by the **Le Mieux Eye Wrinkle Corrector**, with **Sothys' Hydra Hyaluronic Acid⁴ Youth Creams**, **Skin Script**, **GlyMed Plus** and **Circadia** also near the top. See our guide to the [best Le Mieux products at Skin Beauty](/best/best-le-mieux-products/) and our [Le Mieux Eye Wrinkle Corrector review](/reviews/le-mieux-eye-wrinkle-corrector-review/).

## Prices, deals and rewards

Skin Beauty runs frequent promotions: sitewide and best-seller discounts, a **sale section**, **gift with purchase** offers and **free samples**. Registered members can **earn points** on qualifying orders and access promotions on exclusive brands. Newsletter subscribers are also offered a discount on their next order.

## Shipping

- **Free shipping on U.S. orders over $59.**
- Orders ship via carriers including USPS and FedEx, with tracking emailed once shipped.
- **International shipping** is available. Customs duties and taxes may apply depending on the destination.
- **Shipping insurance** is offered, and the store encourages selecting it: once tracking shows a package as delivered, Skin Beauty is generally not responsible for loss or theft.

## Returns: read this before you buy

Skin Beauty's return policy is **strict**, so it deserves a close look before you order:

- Returns must be requested within **14 days of delivery**.
- Products must be **unopened, unused** and in original condition with seals intact.
- A **15% restocking fee** applies, and **return shipping is at your cost**.
- On orders that shipped free, the **original shipping fee is deducted** from your refund.
- **No returns for allergic reactions.** Clearance items, heat-sensitive and perishable items and vitamins are final sale.

> **Tip:** Because opened products cannot be returned, even if you react to them, Skin Beauty is best for **restocking products you already know suit your skin**. For anything new, check the full ingredient list (Skin Beauty publishes them on product pages) and patch-test first.

## Customer support

Support is available by **live chat** (the fastest option) and phone from **9 AM to 4 PM Pacific time, Monday to Friday**, and by email. Skin Beauty also hosts a **skin Q&A section** where you can ask questions about products.

## Is Skin Beauty an authorized retailer?

Skin Beauty describes itself as an **authorized retailer** in its site title and lists **brand authenticity** among its reasons to shop there. Some professional skincare brands publish their own lists of authorized online sellers; if authenticity is especially important for a particular brand, it is worth checking that brand's list before ordering.

## Who should shop at Skin Beauty?

**A good fit if you:**

- Already know which professional or spa products work for you and want good prices and free shipping.
- Are looking for hard-to-find brands in one place.
- Like collecting rewards points, samples and gifts with purchase.

**Think twice if you:**

- Want to try a new product with the option to return it after opening.
- Have very reactive skin and prefer retailers that accept returns of opened items.

## The bottom line

Skin Beauty offers an impressive selection of professional and luxury skincare, regular deals and free U.S. shipping over $59. Its strict return rules, especially the 15% restocking fee and the no-returns-after-opening policy, are the main drawback. Use it to restock trusted favorites at a good price, and be more cautious when trying something new.
