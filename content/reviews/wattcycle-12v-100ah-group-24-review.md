## Overview

The **WattCycle 12V 100Ah Group 24** is first on WattCycle's best-selling list. It's a **LiFePO4** battery built to drop into a standard **Group 24** battery box in an RV, camper, boat or small solar setup.

## Capacity and power

- **12.8V, 100Ah, 1,280 Wh**, usable to **100% depth of discharge**, per WattCycle.
- **100A BMS:** 1,280 W continuous output and **300±50A** for up to 5 seconds, which helps with motor start-up loads.
- Rated **5,000+ cycles at 100% DOD**, 6,000+ at 80% and 15,000+ at 40%.

## Charging and temperature

- Charge at **14.4V ±0.2V**; WattCycle recommends **20A** (up to 100A).
- Charges between **32°F and 122°F**; discharges between **-4°F and 158°F**.
- Below **32°F** the BMS stops charging and recovers at about **41°F**. There's **no self-heating**, so winter campers may need a heated model or an insulated box.

## Build

- **10.2 × 6.6 × 8.2 in**, **23.0 lb**.
- **IP65**, flame-retardant ABS case, **M8** terminals.
- Expandable to **4S4P** for 24V or 48V banks and more capacity.
