## Design and features

### Two shorts in one

A **2-in-1 short** combines a loose outer short with a fitted inner compression liner. MIER says the liner is designed to support the thigh muscles, and many runners choose lined shorts to reduce inner-thigh chafing, while the outer short gives you coverage and a regular look for the gym or street.

### Pockets for everything

Storage is where these shorts stand out:

- **Phone pocket in the liner:** your phone sits in the liner against your thigh instead of in a loose outer pocket.
- **Two slant side pockets** on the outer short.
- **A small hidden pocket inside the right side pocket** for keys, cash or cards.

The one thing missing is a **zippered** pocket on the outer short. If you want zips, see the [PaceLite Linerless 5"](/best/mier-sprintdry-2-in-1-vs-pacelite-linerless/).

### Fabric and fit

The outer short uses MIER's **SprintDry™** quick-dry, moisture-wicking fabric. The **liner is a 4-way stretch fabric with 11% spandex**, with mesh air vents throughout for breathability. An **elastic waistband with an internal drawstring** lets you fine-tune the fit, and a **side-split hem** adds freedom of movement. MIER also lists the fabric as **water-resistant** with a **color fastness rating of 4**, and the shorts are **machine washable**.

A nice style detail: the top of the waistband matches the liner color, giving each colorway a two-tone look.

## Sizing and colors

The shorts come in **XS to 3XL** and **10 color combinations**. Use MIER's size chart on the product page. Since compression liners are designed to fit snugly, check the waist and thigh measurements carefully if you are between sizes.

## Price and value

At **$32.99**, the SprintDry 2-in-1 is affordable for a short with this feature set: a compression liner, phone storage and quick-dry fabric. Add MIER's **free shipping over $60** (two pairs qualify) and **free 30-day returns**, and the value is hard to argue with.
