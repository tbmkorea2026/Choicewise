## Laser safety first

The modules covered here put out **20W to 60W** of blue 450 nm light. Lasers of this kind are Class 4 under the international classification, which means a direct or reflected beam can cause instant eye injury and burns, and can start fires.

- Wear **goggles rated for 450 nm** every time, even if the module has a filter window. One LaserTree page says you can watch through the window "without goggles"; we don't recommend relying on that.
- Use an **enclosure** and **fume extraction**. Never cut **PVC or vinyl**, which release toxic, corrosive chlorine gas when burned.
- **Never leave a job running** unattended and keep an extinguisher nearby.
- Check local rules before buying high-power or invisible infrared lasers.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** reviews are **few**. The K30 has 13, the K40 one, and the K60 and K20 Pro none.
- **Trustpilot:** **4.2 from 15 reviews**, 73% 5-star and 27% 1-star, on an unclaimed profile. Praise centers on patient technical support and quick replacements. Complaints describe modules failing after short use, emails going unanswered, and fees on shipping and returns.

## Shipping

- **Warehouses:** product pages list **China** (ships within 24 hours, about 5–8 days), the **US** (2–5 days) and **Poland** (1–5 days to most EU countries; not the UK).
- **Fees:** the shipping policy lists **$15 standard** (4PX) and **$35 expedited**, while some product pages mention free expedited shipping. Check at checkout.
- **Policy times:** US standard 7–14 business days, plus 2–3 days of processing.
- **Duties and taxes:** may apply, and LaserTree says it isn't responsible for them.
- **Not shipped to:** several countries, including Israel, Thailand and "South America".

## Returns and warranty

- **Returns:** **30 days**, unused and in original packaging, after emailing customer service. The policy can't decide whether the 30 days start at purchase, delivery or receipt.
- **Costs:** you pay return shipping for your own reasons. Shipping charges aren't refunded. The policy conflicts on who pays when LaserTree sends the wrong item.
- **Warranty conflict:** product pages say **one-year warranty**. The replacement policy covers **3 months** from receipt: free within 15 days, then a **15% fee** (15–30 days) or **20% fee** (30 days–3 months), plus return shipping. Return the item within 7 days of approval.
- Support emails use the **micost-optotech.com** domain.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** experienced hobbyists who want more cutting power for an existing engraver and already have an enclosure, goggles and ventilation.
- **Think twice:** beginners, anyone who needs a long, clear warranty, or buyers who'd rather have a complete machine with local support.

LaserTree publishes more data than many module sellers, and its K-series offers a lot of power for the money.
