## Overview

**Opatra** is a British beauty-technology brand selling **at-home LED, radio-frequency and massage devices**, skincare, oral care and professional salon equipment. According to its Our Story page, it has developed products since 2010, is headquartered in the UK with offices in Spain and Las Vegas, and trades in over 45 countries. Its legal details list **Opatra Limited, company no. 07184706**, at a London registered address, with returns going to Borehamwood.

This review covers prices, best sellers, ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on Opatra's store and policies as of October 2026, Trustpilot and news reports. We haven't used these devices ourselves.

## Read this first: the Hong Kong sales probe

In **August 2026**, Opatra's name hit the news in Hong Kong:

- A woman reported being pressured into spending about HK$60,000 at the Sha Tin branch, and a video of the incident spread online.
- **Customs arrested a financial manager and an administrative manager** of the retailer on suspicion of breaching the Trade Descriptions Ordinance ([TVB News](https://news.tvb.com/en/1190102-2OPATRALONDONmanagersarrestedoversuspectedunfairsalespractice)). A [saleswoman was arrested later](https://www.dimsumdaily.hk/saleswoman-arrested-over-alleged-coercive-sales-at-opatra%E2%80%91linked-chain-in-third-arrest/).
- At least **six complaints**, mostly from middle-aged or elderly people, involved HK$1,800 to HK$100,000, and the branches [suspended operations](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/340413/Two-arrested-as-Customs-probe-UK-brand-Opatra-London-local-retailer).
- **The company's position:** Opatra's UK head office says the stores are run by a Hong Kong distribution partner and that it will cooperate with the authorities. The distributor [denied misconduct](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/340254/) and said transactions were voluntary.

These are allegations. We found no reported convictions, and our most recent coverage dates from early September 2026. The case concerns physical stores in Hong Kong, not this online store, but one Trustpilot reviewer also described a sales experience as predatory. **Whichever channel you use, don't sign or pay under time pressure.** An online purchase lets you compare prices first.

## Safety and realistic expectations

- **Read the manual first.** Some product pages say contraindications are in the user manual but don't list them, so ask **info@opatra.com** before you buy if you're unsure.
- **Ask a doctor first** if you're pregnant, have implanted electronics, have a skin condition or take medicine that makes you sensitive to light. This is general caution, not Opatra's wording.
- **Protect your eyes** and follow the stated treatment times, especially with eye-area devices and LED lights.
- **Be skeptical of absolute claims.** The Synergy Eye page says "No side effects" and "Immediate results". We wouldn't rely on those statements.
- **Evidence:** Opatra says its formulas use "clinically trialed" ingredients, but the pages we read don't link to trial results for each product.

## Ratings: store vs Trustpilot

- **On Opatra's product pages:** scores of 4.8–5.0, but from very few reviews (1 to 18 on the best sellers above). Those samples are too small to judge a £2,999 device.
- **On Trustpilot:** **4.5/5 from 1,054 reviews**, with 86% 5-star, 3% 4-star, under 1% 3-star, 1% 2-star and **9% 1-star**. The profile is claimed, Opatra invites customers to review, and it has replied to 93% of negative reviews.
- **Recent praise** focuses on helpful staff, consultations and customer care.
- **Recent complaints** include devices failing, slow replacements, hard-to-reach support and a US customer's objection to a $56 warranty fee. One UK reviewer's slow replacement was resolved after a public review.

Many positive reviews mention staff, so they may reflect in-person sales rather than long-term device results.

## Shipping

- **Processing:** 1–3 business days (per Opatra's shipping policy), with tracking by email.
- **Free over £100** to the UK and listed European countries; **£25 under £100**. Delivery is stated as 2–5 business days.
- **US, Canada, Mexico and rest of world:** charges appear at checkout.
- **Duties and taxes** are the customer's responsibility. No shipping to P.O. boxes, Russia or Ukraine.

## Returns

Per Opatra's refund policy:

- **30 days from receipt**, only for **unused** items in **original packaging**, with proof of purchase.
- **You pay return shipping**, and it's non-refundable. Returns go to Borehamwood, UK.
- **Sale items can't be refunded.** Exchanges are only for defective or damaged items.
- **EU consumers** also have a legal 14-day right of withdrawal.

"Unused" matters here: you can't try a £2,999 device and then return it under the normal policy, though EU withdrawal rights allow limited handling to check an item.

## Warranty: "lifetime", with limits

Per Opatra's warranty policy (updated 24 March 2026):

- **First 12 months:** verified defects get a replacement, with a **shipping contribution fee**.
- **After 12 months:** a replacement costs **£40** (or euro equivalent) each time.
- **Conditions:** register the product, original purchaser only, proof of purchase, authorized sales channels.
- **Replacements may be new or refurbished.**
- **Territory:** valid in the **UK and EU**. If you're elsewhere, confirm your cover in writing before buying.
- **Excluded:** normal wear, cosmetic damage, consumables, misuse, water and power surges.

## Who should buy Opatra?

**A possible fit if you:**

- Are in the **UK or EU** and want a premium device with replacement cover.
- Have compared cheaper LED devices and still prefer Opatra's design and support.

**Think twice if you:**

- Are being sold to in person, or feel rushed by a limited-time offer.
- Live outside the UK and EU, and depend on the warranty.
- Expect proven results for the price, or want to try before committing.

Opatra is a real company with solid Trustpilot numbers and quick shipping, but its prices are steep, its warranty has fees and territory limits, and the Hong Kong probe is a serious concern even though Opatra says those stores were independently run.
