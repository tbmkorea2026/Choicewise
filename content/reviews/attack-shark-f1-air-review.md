## Overview

The **Attack Shark F1 AIR** is the #1 best seller on attackshark.com. It's an ultra-light **39 g** wireless mouse aimed at FPS players, combining a **PAW3955MAX** sensor, **Nordic 54L15** chip and **8000Hz** polling in a compact shell.

This review is based on Attack Shark's product page, policies and store ratings as of October 2026. We haven't used the F1 AIR ourselves.

## Key features

Per Attack Shark:

- **Sensor:** custom-tuned **PixArt PAW3955MAX**, up to **60,000 DPI** in 1-DPI steps, **20,000 FPS**, **5-level lift-off distance**.
- **MCU:** **Nordic 54L15**.
- **Connectivity:** **2.4GHz**, **Bluetooth** and **wired**, with **8000Hz** polling in both wired and wireless modes and click latency down to **0.168 ms** with the Shark 8K receiver.
- **Switches:** **Omron optical**, rated for **100 million clicks**; **TTC Gold** wheel encoder.
- **Shell:** high-density injection-molded shell with a glass-feel coating.
- **Battery:** **250 mAh**, up to **720 hours at 1000Hz** under test conditions.

**In the box:** mouse, 8K receiver, 8K USB-C cable and grip tape.

## Size and shape

At **116.1 × 61.6 × 38.0 mm**, the F1 AIR is **compact**. Attack Shark recommends it for **small to medium hands** (under about 19 cm). It's best for **claw and fingertip** grips. If you palm your mouse or have larger hands, consider the ergonomic **V2 AIR**.
