## Overview

**NikolaToy** is an online store selling science-themed models and desk gadgets. According to the brand, it was **founded in 2022** and focuses on designing and producing innovative model toys, and it says it has **over 20 million fans** across YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram. Its catalog of nearly 500 items ranges from working **Stirling engine** and **turbofan engine** models to metal fidget toys, glow tube clocks and RC models.

This review covers what is worth buying, prices, shipping and some important policy details, based on NikolaToy's website as of October 2026.

## What's worth a look

- **Stirling engine models:** small heat engines that run from a heat source, from a simple classic model to versions with generators, LEDs and voltmeters. See our [best NikolaToy Stirling engine models](/best/best-nikolatoy-stirling-engines/).
- **Turbofan engine models:** detailed replicas such as the 3D-printed **TR900**, sold assembled or as a DIY kit. Read our [TR900 turbofan review](/reviews/nikolatoy-tr900-turbofan-engine-model-review/).
- **Science desk gadgets:** noble gas glow tubes, a walnut RGB glow tube clock, magnetic hourglass planters and more.
- **Metal fidget toys:** EDC fidgets, including "Poking Lottery" packs where contents are random.

### What we don't cover

NikolaToy's catalog also includes items modeled on well-known film, comic and video-game characters. We could not find licensing information for these on the product pages, so we have chosen not to review them. The store also sells bows, slingshots and launcher-style toys, which are outside the scope of our reviews.

## Prices

At the time of writing, NikolaToy's Stirling engines start at about **$30** (classic model), with generator and voltmeter versions around **$50–$75**, and decorative models above **$100**. The TR900 turbofan model costs about **$86–$90**. Many items show discounts against higher "regular" prices.

Orders are processed in **1–2 business days**, and tracking can take up to 3 working days to appear.

## Returns and warranty: policies don't fully match

This is the area to be careful about:

- **Product pages** advertise a "**30-day risk-free return policy**" (items must be in original packaging and condition with a receipt), a **1-year warranty** and a **parts replacement service**.
- The **official refund policy page**, however, only covers **order cancellation**: you can cancel within **5 hours** of ordering, and after shipping, customers returning items for personal reasons pay **return shipping and any taxes or duties**. It does not spell out a return window.
- Some product pages also contain unfinished template text in their shipping information section.

> **Our advice:** before a larger purchase, email service@nikolatoy.com and ask them to confirm the return window and who pays return shipping. Keep the confirmation with your order.

## Company information

NikolaToy lists a mailing address in **Boulder, Colorado**, a China phone number, a U.S. SMS number and separate emails for after-sales support and wholesale. Beyond the brand's own description, we found little public information about the company.

## Who should buy from NikolaToy?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want an unusual gift for a science or engineering fan, especially a Stirling engine or turbofan model.
- Are comfortable buying from an online-only store with limited product details.

**Think twice if you:**

- Need clear, guaranteed return terms before buying.
- Want detailed specifications for every product.

## The bottom line

NikolaToy's Stirling engines, turbofan models and science gadgets are genuinely interesting and gift-worthy, and prices are reasonable. The weak points are thin product information and return terms that don't match across the site. Stick to well-described products, confirm the return policy in writing, and you are more likely to have a good experience.
