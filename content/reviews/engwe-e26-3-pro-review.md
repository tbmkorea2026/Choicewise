## Key specs

Per ENGWE:

- **Motor:** **250 W Mivice X700 mid-drive** (500 W peak), **100 Nm** torque.
- **Battery:** removable **LG 720 Wh** (48 V 15 Ah) with BMS protection; **4–5 hours** to charge with the 54.6 V 4 A charger.
- **Range:** **up to 135 km** in pedal assist.
- **Suspension:** **80 mm** adjustable fork with hydraulic lockout and **30 mm air rear suspension** on a **4-bar linkage**.
- **Brakes:** **180 mm hydraulic discs**.
- **Wheels:** **26 × 3.0-inch** all-road tires with reflective sidewalls.
- **Frame:** aluminum alloy, not foldable.
- **Weight and load:** **34 kg** (30.2 kg without battery), **150 kg** payload.
- **Certification:** **CE EN 15194 by TÜV**.

## Safety

Wear a helmet, set the air suspension for your weight and stay within the 150 kg payload. Check local rules for e-bikes on paths and roads.
