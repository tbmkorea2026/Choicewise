## Overview

The **NIIMBOT M2** is the #1 best seller on NIIMBOT's store. It's a small, rechargeable **thermal transfer** label maker: instead of heat-sensitive paper, it uses a **ribbon** to print sharp, durable labels at **300 dpi**. NIIMBOT markets it for many uses (product labels, jars, cables, office supplies, lab storage) and lists the same printer under several product pages.

This review is based on NIIMBOT's product pages, policies and store ratings as of October 2026. We haven't used the M2 ourselves.

## Key features

Per NIIMBOT:

- **300 dpi** thermal transfer printing for crisp text, barcodes and QR codes.
- **Durable labels:** waterproof, oil-, scratch-, heat-, cold- and sun-resistant; NIIMBOT claims **8–10 years**.
- **Color:** swap between **7 ribbon colors** and many label finishes (white, matte black, matte silver, holographic, clear and more).
- **Label sizes:** **20–50 mm** wide, minimum height 9 mm, with automatic positioning.
- **Battery:** **4 hours** of continuous work after **1.5 hours** of charging.
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth to phones (up to 3 iOS and 7 Android devices) and PC printing with a driver.
- **NIIMBOT app:** 20+ fonts, 1,000+ icons, 100+ borders, templates, barcodes, QR codes and batch printing.

**In the box:** the M2, one black ribbon and one roll of 50×30 mm white labels.

## Running costs

The M2 needs **two consumables**: NIIMBOT label rolls and **ribbons**. That makes each label cost more than on a direct-thermal printer like the [B21 Pro](/best/niimbot-m2-vs-b21-pro/), in exchange for durability and color. Bundles with two or three extra label rolls start at **$111.99**.

## Ratings

The M2 shows **4.9/5 from 343 reviews** on NIIMBOT's store, the most of any NIIMBOT printer. NIIMBOT's **Trustpilot** profile is weak (**2.8/5 from 10 reviews**), mostly about shipping and support rather than the printer; see our [NIIMBOT review](/reviews/niimbot-review/).

## Price and returns

- **Price:** **$99.99** with a black ribbon and one roll of labels.
- **Shipping:** NIIMBOT says 2–7 days for US orders on this model; free US shipping on the M2 and its labels at the time of writing.
- **Returns:** 30 days, unused and in original packaging, **at your cost**. Orders **can't be cancelled** once placed.
- **Defects after use:** within 30 days of arrival, returned at your expense for **store credit**. No warranty period is stated on the site.

## Alternatives

- **NIIMBOT B21 Pro** ($69.99): 300 dpi direct thermal, no ribbon, also prints photo labels. See [M2 vs B21 Pro](/best/niimbot-m2-vs-b21-pro/).
- **NIIMBOT M3** ($169.99): thermal transfer with wider 20–78 mm labels.
- **NIIMBOT B1** ($26.99): the cheapest way into NIIMBOT's ecosystem.

All five are compared in [best NIIMBOT label makers](/best/best-niimbot-label-makers/).

## The bottom line

The NIIMBOT M2 is a capable, well-reviewed **thermal transfer** label maker for **$99.99**: 300 dpi, durable labels and colored ribbons in a phone-friendly package. It's the one to get if your labels need to look professional and last. Factor in ribbon costs, the 2-inch width limit and the store's strict return rules. Our verdict: **7.6/10**.
