## Overview

If your speakers sound a little closed-in or dull at the top end, replacing them is the expensive fix. A **super tweeter** is the cheap one. Aperion Audio, an Oregon-based direct-to-consumer speaker brand founded in 1998, has built a following around its super tweeter add-ons. The **PST Planar-Magnetic Ribbon Super Tweeter** is the entry point to that range and currently Aperion's **best-selling product**.

This review draws on Aperion's published specifications and manual, the design of the driver and crossover, and feedback from verified owners (the PST holds a **4.9/5 rating from 145 reviews** on Aperion's site at the time of writing).

## What is a super tweeter?

A super tweeter is an extra high-frequency speaker that sits on top of your existing bookshelf or tower speakers. Many speakers roll off or narrow their dispersion in the highest octaves. A super tweeter takes over in that range to add what listeners usually call **"air"**: more space around instruments, crisper cymbals and a wider, deeper soundstage.

The PST works from 8 kHz up to **40 kHz**, well beyond the roughly 20 kHz limit of a typical tweeter. Most of the audible benefit comes from the overlap region you choose with the crossover, where the ribbon adds detail and wider dispersion to what your speaker already plays.

## Design and build quality

The PST uses a **planar-magnetic "flat ribbon" driver**: a thin Kapton film with an aluminium conductor pattern, suspended in a magnetic field. Ribbon drivers are prized for their low moving mass, which lets them respond quickly and sound detailed and smooth.

The crossover uses non-inductive resistors and film capacitors, and Aperion designs it for a **linear phase response** to keep timing coherent with your main speakers. Each unit sits in a compact, curved cabinet (about 4 × 5.3 × 5 inches) with **5-way gold-plated binding posts**, available in Gloss Black, Pure White, Stealth Black or Gloss Cherry.

## Setup and adjustability

Setup is the PST's biggest strength. There is no extra amplifier and no complicated wiring: you **connect it in parallel to the same terminals as your speaker**, and it draws from the power already going to that speaker.

Two controls on the back let you tune the blend:

- **Crossover:** 8, 10, 12, 14 or 16 kHz, or OFF. Lower settings overlap more with your existing tweeter for a more obvious effect; higher settings add only the very top end.
- **Treble adjust:** 0 to −5 dB, to keep things balanced with bright speakers or reflective rooms.

> **Tip:** Start at 14 kHz with the treble at −2 dB, then move one step at a time. If the sound becomes sharp or fatiguing, raise the crossover point or lower the level. The best setting is usually the one where you miss it when you switch it OFF, rather than one where you obviously hear it.

The PST is designed for main speakers with **85–94 dB sensitivity**, which covers the majority of home bookshelf and tower speakers. Check your speaker's spec sheet before ordering.

## Sound: what to expect

With a good match, owners consistently report a more **open, spacious presentation**: clearer cymbals and strings, more precise imaging and a soundstage that extends beyond the speakers. Vinyl, SACD, FLAC and hi-res streaming listeners tend to get the most out of it.

It is worth keeping expectations realistic. A super tweeter refines a system rather than transforming it. If your speakers already use a ribbon or AMT tweeter, or are voiced on the bright side, the improvement will be smaller. That is why the in-home audition matters.

## Who should buy the Aperion PST?

**Buy it if:**

- You like your speakers but want more detail and air without replacing them.
- You listen to vinyl or hi-res music on a stereo or home theater system.
- You want to try a super tweeter without spending $650–$1,000.

**Skip it if:**

- Your speakers are very efficient (98 dB and above). Aperion's HST is built for those.
- You have a large room or high-end system and want maximum output, in which case the **RST** ($649) or dual-firing **DST** ($999) are better fits.
