## Overview

The **Enabot EBO SE 2 FamilyBot** is the #1 best seller in Enabot's store and the successor to the popular EBO SE. It's a small, round robot camera you **drive around your home from your phone**. You can watch your pets in 2K, talk to them, record around the clock and play with your cat using a **built-in laser**. It comes in "Sunshine Eyes" and "Starlight Eyes" styles.

This review is based on Enabot's product page, policies and public ratings as of October 2026. We haven't used the EBO SE 2 ourselves.

## Key features

Per Enabot:

- **2K camera** (2304×1296) with a 137° view, night vision and built-in filters for photos and videos.
- **Whole-home mobility:** drive it from the app; obstacle avoidance; 9 cm ground clearance to roll under low furniture.
- **Home monitoring:** 24/7 recording, event recording, motion alerts, event history and playback.
- **Pet play:** built-in laser, feather toy, playful sounds and **Pet Fun Patrol**, which plays laser games during a patrol and records your pet's reactions.
- **Two-way audio** and **animated eyes**.
- **Auto-recharge:** returns to its dock using infrared guidance and route memory.
- **Self-righting design** if it gets knocked over.
- **Wi-Fi 6** on 2.4 and 5 GHz.

The low obstacle and slope limits mean it's best on **hard floors and thin rugs**. It won't climb stairs, and thick carpet or tall door thresholds can stop it.

## EBO SE 2 vs EBO SE

The original **EBO SE** ($139) has a 1080p camera, standard 2.4/5 GHz Wi-Fi, simple indicator lights and no laser. The SE 2 adds **2K video**, **Wi-Fi 6**, **animated eyes**, the **laser and feather toy**, and **more accurate docking**, per Enabot. For about $31 more, the SE 2 is the better buy for cat owners.

## Ratings

The EBO SE 2 is new and had **no reviews** on Enabot's store when we checked. Enabot's **Trustpilot** score is **3.3/5 from 74 reviews**: buyers like the camera and features, but some report motors failing and slow returns. See our [Enabot review](/reviews/enabot-review/).

## Safety and privacy

- Don't shine the **laser** into anyone's eyes, and finish play with a toy or treat so your cat can "win".
- Use a strong app password and keep firmware updated; Enabot says video is sent encrypted.
- Keep it away from stairs and water.

## Price, coupon and returns

- **Price:** **$169.99**. Code **TRANTRUNG** takes **10% off** at the time of writing, bringing it to about **$153**.
- **Shipping:** free with taxes and duties included to the US, Canada, Europe, Asia, Australia and New Zealand; US delivery 5–15 days.
- **Returns:** 14 days for any reason, but you pay return shipping and it must show no signs of use. Faulty units can be returned within 7 days at Enabot's cost.
- **Warranty:** 1 year (2 years in Europe), country of purchase only.

## The bottom line

The EBO SE 2 brings Enabot's best pet features (laser, 2K camera, auto-recharge) to a **$169.99** price, with an SD card included and no required subscription. It's a clear upgrade over the original EBO SE. Its small size limits it to flat floors, and there's no store feedback yet, but for a cat owner who wants to check in and play remotely, it's a good-value pick. Our verdict: **7.2/10**.
