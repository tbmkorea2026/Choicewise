## Overview

**aosu** (styled lowercase) sells **wire-free security cameras** built around solar charging and a home hub called **aosuBase**, which stores recordings locally so you don't need a cloud plan. It also sells indoor pan-and-tilt cameras, floodlight cameras and battery video doorbells. Trustpilot lists the company in **Singapore**; US support runs by email and phone.

This review is based on aosu's product pages, policies, store reviews and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested aosu cameras ourselves.

## How aosu systems work

- **Kits** pair solar cameras with an **aosuBase** (32GB) that stores motion clips, extends Wi-Fi and powers **cross-camera tracking**, which merges clips of the same person moving between cameras.
- **No monthly fee is required** with a base. Cloud storage is optional.
- **Standalone cameras** (sold without a base) need a **microSD card** (not included) or a cloud plan.
- The solar cameras **record events, not 24/7 video**, and aosu says charging only works **above -10°C (14°F)**.

## Prices

Product pages always show big "regular" prices next to the sale price, so judge the actual price. Best sellers run from **$49.99** for the IndoorCam P1 Pro to **$599.99** for a 4-camera T2 Ultra kit. Early Black Friday banners were running when we checked. We don't have an exclusive code for aosu.

## Ratings: a big gap

- **On the store:** every best seller shows **4.9/5**, from 141 to 1,093 reviews. Many reviews praise easy setup, clear video and responsive support.
- **On Trustpilot:** **2.7/5 from 148 reviews** on a claimed profile: **33% 5-star, 48% 1-star**. Complaints mention **short battery life**, **solar panels or batteries failing early**, **missed or unloadable events**, **Wi-Fi problems**, **hard returns and refunds** and an AI feature in the app that can't be turned off. One reviewer alleges negative reviews were removed.

We can't tell which picture is more accurate, but the gap is large enough to plan for the return window.

## Shipping

- **Free** to the US, Canada, the UK, Germany, France and Italy (the policy also lists Poland, Spain and Japan transit times).
- **1–2 business days** processing; **2–5 business days** to the US and Canada.
- Prices include import tariffs to listed countries. **No PO boxes.**

## Returns and warranty

- **Cancel within 24 hours** of paying; shipped orders can't be canceled.
- **30-day money-back guarantee** from receipt, **including change of mind**. Email support first, keep the box and all accessories.
- **You pay return shipping**; returns sent without approval or without prepaid postage are rejected.
- **Refunds:** 3–5 business days after approval.
- **Warranty:** **12 months** (24 months in the EEA).
- Product pages advertise "24/7 customer service", but the return policy lists phone support Mon–Fri 9–5 PST.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** homeowners who want solar cameras with local storage and no subscription, and who will test the system within 30 days.
- **Think twice:** if you need 24/7 recording, live somewhere with long, dark winters, or want a long warranty.

## The bottom line

aosu's kits are well equipped for the price, especially the no-subscription local storage and cross-camera tracking. But the gulf between 4.9 on-site ratings and 2.7 on Trustpilot, plus a short warranty and buyer-paid returns, means you should treat the first 30 days as a real trial. Our verdict: **6.6/10**.
