## Overview

**7Artisans** is a Chinese optics company that has become one of the best-known names in affordable mirrorless lenses. According to the company, it began with a **2015 dinner in Shenzhen** between an optical designer, a factory manager and a Leica collector. Officially **founded in 2016**, it was named after the **seven founders** who saw the project through. Its first product was a **50mm F1.1**, released as a 100-unit engineering edition in September 2016.

Today 7Artisans designs and manufactures a broad catalog of lenses for most mirrorless systems. This review looks at the range, prices, and what it is like to buy from the official store, based on 7Artisans' website and policies as of October 2026.

## What we like

### Exceptional value

7Artisans' biggest strength is price. For example, at the time of writing, its AF 25mm, 35mm and 50mm F1.8 APS-C autofocus primes cost about **$106 each**, the 7.5mm f/2.8 Mark II fisheye about **$118**, and the 50mm f/0.95 portrait lens about **$149**. First-party lenses from camera makers in similar focal lengths are typically priced considerably higher.

### Variety and creative options

Few brands offer such a wide mix: ultra-bright f/0.95 primes, fisheyes, tilt lenses, 2× macro lenses and full cine sets. That makes 7Artisans a good place to experiment without spending much.

### Autofocus line-up

7Artisans' newer AF lenses use STM motors and, according to the company, work with cameras' face and eye detection. The store also offers **firmware upgrades** and calibration profiles.

### Local warehouses

Orders to the **US, EU and Brazil** ship from local warehouses where stock allows, with **duties and taxes included**, which removes a common headache when buying from overseas brands.

## What to watch out for

- **Warranty:** a **one-year limited warranty** on all products. You pay shipping to send a lens in; 7Artisans pays for the return.
- **Returns:** requests within **14 days**, for unused items in original packaging. Returns not caused by a quality issue carry a **5% handling fee**, and you pay return shipping.
- **Manual focus:** many lenses are fully manual with no electronic contacts, so no autofocus or EXIF data. Great for some shooters, frustrating for others.
- **Shipping:** free only over **$150**. Orders not shipped from a local warehouse can take longer; the store's FAQ quotes about **7–25 days** for delivery.

## Who should buy 7Artisans lenses?

**A great fit if you:**

- Want to expand your lens kit on a tight budget.
- Enjoy manual focus or creative looks such as fisheye, extreme bokeh or tilt effects.
- Shoot video and want affordable cine lenses.

**Consider first-party lenses if you:**

- Need the most advanced autofocus performance or a longer warranty.
- Shoot professionally and rely on manufacturer repair networks.

## The bottom line

7Artisans offers outstanding value and one of the most creative lens catalogs on the market, now with a solid range of autofocus options. Go in with realistic expectations about the shorter warranty and return window, choose the correct mount, and you will get a lot of lens for your money.
