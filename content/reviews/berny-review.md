## Overview

**Berny** (styled BERNY) is a budget watch brand based in Shenzhen, China. Its site lists **BERNY HK CO LIMITED** in Hong Kong and **Shenzhen BERNY Industrial Co., Ltd.**, and it also runs official stores on **Amazon, AliExpress and TikTok Shop**. The range covers small **gold-tone women's quartz watches**, men's **automatic dive watches**, **titanium field watches** and watches with a **custom dial**.

This review covers prices, best sellers, specs, ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on Berny's store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't worn these watches ourselves.

## What you get for the money

Per Berny's product pages, its best sellers offer:

- **Japanese movements:** Miyota 8215 and Seiko NH35 automatics, and Miyota/Japanese quartz.
- **Sapphire crystals** on the divers and field watches; mineral glass on the women's dress watches.
- **200 m (20 ATM)** water resistance on the automatic divers, 50 m on the titanium field watch, 30 m on the dress watches.
- **Titanium cases** on the field watches (the T2596M weighs a listed 37 g).
- **"Super" luminous** markers on the sports watches.
- **Custom dials and casebacks** on some models (your own logo, name or design; no other brands' logos).

Berny shows strike-through "compare at" prices (for example $299.90 next to the AM339M's $139.99). Judge the actual price, not the discount.

**Watch out for fakes:** Berny says counterfeit websites impersonate it and that its only official site is **bernywatch.com**.

## Ratings: very few independent reviews

**Trustpilot** shows **3.6/5 from just 14 reviews**: 50% 5-star and 29% 1-star. Positive reviews mention **design, value and accurate timekeeping**; negative ones describe **slow or unconfirmed refunds**, an unanswered support request and a movement problem after seven months. Berny replies to negative reviews, but Trustpilot says replies often take over a month. Fourteen reviews is too few to judge a brand.

## Shipping

- Berny's banner promises **free shipping** and "fast delivery", but its shipping policy **doesn't state delivery times**.
- It ships to **P.O. boxes via USPS** (no expedited shipping to P.O. boxes).
- **International orders:** you pay **import taxes and duties** on top of the price.
- Double-check your address: Berny isn't responsible for parcels lost due to address errors.

## Returns and warranty: read carefully

- **Change of mind:** return within **30 days** of delivery; **you pay return shipping**. The watch must be unused, in original condition with all packaging, tags and gifts.
- **Defects, damage or wrong item:** you must **report it within 24 hours of delivery**. After that, Berny says it will consider the product in perfect condition. **Inspect your watch the day it arrives.**
- **Not returnable:** **customized watches**, items from non-returnable promotions, products bought elsewhere, and used or third-party-repaired watches.
- **No exchanges:** place a new order and return the first.
- Refunds can be reduced if you used a bundle or free-shipping promotion, and duties charged to Berny are deducted.
- **Warranty:** **12 months** from purchase for manufacturing defects; paid repairs after that. Battery replacement, scratches and accidents aren't covered.

## Who should buy Berny?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want a **pretty gold dress watch** or a **Japanese-movement automatic** for well under $150.
- Like the idea of a **custom-dial watch** as a gift.
- Will **inspect the watch on delivery day** and keep the box for 30 days.

**Think twice if you:**

- Might not check your parcel straight away (the 24-hour defect window is very short).
- Want an easy exchange for a different size or colour.
- Live outside the US and don't want to pay import duties.

## The bottom line

Berny offers a lot of watch for the money: Miyota and Seiko automatics, sapphire crystals and titanium cases at budget prices, plus inexpensive gold dress watches. But its return rules are strict (especially the 24-hour defect window), independent feedback is thin and the discounts are presented with inflated list prices. Buy from **bernywatch.com** or Berny's official marketplace stores, use code **GOODVICUA** for 10% off, and check your watch the day it arrives. Overall: **6.6/10**.
