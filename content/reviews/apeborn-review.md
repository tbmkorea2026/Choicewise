## Safety first

EMS isn't suitable for everyone. Don't use it if you have a **pacemaker or other implanted electronic device**, are **pregnant**, have **epilepsy** or a **heart condition**, or on broken, irritated or numb skin, and never across the chest or head. Start at a low intensity, and talk to a doctor first if you have any health condition.
