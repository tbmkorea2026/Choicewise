## In one minute

BUCKTOOL is a budget brand of **benchtop workshop machines**: grinders for sharpening, belt and disc sanders for shaping metal and wood, and dust collectors. Prices are well below premium brands and the warranty is a solid **2 years**. What holds it back is **trust and paperwork**: no independent reviews, an About page with no company details, return rules that contradict each other, and several best sellers you can't actually buy right now.

This review is based on BUCKTOOL's product pages, policies and on-site reviews as of November 2026. We haven't used the machines ourselves.

## Things that don't add up

- **Returns:** the FAQ says "30-day money-back, no questions asked", but the refund policy charges return shipping plus a restocking fee for change-of-mind, wrong-order and user-error returns.
- **Wrong specs in a FAQ:** the BG2600's FAQ lists 200 W, a 1x30 belt, 2950 RPM and 6 kg, which matches a different, smaller machine, not the 1/3 HP, 2x42, 39.9 lb BG2600 described on the same page.
- **"Brushless, no belts to replace":** the BD1801DS page calls its induction motor maintenance-free with "no belts to replace". That means no drive belt; you still replace the sanding belts and discs.
- **Company details:** the About page is generic brand copy ("power tools manufacturer and supplier worldwide") with no company name or address.
- **Trustpilot:** a claimed profile since May 2022 with zero reviews.

## Shipping and returns

- **Continental US only.** Free over $80, $5 for orders of $49–$79.99, $15 under $49; 3–7 business days.
- **Warranty:** 24 months.
- **Returns:** 30 days. Free return shipping for defects and approved reasons; **you pay return shipping and restocking** if you change your mind, ordered the wrong item or the problem is user error. On a 40–65 lb machine, return freight isn't cheap.
- **Support:** phone 909-255-1088 (Pacific time).

## Safety basics for grinders and sanders

Wear eye protection and keep the tool rest and spark guard adjusted close to the wheel. Never grind aluminium or wood on a grinding wheel meant for steel, and don't wear gloves near a spinning belt or wheel. Collect or vacuum fine dust, especially from wood and composites.

## Best for, and who should skip

- **Good fit:** a home shop that wants a sharpening grinder or small belt sander at a budget price, and buyers happy to check specs carefully.
- **Think twice:** if you need independent reviews before buying a $200+ machine, live outside the continental US, or might want to return it on a whim.
