## Overview

The **Fanttik X9 Pro** is the first product in the "Hot Sale" block on Fanttik's homepage and the cheapest inflator among its best sellers. It's a palm-sized cordless air pump with a **150 PSI** maximum, a digital screen, **preset modes with auto-stop** and an **integrated yellow hose**: pull the hose end out to switch it on, push it back in to switch off.

This review is based on Fanttik's product page, FAQ and policies as of October 2026, plus its on-site reviews. We haven't tested the X9 Pro ourselves.

## Inflation and battery

- **Max pressure:** 150 PSI, far more than a car tire needs; it's there for bikes and other high-pressure uses.
- **Speed:** the spec table says **70 seconds** to take a 25-inch tire from 30 to 35 PSI; the feature text says "only 1 min" for a compact car tire. Treat those as top-up times, not refills of a flat tire.
- **Battery:** **18.5 Wh** (7.4 V, 2.5 Ah, two 2,500 mAh cells), not replaceable. Fanttik rates it for **3 top-ups** of a 25-inch tire from 0 to 35 PSI per charge and up to **23 minutes** of continuous running.
- **Charging:** USB-C at **10 W**. There's **no power bank output**.

## Controls and design

- **4+N modes** (Fanttik's term) with presets and a custom target; it stops at the set pressure.
- **Units:** PSI, kPa and bar (hold the mode button 2–3 seconds to switch).
- The screen shows the tire's current pressure as soon as the hose is connected.
- **20 lm LED light**, a cooling system, and a slot in the body for the adapters.
- **Weight:** 0.96 lb. Fanttik lists it as compatible with **sedans, motorcycles and bikes**.
