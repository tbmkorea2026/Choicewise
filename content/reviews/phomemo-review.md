## Ratings: a big gap

Product pages show hundreds of reviews (321 on the PM241-BT, 1,752 on the M08F tattoo printer). **Trustpilot** is far less positive: **2.9/5 from 189 reviews**, with **59% 1-star** and 23% 5-star. Trustpilot notes the company hasn't invited reviews recently, so the reviews may not be representative, and that Phomemo replied to 92% of negative reviews. Its AI summary says buyers like the portable designs and the app for simple labels, but opinions on print quality, the app and support are divided.

## Shipping

- Orders are processed in **1–3 business days**, then delivery takes **3–15 business days**.
- **US, UK, Australia, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines:** free standard shipping from **$29.99**, otherwise $4.99.
- Canada, Germany, France and others: free from $69.99. Other regions have higher thresholds.
- Phomemo says some orders ship from local warehouses in the US, Germany and the UK.

## Returns and warranty

- **30-day money-back guarantee** from the day you receive the package, for items bought on phomemo.com.
- Items must be **unused**, with **all original packaging, accessories and manuals**. Otherwise you may get a **restocking fee** or **store credit**.
- **You pay return shipping** (and any tariffs) for non-quality returns. Phomemo covers shipping for quality problems.
- Orders **can't be cancelled** once processing has started.
- **Warranty: 1 year** from purchase; faulty products are replaced under Phomemo's terms.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** online sellers who print lots of 4x6 labels, crafters and students who want cheap inkless stickers, and anyone who wants a light printer for travel.
- **Think twice:** if you need color printing, long-lasting archival prints, or a very generous return policy.
