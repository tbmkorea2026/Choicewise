## Overview

The **Carpuride W602BS** is the first product in Carpuride's "Best Selling" collection. It's a **6-inch wireless CarPlay and Android Auto screen** made for **BMW motorcycles**: it slides into the bike's **original navigation mount**, takes power from it, and is controlled with BMW's **Wonder Wheel** on the handlebar. Price: **$449.99** (listed down from $599.99).

This review is based on Carpuride's product page, policies and customer ratings as of October 2026. We haven't tested the W602BS ourselves.

## Key features

Per Carpuride's product page:

- **6-inch IPS touchscreen**, 2.5D curved glass, **1,000 nits** for sunlight, with an automatic light sensor.
- **Wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto** for maps, music, calls and messages.
- **Wonder Wheel control:** rotate to scroll, press to select, long-press to switch between the nav screen and BMW's TFT mode.
- **Bike data:** speed, engine RPM, mileage and **tire pressure** (needs factory TPMS or Carpuride's sensors).
- **Dual Bluetooth 5.0:** send audio to one or two helmets, with a two-rider intercom up to 10 m.
- **SOS mode:** after a crash it counts down 10 seconds, then calls your emergency contact unless you cancel.
- **IP67 waterproof**, compass, barometer, free over-the-air (OTA) updates.
- **Built-in Type-C port** to power it and change settings off the bike.

## Installation

Carpuride says installation takes about **5 minutes**: make sure the bike has BMW's navigation mount, line up the connector, and slide the unit in. **No wiring or drilling.** That's the main advantage over universal screens, which need a handlebar bracket and a battery connection.

## Compatibility: check two lists

1. **Your bike:** the W602BS only works with **specific BMW models** that have the Wonder Wheel and factory nav mount. Carpuride links a BMW compatibility list on the product page.
2. **Your phone:** Carpuride warns it's **not compatible with HarmonyOS and some Android models**; there's a phone compatibility list too.

## Ratings

- **Store:** **4.73/5 from 231 reviews**, split 168 five-star and 63 four-star, with **no lower ratings shown**. That's the same pattern across Carpuride's store, so treat it with caution.
- **Brand on Trustpilot:** **4.3/5 from 442 reviews** (21% 1-star). Several BMW riders praise support and Wonder Wheel updates; complaints are mainly about units failing and repair shipping costs. See our [Carpuride review](/reviews/carpuride-review/) for details.

## Things to know

- **Riding safety:** set your route before you ride and use the Wonder Wheel, not the touchscreen, while moving.
- **No memory card included** (supports up to 256 GB).
- **Returns:** 30 days with prior approval; opened units without a fault can be refused and you pay return shipping.
- **Warranty:** 2 years on the screen and board; after 3 months, you pay shipping to Carpuride's service center.
