## What ONEVAN claims

- **2800 N·m** maximum tightening torque and **2800 W** power.
- **0–8000 RPM**, **0–8500 IPM**.
- **M12–M30** standard bolts, **M10–M24** high-strength bolts.
- **3 forward / 2 reverse** settings with an automatic mode and reverse auto stop.
- **LED** work light, brushless motor.

## Why we're skeptical of the numbers

Leading tool brands publish far lower torque figures for their most powerful 18V impact wrenches, and ONEVAN's own listing says loosening torque is "not provided." The stated vibration level, **20 m/s²**, is also high. None of this means the wrench is weak, but the headline numbers aren't independently verified, so compare tools on reviews rather than on spec sheets.

## Safety

- Use **impact-rated sockets** only.
- **Lug nuts:** finish with a torque wrench to the vehicle's spec.
- Wear eye protection; this is a heavy, high-vibration tool.

## Returns and warranty

- **60-day** returns; faulty items are returned or exchanged free.
- Non-faulty returns: you pay shipping plus a **5% restocking fee**.
- **No warranty period** stated in ONEVAN's policies.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** home mechanics with Makita-style batteries who want a strong, cheap wrench for wheels and suspension work.
- **Think twice:** if you need verified torque, a long warranty, or a tool for daily professional use.

## The bottom line

A well-reviewed budget impact wrench with useful settings and a low price, sold with numbers we don't trust. Our verdict: **6.8/10**.
