## What the listing tells you (and doesn't)

**Stated:** 48V, 314Ah, 16kWh, 210A BMS, metal case with wheels, for home solar, RV and off-grid backup.

**Not stated in text:** weight, dimensions, rated cycle life, operating and charging temperatures, communication ports (CAN/RS485), parallel limits, certifications. Some of this appears in the product images; ask DATOUBOSS in writing before ordering if you need it for an inverter or an installer.

## What buyers say

On the product page, reviewers report it **paired with a DATOUBOSS DT4862 inverter and six 510W panels**, and say the measured state of charge **matched the nominal value**. Remember the 4.88/389 rating is shared across the store. On Trustpilot, one buyer said a 16kWh battery ordered on 3 November was **still not dispatched on 22 November**.

## Warranty and returns, in plain words

- **0–18 months:** free replacement if defective.
- **After 18 months:** you pay the difference after depreciation.
- **Returns within 30 days:** must be resellable and boxed; **you pay** shipping for non-defective returns, which on a heavy battery is expensive.

## Safety

A 16kWh battery stores a lot of energy. Fuse it correctly, keep it in a dry, ventilated, frost-protected space, and use a qualified electrician for anything connected to your home or the grid.
