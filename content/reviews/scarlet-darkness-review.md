## Overview

**Scarlet Darkness** sells historically inspired and alternative women's clothing: **renaissance faire** dresses, **corset vests**, **Victorian blouses**, **steampunk** jackets, cloaks and **cottagecore** milkmaid dresses. Orders ship from China (the return address is in Dongguan), and the store also sells on marketplaces.

This review is based on Scarlet Darkness's product pages, policies, on-site ratings and Trustpilot as of October 2026. We haven't ordered from the store ourselves.

## Ratings

The gap between on-site and independent reviews is the biggest red flag:

- **On the store:** the top dresses and corset vest show **4.6–4.9/5**, but from only **8–27 reviews** each.
- **Trustpilot:** **2.9/5 from 216 reviews** (claimed profile). **45% are 1-star** and 38% are 5-star. Complaints focus on **weeks without shipping updates**, missing or wrong items, **costly international returns** and partial refunds or store credit instead of full refunds, and slow support. Positive reviews praise the **designs** and sometimes the fabric and fit.

## Shipping

- **Processing:** usually 1–2 business days, sometimes 5–7, and **3–4 weeks** for small-batch styles.
- **Delivery:** the FAQ quotes **7–25 working days** (10–30 for remote areas).
- **Cost (North America, Australia):** **$8.99–$12.99** standard (10–20 days) or **$18–$25** express (7–10 days); **free over $89**. EU and Middle East rates differ.
- The contact page warns that some **shipping emails get blocked** and to email again if nothing arrives within 20 days.

## Returns

- **14 days** from receiving your order, **tags intact**, no marks or makeup.
- **Email support first**; unauthorized returns aren't processed.
- **You pay return shipping**, and the temporary return address is in **Dongguan, China**.
- **Sale, promotional-gift and final-sale items** can't be returned or exchanged.
- Hygiene items (swimwear, bodysuits, intimates, stockings) aren't returnable.
- You can **cancel for a full refund before the order ships**.

## Company details

The site gives support emails (support@ and support01@scarletdarkness.com) and a Facebook page but **no company name or business address**. Its terms of service name **"Belle Poque Offcial"**, another brand, which suggests a shared or reused template.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** costume and renaissance-faire shoppers who want inexpensive, pretty pieces, order **weeks ahead** and size carefully.
- **Skip:** if you need it fast, may need to return it, or are buying sale items you're unsure about.

## The bottom line

Scarlet Darkness has appealing, affordable designs and good on-site ratings, but long delivery times, short buyer-paid returns to China and a 2.9 Trustpilot score make it a store to approach carefully.
