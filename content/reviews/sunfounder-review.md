**SunFounder** makes kits and accessories for makers and STEM learning: **Raspberry Pi cases and touchscreens**, **AI robot kits** like the PiCar-X and PiDog, and **Arduino and ESP32 starter kits**. Its About page lists **SunFounder Inc.** with a registered office in **Wilmington, Delaware**; its Trustpilot profile lists **China** as the location, and orders ship from a **China warehouse**.

This review covers prices, best sellers, ratings, shipping, returns and warranty, based on SunFounder's store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't built these kits ourselves.

## Ratings

- **On the SunFounder store:** the best sellers above are rated **4.7–4.9/5**, each from **115–200 reviews**.
- **On Trustpilot:** **4.7/5 from 95 reviews** (claimed profile). 84% are five-star and 10% are one-star.

Positive reviews praise **fast, helpful support**, **build quality** and **clear instructions**, especially for the Pironman 5 and PiDog. Negative ones mention **order delays**, **damaged or missing parts** and **software setup problems**.

## Shipping

- **Free shipping on all orders**, with no minimum.
- **Processing:** within **24 hours**; weekend orders ship the following Monday.
- **Ships from:** SunFounder's **China warehouse**.
- **US, Canada, UK, most of Europe, Japan, Australia:** registered airmail **7–14 working days**; orders over **$400** go by **DHL or UPS** in **3–7 working days**.
- **Brazil, Israel, South Africa:** airmail **15–30 working days**.
- **Import duties:** the shipping page says **the buyer pays** any import duties. Some product images (for example the Pironman cases) say **"US duties & EU VAT included"**, so check the total at checkout.

## Returns and warranty: read carefully

- **Return window:** the Warranty & Returns page allows a return or refund request **within 15 days** of receipt. Some product pages mention a **30-day** refund policy, so confirm before relying on it.
- Items must be **complete and in their original packaging**.
- **You pay return shipping** unless the item arrived **damaged or defective**.
- **5% restocking fee** on items worth **$500 or more**.
- **Defects:** report with photos or video; SunFounder says it responds **within 48 hours** and offers a replacement or other compensation.
- **Warranty voided by:** opening and repairing the product yourself, modifying parts or flashing firmware, accidental damage and misuse. The **warranty length isn't stated**.
- Refunds are processed within **2 working days** of receiving the return.

## Who should buy from SunFounder?

**A good fit if you:**

- Already own (or plan to buy) a **Raspberry Pi 5** and want a cooled mini-PC case with NVMe storage.
- Want a **robotics or AI kit** with step-by-step Python tutorials.
- Are learning **Arduino or ESP32** and like guided projects.

**Think twice if you:**

- Need your order within a few days (airmail takes up to 14 working days).
- Expect an all-in-one kit: most products need a Raspberry Pi or other parts bought separately.
- Want a long, clearly stated warranty or generous return window.

## The bottom line

SunFounder makes some of the most popular Raspberry Pi 5 cases and maker kits around, with good ratings and free shipping. Read each product's "what you need" list, allow two to three weeks for delivery, and check duties and the return window before you order.
