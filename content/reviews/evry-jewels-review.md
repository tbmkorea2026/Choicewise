## Materials and "waterproof" claims

According to EVRY's help center, pieces are either **14k gold or silver plating over brass** or **18k gold or silver plating over stainless steel**, with an **anti-tarnish coating**, and the help center admits plated pieces **can still tarnish over time**. Many listings offer both **"Waterproof"** and **"Water Resistant"** options without defining the difference, and EVRY still recommends removing jewelry before swimming, bathing or washing hands. Stainless-steel pieces are a **darker shade** of gold or silver.

- **Trustpilot:** **3.9/5 from 10,070 reviews** (claimed, paid subscription): **67% 5-star, 20% 1-star**. Praise focuses on price, looks and some pieces holding up in water; complaints mention **missing or late parcels**, **wrong items**, pieces that **tarnish or break**, and slow support.

## Shipping

- **Processing:** usually **2–3 business days**; custom and pre-order pieces take **4–6 weeks**.
- **Delivery (after shipping):** about **3–7 business days** across the US and **2–7** across most of Canada.
- **Costs** are shown at checkout; there's no stated free-shipping threshold, and shipping fees aren't refundable.
- **Lost parcels:** with paid **Package Protection**, EVRY reships free; without it, reshipping costs **$6**.
- International orders may owe duties and taxes.

## Returns and warranty

- **All sales are final:** no returns, exchanges or cancellations. You get **30 minutes** to change your address.
- **30-day warranty** for manufacturing defects (broken clasp, missing stone) on website orders: a **replacement** if in stock or **store credit**, never a refund. Claims need photos and approval.
- A **paid protection plan** at checkout extends coverage to one year.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** if you want cheap, trendy pieces and accept that they're a low-cost, final-sale purchase.
- **Skip:** if you might change your mind, want solid gold, or have sensitive skin and need exact alloy details.
