## Overview

Most independent restaurants and cafés cannot afford an R&D chef or a market research agency. **Gapgastro** wants to fill that gap with a single, fixed-price package: local market research, custom recipes designed to set you apart from nearby competitors, and a pricing and menu strategy, delivered by email for **$290**.

This review is based on Gapgastro's own website, offer page, Terms of Service and Refund Policy as of October 2026. We have not purchased the package, and Gapgastro has not published customer results we could verify, so we focus on exactly what you get, what it costs and what the risks are.

## How Gapgastro works

According to Gapgastro, the process has five steps:

1. **Sign up** and complete an intake form about your business, market and goals.
2. **Fill in the brief:** your menu, kitchen setup, competitors, customers and constraints.
3. **Gapgastro researches** your local market using surveys and AI-assisted research to map gaps, pricing and opportunities.
4. **You receive the deliverables** by email: custom recipes and a strategy tailored to your kitchen.
5. **You test the ideas** in-house with your own team.

The key point is that Gapgastro does the research and creative work, but **execution is up to you**. Results will depend on how well your kitchen and front-of-house put the ideas into practice.

## What's included in the $290 package

The Premium Plan, which Gapgastro calls "The 35X Revenue Expansion Sprint", includes:

- **3 custom recipe differentiators:** dishes designed to be unique in your local market.
- **Ingredient sourcing and kitchen workflow guide** to help keep costs and prep realistic.
- **Menu placement strategy:** where to position items on your menu.
- **Competitor gap snapshot:** what nearby competitors offer and where the openings are.
- **Profit and pricing sheet** to help you price new items with margins in mind.
- **30-day revenue activation plan** for launching the new items.
- **3 social media post concepts** to promote them.
- **Demand validation score** to estimate how likely the concept is to succeed.
- **Free bonus: ZIP code exclusivity**, listed as a $150 value. The website does not spell out the exact terms, so ask Gapgastro what it covers before you buy.

Gapgastro says each package involves **55+ hours of human R&D**. The team named on the website includes an R&D chef, an executive chef, a food scientist with 5+ years of experience and a market researcher.

## Confidentiality and ownership

This is one of Gapgastro's strongest points. The company states that anything you share is kept strictly confidential and that it **does not sell, publicly share or reuse** your recipes, business information or custom research for another client. Under its Terms, you may use the customized deliverables in your business, while Gapgastro keeps ownership of its underlying methods and frameworks.

## The "35X results" promise: what it really means

Gapgastro's website prominently promises **"35X results in 6 months"**. If you don't see them, it says it will run **two additional full work cycles** of the same package for free (which it values at $2,900).

Here is what you should know before relying on that promise:

- **It is not a money-back guarantee.** Gapgastro's Refund Policy states that any performance promise "is not a cash-refund guarantee". You get more work, not your money back.
- **There is no published evidence for the 35X figure.** Gapgastro openly says it is "just getting started" and does not yet have case studies, and the example on its homepage is labeled as an illustrative pattern, not a client result.
- **The Terms of Service say results may vary** based on implementation, pricing, location, demand, competition and marketing.
- The website does not define exactly what "35X" measures, so ask Gapgastro to clarify in writing before you buy.

To its credit, Gapgastro is upfront about being new and says it does not use fake testimonials. We simply recommend treating 35X as an aspiration, not an expected outcome.

## Pricing, refunds and support

- **Price:** $290 one-time, listed as 80% off a $1,450 regular price. Gapgastro's referral program states that customers referred through a partner link or coupon get **10% off**, so check the final price at checkout.
- **Refunds:** generally **non-refundable once work has started**. Cancellation before work begins may be refunded case by case.
- **Corrections:** if deliverables are incomplete or materially incorrect, Gapgastro says it will work with you to correct them.
- **Support:** solutions@gapgastro.com, with replies within two business days.
- **Free option:** a **free menu audit**, where you upload up to three photos of your menu for a professional review.

## Who should consider Gapgastro?

**It may be a good fit if:**

- You run an independent restaurant, café or other food business and want fresh, differentiated menu ideas.
- You don't have an in-house R&D chef and can't justify a consultant's fees.
- You have a team that can test and roll out new dishes within the next month or two.

**Look elsewhere if:**

- You need proven results, case studies or references before spending money.
- You want a refundable purchase or hands-on, in-kitchen support.
- You expect the package alone to transform revenue without changes to marketing or operations.

## The bottom line

Gapgastro offers a genuinely useful idea at an accessible price: custom recipes, local market research and pricing guidance for $290, with a clear confidentiality promise. The trade-offs are real, though: it is a new company without a track record, the 35X promise is unproven and is not backed by a refund, and you are responsible for execution. **Our advice: start with the free menu audit.** If the feedback is specific and useful for your business, the $290 package is a reasonable, low-cost experiment.
