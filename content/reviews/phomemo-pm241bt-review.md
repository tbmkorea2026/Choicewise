## Overview

The **PM241-BT** is first on Phomemo's best-selling list. It's a desktop **shipping label printer** aimed at small online sellers: print a 4x6 label from a marketplace or carrier site, peel it and stick it on the box, with **no ink** to replace.

This review is based on Phomemo's product page, specs, policies and customer ratings as of November 2026. We haven't tested it ourselves. For the brand overview, see our [Phomemo review](/reviews/phomemo-review/).

## Printing

- **203 dpi** direct thermal printing, which Phomemo says gives sharp, smudge-free text and barcodes.
- Up to **150mm/s**, about **72 labels per minute** according to Phomemo.
- Works with **USPS, UPS, DHL, Amazon, eBay, Shopify** and other platforms.

## Connecting

- **Bluetooth** for iOS and Android phones and tablets.
- **USB** for computers, which Phomemo recommends for stable printing.

## Labels

You can use plain white 4x6 shipping labels or Phomemo's holographic, gradient, patterned and shaped (hearts, stars) rolls. Phomemo says the PM241-BT is **not compatible with HUOJI thermal labels** and doesn't suggest other third-party labels, so budget for Phomemo-brand labels.

## Price, returns and warranty

- **$110.99** (list $159.99) when we checked, with US, UK, EU and AU plug options. Code **TRANTRUNG** takes **10% off**.
- **Free US shipping** on orders of $29.99 or more; 1–3 days processing plus 3–15 business days delivery.
- **30-day money-back guarantee**, but items must be unused and complete, and you pay return shipping unless it's faulty.
- **Warranty:** 1 year.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** sellers shipping a few to a few hundred parcels a week who want to print from a phone or laptop without ink.
- **Think twice:** if you need 300 dpi for tiny barcodes, want to use any cheap third-party label, or need color.

## The bottom line

The PM241-BT covers the basics small sellers need: Bluetooth and USB, fast 4x6 printing and no ink, at a heavily discounted price. The 203 dpi head, label compatibility warning and the brand's weak Trustpilot score are the limits. Our verdict: **7.6/10**.
