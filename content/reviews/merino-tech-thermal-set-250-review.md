## Caring for merino

Merino lasts longer when you treat it gently. Merino.tech's instructions:

- **Machine wash cold** with a **mild or wool-specific detergent**.
- **No fabric softener or bleach.**
- **Lay flat to dry.**
- Wash before storing, and store in a dry, airtight place to keep moths away.
