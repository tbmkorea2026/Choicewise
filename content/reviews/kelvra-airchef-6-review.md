## Overview

Dual-basket air fryers solve the biggest frustration with single-basket models: cooking a full meal in batches. The **Kelvra AirChef 6** splits its 6-quart capacity into two independently controlled baskets, so fries and chicken can cook side by side at different settings.

{{cta}}

## Cooking performance

Results are consistently crispy and evenly browned. Frozen fries reach a golden finish with a single shake, chicken thighs come out juicy with crackling skin, and roasted vegetables caramelise nicely. Preheating is quick, which makes it a real alternative to the oven for small meals.

## Design and ease of use

The control panel is clear, with dedicated buttons for each zone. **Sync Finish** is the standout feature: set each basket's time and temperature, and the AirChef 6 staggers the start so both finish together. **Match Cook** copies one zone's settings to the other for larger batches.

## Cleaning

Both baskets and crisper plates are non-stick and dishwasher-safe. A quick soak handles stubborn grease. We recommend avoiding metal utensils to protect the coating.

## Is it worth it?

For households that cook for three or more, the AirChef 6 is one of the most practical kitchen upgrades you can make. If you live alone or have limited counter space, a compact single-basket model will serve you better.
