## Overview

The **Berny 2946L** is the #1 best seller in Berny's store. It's a tiny women's dress watch with an **oval, cloud-shaped case** in gold tone, inspired (per Berny) by European vintage copper mirrors. It runs on a **Japanese Miyota quartz** movement and comes with a gold (champagne), white, green, blue or black dial.

This review is based on Berny's product page, policies and public ratings as of October 2026. We haven't worn the 2946L ourselves.

This is a **petite** watch, like the small vintage pieces that are popular again. It will look delicate on most wrists; if you prefer a bolder look, the square **2166L** has a wider 16 mm bracelet. See [2946L vs 2166L](/best/berny-2946l-vs-2166l/).

## Specs and build

- **Movement:** Japanese Miyota quartz, so it's accurate and needs a battery change every few years (battery replacement isn't covered by the warranty).
- **Crystal:** mineral glass. It's harder than acrylic but scratches more easily than sapphire.
- **Water resistance:** 3 ATM (30 m), i.e. splash-resistant only.
- **Case back:** solid. No lume on the dial.

## The bottom line

The 2946L is a **pretty, petite gold dress watch** with a Japanese quartz movement for **$64.99**. It's light, thin and comes in five dial colours, which explains why it's Berny's best seller. Treat it as a fashion piece rather than a rugged watch (mineral glass, 3 ATM), make sure it fits a wrist up to 175 mm, and inspect it the day it arrives. Our verdict: **6.6/10**.
