## Overview

The **NikolaToy TR900 Turbofan Engine Model** is one of the store's best sellers and one of its most eye-catching pieces. It is a **25 cm, 3D-printed** model styled after a large airliner turbofan, with a **cutaway design** that shows the fan at the front, the compressor and turbine stages along the core, and the exhaust cone at the back.

This review is based on NikolaToy's official product information and photos as of October 2026. The official specifications are brief, so we stick to what NikolaToy states and point out what you should confirm before buying.

## Design and build

The TR900's appeal is visual. The front fan is shown in silver, and the cutaway core is colored to highlight the different sections, from the compressor stages to a red-accented hot section and the exhaust cone, mounted on a branded black stand. It works as a **desk or shelf display piece** and a conversation starter for anyone interested in how jet engines work.

NikolaToy lists the model as **PLA**, made with **high-precision 3D printing**. That keeps it light at **450 g**, though it won't have the heft of a metal model.

> **What isn't listed:** the product page doesn't specify whether the fan is motorized, how it is powered or whether the lighting shown in photos is included in both versions. If those matter to you, email NikolaToy before ordering.
