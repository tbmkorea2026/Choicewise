## Overview

**ROCKBROS** is a cycling-accessories brand whose founder dates the idea to a long mountain ride in 2007. **rockbrosbike.us** is its **US store**, listing an address in **Elizabeth, New Jersey**, and selling pumps, mirrors, saddles, bags, lights, sunglasses, apparel, tools and a few bikes. (We also reviewed the separate [ROCKBROS Australia store](/reviews/rockbros-review/).)

This review is based on the store's product pages, policies, on-site reviews and Trustpilot as of November 2026. We haven't tested ROCKBROS products ourselves.

## Ratings

- **On the store:** **4.61–4.82/5** across the top 5, from **11 to 95 reviews** per product.
- **Trustpilot:** **4.0/5 from 80 reviews**, claimed and listed in New Jersey: **60% 5-star, 25% 1-star**. Praise covers comfortable apparel, durability and helpful support; complaints mention shipping delays, tracking problems, slow email replies and some component failures.

## Shipping

- Ships to the **contiguous US**; some remote areas may not qualify.
- **$5.99 under $50**, **free over $50**; orders usually ship within 1 business day and arrive in **3–4 business days**.
- Product pages add that if US stock runs out, orders **ship from overseas** in **7–10 business days**.
- Bikes cost **$40–$200** to ship and take weeks.

## Returns and warranty

- **30 days** from receipt, with **return authorization** first. Items must be unused, with tags and packaging.
- **Change of mind or fit:** **you pay** return and exchange shipping. **ROCKBROS errors or defects:** free return label.
- **Damaged, defective or wrong items:** report within **72 hours** of delivery.
- **Final sale:** sale and clearance items, gift cards and custom products. Since many products show sale prices, check before you buy. (The site footer promises "no questions" full refunds; the policy is stricter.)
- **Refunds** take 5–10 business days after approval.
- **Warranty:** **2-year limited** on direct purchases. The mirror page mentions a 1-year replacement guarantee.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** US riders who want inexpensive, well-rated accessories and can live with paying return shipping.
- **Think twice:** if you buy discounted items you might return, or need precise specs (double-check pages that contradict themselves).

## The bottom line

ROCKBROS USA sells genuinely cheap, popular cycling upgrades with fast US shipping and a 2-year warranty. Its policies are stricter than its marketing suggests, and some product pages are sloppy.
