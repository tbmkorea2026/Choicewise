## Overview

The **36V Down Tube Hailong 1-2 battery** comes first in Yose Power's best-selling sort. It's a lithium-ion pack in the popular **Hailong** down-tube case, sold in **15.6Ah (577Wh)** for **€219** or **20Ah (740Wh)** for **€259**. It mounts on a rail on the bike's down tube, locks with a key, and has a four-level charge indicator, a power switch and a charge port.

This review is based on Yose Power's product page, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the battery ourselves. Specs and compatibility claims are Yose Power's.

## Safety first

- **Match voltage and connector:** this is a **36V** pack with a **4-pin** discharge port. Fitting it to a 48V system, or forcing an adapter onto the wrong plug, is unsafe.
- **Charge with the supplied 42V charger** on a hard, non-flammable surface, and don't charge a damaged or wet battery. Yose Power's product image warns that charge current must not exceed 5A.
- **Store it partly charged** and top it up at least monthly if unused; Yose Power says skipping this can void the warranty.
- **Road rules:** in the EU and UK, a pedelec motor is limited to **250W** rated power. The battery itself is fine; check the motor you pair it with.

## Key features

- **Two capacities:** 15.6Ah/577Wh or 20Ah/740Wh, both 36V.
- **High current:** 10A standard, **30A max**, 60A protection, listed for **250W–900W** 36V kits.
- **Fast charger:** **42V 3A**, EU plug, included. The 13Ah Hailong battery comes with a 2A charger.
- **Size and weight:** **367 × 90 × 111 mm**, about **3.5–3.8 kg**.
- **In the box:** battery, charger, two keys, discharge connector and an **XT60 adapter**.
- **Cells:** listed as "High Power 18650"; the brand isn't stated. Yose Power claims **1,000+ cycles**.

## What to check first

1. **Mount:** your bike needs a Hailong 1-2 style rail and enough frame space for a 111 mm-tall case.
2. **Connector:** 4-pin discharge port. If your old battery has a **5-pin gold-plated round plug**, look at the [13Ah Hailong battery](/best/yosepower-hailong-1-2-vs-hailong-13ah/) instead.
3. **Controller:** must be 36V, and ideally rated within the battery's 30A max.

The battery has an **18-month** warranty and the charger **12 months**. ## Who should buy

- **Yes:** DIY builders with a 36V kit who want lots of range, or owners replacing a Hailong 1-2 battery with the same 4-pin port.
- **Think twice:** owners of brand-name e-bikes with proprietary batteries, or anyone who can't confirm the mount and connector.
