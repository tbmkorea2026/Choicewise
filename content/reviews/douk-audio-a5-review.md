## Overview

The **Douk Audio A5** is the #1 best seller on Douk Audio's store. It's a small **TI TPA3255** class D stereo amplifier designed for passive speakers, with an **adjustable high-pass filter** and **swappable op-amps** as its two party tricks.

This review is based on Douk Audio's product page, policies and store ratings as of October 2026. We haven't used the A5 ourselves.

## Key features

Per Douk Audio:

- **TPA3255 with PFFB:** post-filter feedback and a **4-layer immersion gold PCB** to lower distortion (**THD+N 0.002%**, **SNR ≥114dB**).
- **High-pass filter, 30–200Hz:** trims bass from the main speakers for a cleaner sound with small bookshelf speakers.
- **3.5mm AUX output (3.7V):** feed a powered subwoofer or a second amp.
- **Swappable op-amps:** two NE5532 chips under a **tempered glass** panel; Douk lists 2134, 2604, 2068 and MUSES02 as upgrades.
- **Look:** transparent top with adjustable internal LED lighting.

**Inputs and outputs:** one stereo **RCA** input; **banana** speaker jacks and the 3.5mm AUX output. There's **no Bluetooth, remote, tone control or headphone jack**.

## Real power output

The "300W×2" on the product title is a maximum. Douk's own rated-power table:

| Power supply | 4Ω | 6Ω | 8Ω |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 32V/5A (included, $89.99) | 78W×2 | 71W×2 | 65W×2 |
| 36V/6A | 107W×2 | 100W×2 | 94W×2 |
| 48V/5A (included, $109.99) | 120W×2 | 110W×2 | 102W×2 |

For most bookshelf speakers, the 32V version is enough. Pick the **48V** version for less sensitive speakers or larger rooms.

## The bottom line

The Douk Audio A5 packs a TPA3255 amp, PFFB and a genuinely useful **high-pass filter** into an **$89.99** box, with strong store reviews. It's a good pick for a budget 2.1 system with a separate DAC or streamer. Just read the rated power table, not the 300W headline, and know you'll need another source for Bluetooth. Our verdict: **7.9/10**.
