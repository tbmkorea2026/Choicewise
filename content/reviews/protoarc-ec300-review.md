## Overview

The **ProtoArc EC300** is listed first in ProtoArc's Best Sellers collection. It's an ergonomic mesh chair built around a **height-adjustable backrest**: instead of moving only a lumbar pad, the entire back moves up and down.

This review is based on ProtoArc's product page, policies and store ratings as of November 2026. We haven't sat in the EC300 ourselves. A chair can support better posture, but it isn't a treatment for back pain.

## Key features

Per ProtoArc:

- **Backrest:** soft-touch mesh with **5 height positions** over 2.36 inches.
- **Lumbar:** wide lumbar and sacrum support that moves with the backrest.
- **Seat:** **55D high-density foam**, with a **5-position slide** over 1.97 inches.
- **Headrest:** **3D**, adjusting in height, depth, rotation and pivot.
- **Armrests:** **2D** height-adjustable.
- **Recline:** locks at **90°, 105°, 120° and 130°**, plus a tilt tension knob.
- **Base:** alloy base, **Class 4 gas lift** and 2.36-inch quiet-roll casters.

**In the box:** chair, headrest, armrests, seat cushion and manual. Assembly takes about 20 minutes.

## Fit

ProtoArc recommends the EC300 for users about **5'4"–6'1"** and **90–220 lb**. If you're outside that range, look elsewhere rather than risk paying to return a bulky box.

## Ratings

The EC300 shows **4.91/5 from 22 reviews** on ProtoArc's store. The cheaper EC200 has more feedback, at 4.83/5 from 180. ProtoArc's Trustpilot profile has only 3 reviews; see our [ProtoArc review](/reviews/protoarc-review/).
