## Overview

The **BodegaCooler TWW75** is the first non-accessory product on BodegaCooler's best-selling list and carries a "TOP 1 Choice" label on its page. It's a large, wheeled **dual-zone compressor fridge** for overlanding, camping and RV trips.

This review is based on BodegaCooler's product page, specs, policies and store reviews as of November 2026. We haven't tested the TWW75 ourselves. For the brand overview, see our [BodegaCooler review](/reviews/bodegacooler-review/).

## Capacity and cooling

- **80 quarts (75L)** split into **48 QT** and **31 QT** zones, each set independently from **-4°F to 68°F**.
- **Max and Eco modes**; BodegaCooler says the left zone drops from 68°F to 32°F in **15 minutes** on Max when empty.
- Room for about **102 cans, 60 water bottles or 33 wine bottles**, per BodegaCooler.

## Power and battery protection

- Runs on **12V or 24V DC** and **100–240V AC**; rated input **90 W** (DC 6.0 A at 12V).
- **3-level battery protection** (H, M, L) to avoid draining your vehicle battery.
- An **optional detachable battery** gives 4–10 hours of cooling, per BodegaCooler, and is the only way to run it from solar.

## Design

- **Two side-opening doors** that can be removed or reversed, a **drain plug**, **LED light** and a dual-zone **basket**.
- **Wheels and a retractable handle**, which you'll want at about **56.6 lb** empty.
- The spec list gives **32.5 × 21 × 18.5 in**, but the size table lists **36.89 in** wide. Noise is listed as both **<42 dB** and **43–47 dB**. Check both figures before buying.

## Price, returns and warranty

- **$649.99** (compare-at $799.99); code **TRANTRUNG** takes 12% off at the time of writing.
- **Free shipping** in the mainland US.
- **30-day returns** if unused and in the original box; **you pay shipping both ways** for a change of mind.
- **Warranty:** 24 months, +1 year if you register within 10 days.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** groups and long trips that need lots of separate fridge and freezer space, with room in the vehicle.
- **Think twice:** if you lift it into a vehicle often, or if one zone of 50–60 quarts is enough (the T series is lighter and cheaper).

## The bottom line

The TWW75 packs real dual-zone capacity, flexible power and useful battery protection into one wheeled box. Its weight, conflicting specs and the brand's service record are the trade-offs. Our verdict: **7.3/10**.
