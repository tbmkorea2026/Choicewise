## Piercing safety first

- **Fresh piercings:** OUFER's titanium hoops say they're "made for healed piercings." Let a professional piercer choose jewelry for a new piercing.
- **Material:** ASTM F-136 titanium is the alloy commonly used for implants and recommended for sensitive skin. ASTM F-138 is implant-grade steel but contains some nickel, so if you react to nickel, choose titanium.
- **Size:** check gauge (thickness) and inner diameter before ordering; a hoop that's too tight can irritate a piercing.
- **Irritation:** if a piercing becomes red, swollen or painful, ask a piercer or doctor before changing or removing the jewelry.

- **60-day** return or refund window.

- **Quality problem:** OUFER pays the shipping.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** anyone with healed piercings who wants inexpensive titanium or steel hoops in lots of sizes and colors.
- **Think twice:** for a brand-new piercing, if you're in the EU and ordering under $30, or if you want premium solid-gold pieces with easy returns.
