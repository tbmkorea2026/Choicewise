## In one minute

An OBD2 scanner plugs into the port under your dashboard and tells you **why the check-engine light is on**, and better ones also read **ABS, airbag, transmission and body** systems and run **service resets**. THINKCAR's pitch is doing that **without yearly software fees**. Prices run from a **$57 Bluetooth dongle** to **$500+ tablets** that dealers-lite shops use. Ratings are among the best we've seen for a brand store.

This review is based on THINKCAR's product pages, policies, on-site reviews and its Trustpilot profile as of November 2026. We haven't used the scanners ourselves.

## Updates: the part that matters most

Many scanner brands sell the hardware cheaply and then charge every year for software. THINKCAR's best sellers mostly don't:

- **Lifetime free updates:** ThinkScan 689BT, MUCAR 892BT, ThinkScan 662, MUCAR BT200 MAX, BD6.
- **1 year free:** ThinkDiag 2 (renewal needed after that to get new vehicle coverage).

## Shipping, returns and warranty

- **Shipping:** free standard to the US and EU (3–15 days plus 1–3 days processing); fast shipping $29.99 (US). Elsewhere free over $200.
- **Returns:** 30 days, same condition with original packaging; activated devices accepted if undamaged. **You pay and insure return shipping.**
- **Warranty:** 1 year.
- **Discount:** code **QVVGOFDI** = 10% off (may not stack with automatic sale prices).

## Use it safely

Bi-directional tests and ECU coding switch real components on and off. Follow on-screen instructions, keep the car in park with the parking brake on, and don't code modules you don't understand. A scanner tells you what's wrong; it doesn't repair it.

## The bottom line

Strong ratings, real lifetime updates on most models and prices well below pro tools. Check compatibility, avoid the ThinkDiag 2 if you hate renewals, and keep the box for 30 days.
