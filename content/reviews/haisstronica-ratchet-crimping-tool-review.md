## How it works

### Full-cycle ratchet

The defining feature is the **full-cycle ratchet**: once you start squeezing, the jaws won't open until the crimp is complete. That helps you apply **consistent, repeatable pressure** on every connection, the main advantage of ratcheting crimpers over simple non-ratcheting ones. A **release lever** lets you open the jaws mid-cycle if you need to reposition a terminal.

### Color-coded crimp positions

The jaws have **marked, color-coded positions** that correspond to the standard terminal colors (red, blue and yellow for increasing wire sizes). You match the terminal color to the jaw position, insert, and crimp.

### Pressure adjustment

An **adjustment** on the ratchet lets you fine-tune crimp pressure for different terminal brands or a tighter or looser crimp.

## What it works with

The tool is designed for **heat shrink insulated terminals** in the **AWG 22-10** range, including the butt, ring, fork, spade and bullet terminals found in Haisstronica's [Heat Shrink Wire Connectors Kit](/best/best-haisstronica-products/). Haisstronica's own product pages recommend this crimper for that kit.

It is **not** designed for non-insulated or open-barrel terminals, which need a different crimp shape. If your projects use those, look at Haisstronica's dedicated crimpers or a tool kit with multiple crimpers.

> **Check the gauge range:** Haisstronica lists this crimper as **AWG 22-10**, but the tool shown in the product photo has a jaw marked **26-10 AWG** with an extra 26-24 position. If you need to crimp very thin wire, confirm the current version with Haisstronica before ordering.

## How to make a good heat shrink crimp

1. **Strip** the wire to the length of the terminal barrel.
2. **Match** the terminal color to the marked jaw position.
3. **Crimp** with a full squeeze until the ratchet releases.
4. **Tug-test** the connection.
5. **Heat** the terminal evenly with a heat gun until the tubing shrinks and the adhesive seals.
