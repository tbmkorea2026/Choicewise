## Overview

Mid-range earbuds have improved fast, but few combine **strong noise cancelling, great sound and all-day comfort** without a premium price. The Nuvio Pods Pro 2 do exactly that, which is why they top our [best wireless earbuds](/best/best-wireless-earbuds/) guide.

## Sound quality

Out of the box the tuning is warm and full, with punchy bass that never muddies vocals. Detail retrieval is impressive for the price: acoustic tracks have a clear sense of space, and the treble is crisp without being harsh. The companion app's 10-band EQ lets you fine-tune the sound, and the presets are sensible rather than gimmicky.

## Noise cancelling

This is where the Pods Pro 2 punch well above their price. Low-frequency rumble from trains, planes and air conditioning is reduced dramatically, and mid-range chatter is noticeably softened. The transparency mode sounds natural enough for quick conversations without removing the buds.

## Comfort and battery life

Three sizes of silicone tips are included, and the buds sit flush in the ear. We found them comfortable for long listening sessions. Battery life of around 8 hours with ANC on is above average, and a 10-minute charge gives roughly 2 hours of playback.

> **Tip:** Run the in-app fit test after choosing your tips. A proper seal improves both bass and noise cancelling.

## Who should buy them?

If you commute, work in busy spaces or simply want one pair of earbuds that does everything well, the Nuvio Pods Pro 2 are an easy recommendation. Audiophiles who need lossless playback, or athletes who need a secure hook design, should look at the alternatives in our ranking.
