## Overview

**Mewcats** is an online store for cat products: **strollers and carriers on wheels**, **cat trees and "villas"**, **solid wood cat doors**, **wall shelves**, **beds**, **apparel** (including clothes for hairless Sphynx cats), **collars** and **toys**. Its contact page lists **Ningbo Jiaqing Decoration Engineering Co., Ltd.** in **Ningbo, China**, and a Trustpilot reviewer from Denmark reports their order shipped from mainland China.

This review covers the range, prices, ratings, shipping and returns, based on Mewcats' store and policies as of October 2026. We haven't ordered from Mewcats ourselves.

### Who makes the products?

Mewcats lists itself as the vendor, but some product photos show **other brand names**, such as **"BoSiMao"** on a stroller and **"BELLO"** on another, and several cat furniture items are labeled **JCAT**. That suggests Mewcats **resells products from other manufacturers**. That isn't unusual, but it means Mewcats' own after-sales support matters.

## Shipping: contradictory information

Mewcats publishes two different pictures:

- **Shipping policy:** processing **1–3 days**, then **standard shipping 7–15 business days** or **free shipping 20–40 business days**. It still mentions Coronavirus delays.
- **FAQ:** **free shipping to over 200 countries**, in-stock items **ship within 2 working days**, and **no extra shipping, customs or duties fees**.

The store also has a **$45 "Shipping Fee Link"** product to pay extra shipping costs agreed with customer service. For a large cat tree or villa, **ask about shipping costs, delivery time and duties before ordering**.

## Returns: read before buying

- **30 days from purchase**; items must be **unused, in original packaging**.
- **Serious quality problems:** Mewcats says it pays return shipping. **Damaged packaging affecting use:** free replacement or resend.
- **Changed your mind:** **you pay return shipping**, which could be costly for a bulky item going back to China.
- **Non-returnable:** **sale items**, **custom products** (such as made-to-order cat doors) and gift cards.
- **Cancellations:** **5% fee** before dispatch; **no cancellations** after dispatch. (The FAQ separately says you can cancel within 12 hours.)
- **Refunds:** within **10 business days** of approval.
- **Lost packages:** reported in time, Mewcats reships free if the carrier declares a loss.
- **EU customers** get a **14-day cooling-off period**.

We couldn't find a published **warranty** policy.

## Who should buy from Mewcats?

**A good fit if you:**

- Want an unusual cat stroller, cat tree or piece of cat furniture you can't easily find elsewhere.
- Can wait several weeks and are confident about size and fit before ordering.

**Think twice if you:**

- Might want to return a large item.
- Need fast delivery or local after-sales support.

## The bottom line

Mewcats' selection is impressive and its products are well rated on the store, but the business behind it is thinly documented and its policies favor the seller.
