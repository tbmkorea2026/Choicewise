## Overview

Automatic feeders are a lifesaver for busy schedules, but most are designed for one pet. The **Furlo SmartFeeder Duo** splits each portion between two stainless steel bowls, which helps reduce mealtime squabbles.

{{cta}}

## Performance

Portion accuracy was consistent across a range of kibble sizes, and the anti-jam rotor did its job. Schedules are easy to create in the app, and you can trigger an extra snack remotely.

## Camera and app

The 1080p camera with night vision is a nice way to confirm your pets have eaten. Live view is free, while cloud video history requires a subscription.

## Verdict

For homes with two cats or small dogs, the SmartFeeder Duo is reliable and thoughtfully designed. Single-pet owners can save money with a simpler model.
