## Overview

**ROCKBROS** is a Chinese cycling-gear brand whose founder says it started after a 2,200 km ride from Sichuan to Tibet in 2007. It now sells eyewear, pumps, lights, pedals, helmets, bags, tools, apparel and bikes worldwide. **rockbros.com.au** is the Australian store, operated by **ROCKBROS SPORTS PTY LTD**, with a Melbourne warehouse and a **brand store and workshop at 440 Station Street, Box Hill**.

This review is based on the store's product pages, policies and on-site ratings as of October 2026. We haven't tested ROCKBROS gear ourselves.

## Shipping

- **Dispatch:** orders received by 5 pm AEST typically dispatch within **two business days**, mostly from **Melbourne**.
- **Accessories and apparel:** **A$10** economy (2–4 business days, Australia Post tracked) or **A$15** express (1–2 days).
- **Free shipping** on accessory orders over **A$99**, but **not during promotions or sales**.
- **Bulky items (over 3 kg):** flat **A$25**.
- **Overseas warehouses:** some new or back-ordered items ship from Germany, the US, Japan or China for **A$15** and take **15–20 business days**.
- **Click and collect** from the Box Hill store is free.

## Returns and warranty

- **Returns:** within **14 days** of purchase, **new condition with original packaging**; **you pay return shipping**, and shipping fees aren't refunded. Product pages repeat that change-of-mind and wrong-size returns are at your cost.
- **Exchanges:** the same policy says you can exchange within **10 days** "at no extra cost" if an item is damaged or you're unhappy, which doesn't fully match the return terms. Email support first.
- **Warranty:** **12 months** for manufacturing defects on accessories and equipment; bike frames get **5 years**. Accidents, misuse and normal wear aren't covered.
- **Australian Consumer Law** guarantees for faulty goods apply on top of these policies.
