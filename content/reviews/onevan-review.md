## Overview

**ONEVAN** sells low-cost **cordless tools**, best known for its **1/2" brushless impact wrenches**. Its range also includes string trimmers, snow shovels, nail guns, paint sprayers and batteries. Almost everything runs on **Makita-style 18V batteries**, and most tools come "tool only" or as a kit with two batteries and a charger. ONEVAN says it was founded in **2021** and designs and manufactures its own tools.

This review is based on ONEVAN's product pages, policies and customer ratings as of November 2026. We haven't tested the tools ourselves.

## About those specs

ONEVAN's wrenches are sold on very big numbers: **2800 N·m** and **2800 W** for its top model, **1800 N·m** and **1200 N·m** for the others. These figures come only from ONEVAN, they're much higher than major brands publish for their flagship 18V wrenches, and the 2800 N·m listing says loosening torque is "not provided." We'd treat them as marketing, not measured results.

## Safety

- Use **impact-rated sockets** with any impact wrench.
- Don't rely on an impact wrench to set **wheel lug nut torque**; finish with a torque wrench to your vehicle's spec.
- Use eye protection, and keep hands clear of rotating parts.

## Ratings: mixed

Store reviews are good for the wrenches: **4.5/5 (455)** for the 2800 N·m, **4.6 (315)** for the 1200 N·m, **4.5 (245)** for the 1800 N·m. The trimmer scores just **3.6 (121)**. **Trustpilot** shows **3.4/5 from 75 reviews** (51% 5-star, 30% 1-star); its AI summary says buyers like the power, build and value, but the 30% 1-star share shows a sizable group of unhappy customers.

## Shipping

- Orders ship in **1–2 business days** from warehouses in Germany, France, the UK, the US and elsewhere.

## Returns and warranty

- **60 days** to request a return.
- **Faulty or damaged items:** free return or exchange.
- **Change of mind:** you pay return shipping, and a **5% restocking fee** is deducted.
- **Warranty:** no warranty period stated in the policies we read.

## Who should buy

- **Yes:** DIYers with Makita-style batteries who want a cheap impact wrench for tire swaps and occasional jobs.
- **Think twice:** professionals, anyone who needs verified torque figures, or buyers who want a clear multi-year warranty.

## The bottom line

ONEVAN tools are cheap and run on popular 18V batteries, and the wrenches score well with store reviewers. Unverified specs, no stated warranty and a 3.4 Trustpilot score hold it back. Our verdict: **6.2/10**.
