## Who runs WiseChoiceKR

WiseChoiceKR is an independent review site run by **ParkChoice**, the pen name of the site's founder and editor, based in **South Korea**. The site launched in **October 2026**, and every review and buying guide is researched and written by ParkChoice.

Most of the brands we cover sell online and ship internationally, which is where comparing stores matters most: prices, shipping times, import fees and return terms can vary a lot from one shop to the next.

## How we choose what to cover

- **Brands readers can actually buy from.** We focus on online stores that ship to the US, Europe and Asia.
- **The store's own best sellers.** In our top-5 guides we list products in the order the store ranks them by sales, rather than picking favourites.
- **Research, not hype.** Reviews are based on official specs, store policies and customer ratings (including Trustpilot). We haven't handled most of these products ourselves, and we say so in every review.

## Our mission

Shopping online should not mean opening thirty tabs, decoding marketing claims and second-guessing every review. **WiseChoiceKR exists to do that work for you.** We research brands and their products, from specs and prices to shipping, returns and warranty terms, and publish clear, honest guides you can act on.

## What makes us different

- **We read the fine print.** Every brand review covers shipping costs, return windows, who pays return shipping and warranty terms, not just the product.
- **We flag what doesn't add up.** When a brand's claims contradict each other, we say so.
- **We're clear about our method.** Our reviews are research-based, and we say when something couldn't be confirmed. We never invent tests, ratings or experiences. See [how we review](/how-we-review/).
- **Rankings cannot be bought.** Brands cannot pay for placement, ratings or coverage, and they never see our content before it is published.
- **We tell you who should *not* buy.** Every review includes honest cons and clear "best for" guidance.

## How we make money

WiseChoiceKR is reader-supported. When you buy through some links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. Most brands we cover run affiliate programs we take part in, but that never changes what we write. Read our full [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).
